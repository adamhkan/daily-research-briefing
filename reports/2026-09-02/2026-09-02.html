Executive Summary

  • Foundation and VLA models continue to push dexterous and long-horizon manipulation, with several works focusing on self-evolving world models, temporal and physical grounding, and non-gradient deployment-time adaptation.
  • Navigation research spans embodied indoor agents, off-road ground vehicles, aerial/underwater/aquatic platforms, microrobots, and autonomous driving, with emphasis on cross-platform generalization, hazard awareness, and benchmarking robustness under corruptions and degradation.
  • Motion planning advances include certified graph-of-convex-sets planning, adaptive continuous-time collision checking, multi-modal vehicle trajectory planning, and new complexity results and benchmarks for multi-agent and routing problems.
  • Humanoid and soft-body control results demonstrate agile whole-body behaviors (brachiation, blind manipulation, soft-arm exploration) using rich proprioception and reinforcement learning, highlighting whole-body and distributed contact as powerful sensing channels.
  • Simulation, benchmarks, and evaluation suites (Behavior-Skill, RoboPhys-3D, Agri-Sim, DriveDegrade, microrobot navigation, ultrasound and palpation twins) are maturing to expose intermediate skills, 3D consistency, sim-to-real fidelity, and realistic safety/robustness limits.
  • Selective works from Carnegie Mellon University, New York University, and ETH Zurich contribute respectively to training-free VLA correction, touchable telepresence, and wearable soft robotic massage systems.

Training-Free Action Correction for VLA Model Failures via Language FeedbackCarnegie Mellon UniversityCorrectVLA introduces a deployment-time framework that turns a single task-level natural language correction into uniform, additive adjustments on action magnitudes, without modifying VLA policy weights. It reliably fixes execution misalignment failures in simulation and on a real xArm7 under environment shift, restoring near-perfect task success when the underlying policy’s semantic intent is correct but its action scaling is off.Link
Feelium: A Touchable Blimp Body for Aerial TelepresenceNew York UniversityFeelium is a blimp-based telepresence robot whose inflatable skin acts as a shared haptic surface between a remote VR user and co-located partners. Onboard sensing and rendering let physical touches on the blimp be visualized to the remote user, enabling rich, embodied social interaction in aerial telepresence beyond conventional audio-visual channels.Link
MiBOT: A head-worn robot that modulates cardiovascular responses through human-like soft massageETH ZurichMiBOT is a silent, head-worn massage robot that uses pneumatically actuated soft tactors and pressure-feedback control to mimic human head massage. In controlled user studies, it reduces blood pressure and heart rate comparably to human-administered massage, demonstrating the potential of soft wearable robots for physiological regulation and rehabilitation.Link

LightNav-0: Eliciting VLM Spatial Intelligence for Generalist Embodied NavigationLightNav-0 leverages a compact VLM backbone for unified embodied navigation, representing tasks through dual-channel pointing tokens and a residual vector-quantized action tokenizer that maps spatial intent to robot-specific trajectories. Trained across 4k+ hours of navigation data, it achieves SOTA monocular success on 10 simulated benchmarks and shows strong zero-shot transfer across robots and environments.Link
Zeva: In-Context Causal Learning for Generalizable Embodied ManipulationTsinghua UniversityZeva enables robots to improve manipulation performance online by encoding each executed action and state change into a causal interaction signal stored in a dual-timescale memory. Retrieved signals are injected into a frozen policy as context, allowing self-evolution during deployment—success rates improve with experience without any gradient updates to the base model.Link
Behavior-Skill: A Fine-Grained Benchmark for Evaluating Vision-Language-Action Policies in Long-Horizon TasksInc., Guangzhou, China.; National University of Defense Technology; Peng Inc.Behavior-Skill reorganizes long-horizon mobile manipulation evaluation around 235k labeled skill instances extracted from 10k demonstrations over 50 household tasks. Each skill has restorable intermediate states and success predicates, enabling skill-level evaluation that reveals non-uniform failure patterns—especially in contact-rich skills—for VLA policies like pi0.5 and GR00T.Link
CanonNav: Disentangling Navigation Behavior from Camera Geometry in Cross-Platform Visual NavigationCanonNav transforms visual observations and trajectories into a camera-consistent canonical space to separate navigation behavior from platform-specific camera geometry. Combined with safety and local-progress supervision derived from an offline traversability estimator, this enables RGB-only policies that outperform RGB and even RGB-D baselines across varied camera setups and environments.Link
Motus2: A Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous ManipulationMotus2 is a unified world model for dexterous manipulation that shares parameters across a policy, dynamics simulator, and value evaluator, forming a closed decision-and-learning loop. Scaled across monocular and stereo egocentric data with tactile feedback and instantiated on a dual-arm, dual-hand platform, it uses both successes and failures to continuously improve manipulation competence.Link
Contrast-Free Autonomous Navigation of Untethered Endovascular Microrobots Using Single-Plane FluoroscopyVISTA builds a 3D digital twin of vascular anatomy and discretizes vessel centerlines into milestones, enabling 3D navigation of magnetically actuated microrobots from single-plane fluoroscopy alone. In phantoms and in-vivo rat experiments, it cuts navigation time, corrective commands, and radiation exposure by up to 62%, 98%, and 57% versus human-in-the-loop control.Link
Sampling-based Certified Planning with Graphs of Convex SetsThis work augments graph-of-convex-sets planning with continuous-time collision certification by sampling overlaps and faces, verifying candidate paths via clearance certificates, and repairing failures with in-region detours. On a challenging 14-DOF bimanual benchmark, it returns zero invalid trajectories versus many for a standard GCS planner, while finding certified solutions faster.Link
Learning Agile Perceptive Traversal of Sparse 3D Structures for HumanoidsThe authors train a lidar-based RL policy for humanoid monkey-bar traversal, combining privileged expert sub-policies with an attention encoder and recurrence to handle sparse 3D geometry. After sim-to-real transfer that models sensing and hardware limits plus hook end-effectors, the real robot completes jump-up, brachiation, and landing in 14/15 trials and brachiates at up to 0.5 m/s.Link
LARC: Lazy Adaptive Reachability Certification of Robot Manipulator TrajectoriesLARC certifies continuous-time obstacle clearance for piecewise-cubic joint trajectories by recursively bisecting only intervals with uncertain clearance, using inflated midpoint capsules bounded by exact speed maxima. On hundreds of 7-DOF trajectories, it matches a dense fixed-time-check baseline’s decisions with about a quarter of the work and over 10× median speedup, without certifying any audited collisions.Link
Blind Dexterity: Whole-Body Humanoid Manipulation via Pure ProprioceptionGerman Research Center for Artificial IntelligenceUsing only joint encoders on a Unitree G1 humanoid, the authors train policies that treat compliant contact as a whole-body tactile channel to walk, trap a soccer ball, find and lift a suitcase handle, and mount a randomly placed skateboard. Separate compact estimators decode task-relevant state from short proprioceptive histories, showing that encoder-based sensing plus compliant actuation can underpin rich whole-body manipulation.Link
