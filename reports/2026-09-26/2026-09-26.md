## Executive Summary

- Dexterous and whole-body manipulation sees multiple advances, from real-time whole-hand force regulation and cooperative arm–hand planning to whole-arm oversized object lifting and impact-aware catching.
- World models and action models for control continue to mature, with Koopman-accelerated diffusion MPC, action-discriminative world models, delta and streaming world-action models, and representation-space planners improving speed and robustness.
- Navigation, locomotion, and state estimation progress via uncertainty-aware dynamic navigation, GNSS-based outdoor stacks, Doppler LiDAR odometry and initialization, robust quadruped and humanoid parkour, and remote-camera visual navigation.
- LLM/VLM-based agents, harnesses, and VLAs are increasingly used for long-horizon mobile manipulation, task and motion planning, self-adaptive deployment, and policy evolution, with several benchmarks targeting recovery, safety, and evaluation gaps.
- Perception and mapping advances (multi-modal octree maps, LiDAR-based place recognition, dense 3D tracking, camera–LiDAR novel view synthesis) strengthen the perception stack underpinning navigation and manipulation.
- Institution-highlighted work from MIT, CMU, UC Berkeley, and NYU spans tape simulation, cable tracing, agentic robot programming from demonstrations, and multimodal camera–LiDAR view synthesis, with broad implications for robotic manipulation and perception.
