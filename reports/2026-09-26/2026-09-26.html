Executive Summary

  • Dexterous and whole-body manipulation sees multiple advances, from real-time whole-hand force regulation and cooperative arm–hand planning to whole-arm oversized object lifting and impact-aware catching.
  • World models and action models for control continue to mature, with Koopman-accelerated diffusion MPC, action-discriminative world models, delta and streaming world-action models, and representation-space planners improving speed and robustness.
  • Navigation, locomotion, and state estimation progress via uncertainty-aware dynamic navigation, GNSS-based outdoor stacks, Doppler LiDAR odometry and initialization, robust quadruped and humanoid parkour, and remote-camera visual navigation.
  • LLM/VLM-based agents, harnesses, and VLAs are increasingly used for long-horizon mobile manipulation, task and motion planning, self-adaptive deployment, and policy evolution, with several benchmarks targeting recovery, safety, and evaluation gaps.
  • Perception and mapping advances (multi-modal octree maps, LiDAR-based place recognition, dense 3D tracking, camera–LiDAR novel view synthesis) strengthen the perception stack underpinning navigation and manipulation.
  • Institution-highlighted work from MIT, CMU, UC Berkeley, and NYU spans tape simulation, cable tracing, agentic robot programming from demonstrations, and multimodal camera–LiDAR view synthesis, with broad implications for robotic manipulation and perception.

Real-Time Force Regulation for Whole-Hand Dexterous GraspingMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyIntroduces a real-time controller that continuously recomputes whole-hand contact force distributions as contacts move across the hand, without requiring tactile sensing. The method enforces friction, actuator, and actuation-consistency constraints, and is integrated with reactive reaching to acquire, maintain, and recover grasps, demonstrating improved robustness in simulation and on a 27-DoF arm–hand system.Link
RAPID: Robot Agentic Programming from DemonstrationsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyRAPID turns a single visual human demonstration into reusable robot programs by automatically inferring task specs, action primitives, and an interactive execution environment. It represents strategies as object-centric relational programs coupled with trajectory-optimization primitives, enabling strong generalization across nonprehensile and prehensile tasks in both simulation and on a real Franka arm.Link
TAPESIM: Efficient Simulation of Adhesive Tape Dispensing for Robotic ManipulationCarnegie Mellon UniversityPresents TapeSim, a tape dispenser simulator that clusters most of the wound tape into a rigid body while keeping a deformable collar and free strip, enabling realistic unwinding, attachment, and reattachment at much lower computational cost. It achieves 3–8× physics-step speedups and better agreement with real tape behavior, supporting robotic tape-handling tasks like box sealing.Link
TRACE: Interactive Bi-Directional Tracing of Monochrome Cables Amid ClutterUC BerkeleyTRACE combines bi-directional cable tracing with interactive perception actions (Divergence Push and Cluster Dilation) to resolve occlusions and crossings among multiple monochrome cables in cluttered scenes. In 110 physical experiments with up to four cables and 40 crossings, it raises correctly traced length from about 60% to ~90%, substantially outperforming prior DLO methods.Link

Do World Models Make Better Robots? A Survey of Evaluation Benchmarks for Predictive Embodied IntelligenceUC BerkeleySurveys 160 benchmarks across policy, embodied-agent, world-model, and prediction-to-action evaluation, highlighting that only 7% explicitly compare world models to direct VLAs and very few execute predictions in closed loop. Proposes a taxonomy and an evaluation protocol aimed at measuring when and how predictive world models yield concrete control advantages.Link
TrackEverything: Long Horizon Dense Tracking via De-Duplicating 3D Scene RepresentationsCarnegie Mellon UniversityTrackEverything represents videos as persistent 3D scene tracks with voxel-based de-duplication, enabling dense tracking of all visible points over sequences exceeding 1000 frames within 40 GB GPU memory. It combines endpoint and trajectory refinement with an efficient 3D feature sampling scheme, outperforming existing dense 3D trackers on TAPVid-3D by over 20% APD.Link
High-Voltage Optocoupler Amplifier for Electrostatic ActuatorsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyDemonstrates a minimalist high-voltage linear amplifier using optocouplers to drive electrostatic actuators up to 20 kV peak-to-peak with ~500 Hz bandwidth. Operating optocouplers in feedback yields higher bandwidth and lower distortion than comparable PWM designs, simplifying the drive electronics for multi-kilovolt electrostatic actuation.Link

Coding Agents for Generalized Task and Motion Planning ProblemsEvaluates coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex on 28 generalized task and motion planning (TAMP) environments, where agents synthesize reusable programs via simulator interaction. Across 98,000 evaluation episodes, these agent-generated programs significantly outperform hand-engineered planners and an LLM generalized planning baseline, maintaining higher success with far less computation as object counts grow.Link
ReVAMP: Vector-Accelerated Motion Planning for Kinematically-Constrained Systems via ReparameterizationIntroduces a motion planner that reparameterizes constrained configuration spaces using analytic inverse kinematics so that samples are constraint-satisfying by construction. The approach enables new vectorization strategies, achieving microsecond–millisecond planning for up to 20-DoF systems and up to 10× speedups over state-of-the-art projection-based planners, opening doors to restructuring manipulation pipelines around very fast replanning.Link
Contact as a Decision Variable: Capability-Tradeoff Contact Selection for Legged Loco-ManipulationFormulates contact selection for legged loco-manipulation as a trade-off between residual wrench, end-effector reach, base mobility, and contact acquisition cost. The proposed CTCS method approximates these capabilities via sensitivity analysis and selective exact evaluations, delivering ~3× speedup over exhaustive evaluation while enabling a Unitree Go2 with arm to choose support surfaces that substantially improve task performance.Link
ADM-Planner: LLM-Guided Long-Horizon Planning for Mobile Manipulators with Attention-Enhanced Dynamic MemoryADM-Planner combines an LLM planner with an attention-based dynamic memory that separates persistent workspace knowledge from object states and uses a bounded retriever to feed only relevant entries into the prompt. In simulation and real mobile-manipulator experiments, it achieves near-perfect success on long-horizon tasks while drastically reducing context size and planner calls compared to static or unfiltered memory.Link
Body-Grounded Replanning for Physically Adaptive ManipulationMicrosoft ResearchProposes a body-grounded replanning framework where an LLM selects high-level manipulation strategies based on internal physical state (e.g., joint loads, mobility constraints) and execution history. The approach adapts strategies mid-task without changing low-level controllers, maintaining high success while reducing physical effort in reaching and contact-rich manipulation both in simulation and on real hardware.Link
Res-HIL: Human-Guided Residual Reinforcement Learning for Sample-Efficient Dexterous ManipulationSiemens; Technical University of MunichRes-HIL learns a residual correction policy on top of a frozen imitation policy using human-in-the-loop interventions that provide both direct residual supervision and reward shaping. Across five contact-rich dexterous tasks, it surpasses full-policy human-in-the-loop RL and unguided residual fine-tuning within about ten minutes of online training, achieving strong improvements with only 20 initial demonstrations.Link
CAMP: Cooperative Arm-Hand Motion Planning in Constrained SpacesHarbin Institute of Technology; KTH Royal Institute of Technology; Key Laboratory for Multi-target Natural Medicine, China Pharmaceutical University, Nanjing, China; TH Royal Institute; The University of Hong KongCAMP models the coupling between arm and hand via feasible hand fibers—collision-free hand configurations attached to each arm pose—and searches layered hand spaces with local arm relaxation. It then compresses candidate trajectories with via-point movement primitives for coarse-to-fine optimization, achieving 84–99% success in constrained manipulation scenarios and validating performance on real robots.Link
CALM: Current Aligned Link Manipulation for Single Arm Oversized Object LiftingThe University of Hong Kong; Tsinghua UniversityCALM enables whole-arm manipulation of oversized objects using motor currents as load-related feedback, with stage-specific policies for repositioning, grasping, and lifting learned in simulation. A current-mapping module aligns simulated and real actuator currents for sim-to-real transfer, and a unified student policy achieves over 73% success on physical oversized-object lifting without real-world RL.Link
Outcome-Sensitive Motion Search for Impact-Aware Dexterous CatchingUniversity of Science and Technology of ChinaIntroduces Outcome-Sensitive Motion Search, which analyzes the sensitivity of catching outcomes to pre-contact motion and defines an outcome-sensitive window to refine demonstrations from a privileged RL teacher. By learning a manifold of successful pre-contact motions and performing targeted search and validation, the resulting imitation policy surpasses the teacher in both catch success and impact mitigation.Link
GPT-6-Astra Lights Up Embodied Navigation: Evaluation in Zero-Shot Vision-and-Language Navigation in Continuous EnvironmentsAssesses GPT-6-Astra as a zero-shot agent for continuous vision-and-language navigation, where it chooses when to observe, how to move, and when to stop using only monocular RGB. On the R2R-CE benchmark it achieves a 79% success rate, substantially outperforming prior zero-shot and supervised systems, while exposing remaining challenges in route execution and goal verification.Link
