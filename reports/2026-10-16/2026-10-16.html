Executive Summary

  • Strong push on robust mobile manipulation and whole-body control, from hierarchical residual fine-tuning (HiRe-MoMa) to modular whole-body coordination (RoboSpan) and mobile-manipulation diagnosis plus agentic scaffolding.
  • Navigation and motion planning see advances in multimodal/demo-driven policies, Gaussian-splatting roadmaps, tiered volumetric maps, and image/benchmark-driven multi-agent navigation in complex 3D scenes.
  • Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation is scaling via large VTLA datasets, force- and tactile-aware foundation models, hierarchical dexterous RL, and new interfaces/benchmarks for force integration and robot-free data collection.
  • New foundations for control and planning include anytime multi-arm Gaussian BP planners, FPTAS for multi-robot motion planning, safe reduced-order MPC, and differentiable contact dynamics for inverse problems and control.
  • VLA-centric work focuses on making policies faster, safer, and more adaptable through deployment-time monitoring/planning, discrete vs continuous action studies, foveated perception, in-context RL, and self-improving agentic systems.
  • Humanoid and locomotion research emphasizes whole-body loco-manipulation from large human datasets, cross-morphology locomotion, residual world models for contact, and expressive whole-body control and references.

Physical Time as a Missing Dimension of Robot Policy Memory DynamicsNew York UniversityProposes Temporal Flow Policy (TFP), a memory architecture whose state evolves explicitly with measured physical time rather than only observation steps. When wrapped around diffusion and sequence policies, TFP improves timing-sensitive manipulation and maintains interpretable temporal encodings that can be modulated at test time.Link
When Do Discrete Actions Make Good Continuous Controllers?Carnegie Mellon UniversitySystematically compares discrete and continuous action parameterizations for robotic policies across simulation and hardware. Finds that discretized actions, when combined with discrete flows or guidance to mitigate factorization errors, can match or outperform continuous actions, challenging the field’s default design assumptions.Link
VIGOR-MPC: Vision-Guided Objective Regulation MPCETH ZurichPresents a teacher–student MPC framework where a privileged long-horizon controller guides a vision-conditioned short-horizon MPC for reference tracking under onboard perception. Demonstrated on autonomous racing, it recovers near-teacher lap times on unseen tracks while cutting control computation by over 90%.Link
FoveaVLA: Foveation Actions Replace the Wrist Camera in VLAsStanford UniversityReplaces wrist cameras in VLA manipulation with learned foveation actions that request high-resolution crops from static external cameras. Built on π₀.₅, FoveaVLA significantly boosts real-world task success—especially for clutter, cloth, and distant objects—using only external RGB-D views.Link
ForceBench: Benchmarking Force Integration for Robotic ManipulationUC BerkeleyIntroduces ForceBench, a standardized eight-task benchmark and dataset for evaluating how VLAs integrate wrist force sensing. Across multiple integration strategies on a shared π₀.₅ backbone, prompt-based force fusion yields the strongest and most consistent gains in contact-rich and property-inference tasks.Link
Back on Track: Deployment-time Policy Improvement via World Model PlanningStanford UniversityProposes a black-box policy monitor that learns a liveness value and compact world model from limited rollouts, then intervenes during deployment when progress stalls. Short-horizon planning in the learned model corrects failures of frozen VLAs and diffusion policies, improving LIBERO and real Franka success without retraining the base policy.Link
Full-Order Safety for Nonlinear Reduced-Order MPCStanford UniversityIntroduces CoRE-MPC, a robust MPC scheme that plans with a nonlinear reduced-order model while propagating certified error bounds to guarantee constraint satisfaction on the full-order system. Achieves large speedups over full-order robust MPC on high-dimensional soft-robot and PDE systems with modest cost increases.Link
Contact-UAN: Unsupervised Actuator Networks as a Residual World Model for Humanoid ControlMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyLearns an actuator-focused residual world model that corrects sim-to-real discrepancies in humanoid contact dynamics while preserving physics priors. Hierarchical Residual Policy Learning for Real-World Mobile Manipulation with Sparse Human GuidanceHiRe-MoMa fine-tunes complex mobile manipulation systems via a hierarchical residual that adds corrections atop a frozen base policy, with separate residual heads for base and arm to respect the platform’s physical hierarchy. Sparse human interventions reshape the critic’s value landscape, enabling about 30 minutes of on-robot interaction to substantially uplift TIAGo++ performance.Link
Tiered-Resolution Volumetric Mapping for Real-Time Simultaneous Planning and Mapping on Natural Outdoor EnvironmentsProposes a LiDAR-based mapping scheme that maintains fine resolution only in a corridor around the current plan while using coarse voxels elsewhere, closing the loop between planner and mapper. On outdoor datasets, this tiered map is an order of magnitude smaller and cheaper to integrate than uniform fine maps while preserving path quality, avoiding coarse-map failures in difficult terrain.Link
DD-GBP: Anytime Multi-Arm Planning and Continuous Execution via Distributed Dynamic Gaussian Belief PropagationIntroduces DD-GBP, a distributed Gaussian-belief-propagation framework that plans joint trajectories for many arms via local factor-graph inference and Gaussian-process parameterization. It supports adaptive horizons, asynchronous message passing, and triggered replanning, achieving state-of-the-art success and scalability in long-horizon, multi-arm, dynamic scenes.Link
TacClaw-Infinity: A Large-Scale Vision-Tactile-Language-Action Dataset for Dexterous ManipulationPresents TacClaw-Infinity, a million-episode VTLA dataset collected via a handheld tactile interface across diverse real-world settings, capturing fingertip forces, images, and end-effector motion. A structured pipeline reconstructs trajectories and annotations, and experiments show cross-embodiment transfer of contact-rich skills to multiple robots without robot-specific demos.Link
Learning Navigation from Multimodal Demonstrations with Geometric Candidate ModesThe Ohio State UniversityProposes a hierarchical policy that first enumerates geometric route “modes” (e.g., pass left or right of an obstacle) and then learns to select and realize them from offline navigation demonstrations. This avoids unsafe averaging of multimodal behaviors and attains >95% success in simulation on seen and unseen maps, with transfer to a bipedal robot.Link
HM3D-M3: A Benchmark for Multi-Agent, Multi-Modal, Multi-Object Goal NavigationChinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of SciencesIntroduces HM3D-M3, a large-scale benchmark where multiple agents must find multiple object goals specified via categories, images, or language in photo-realistic 3D homes. Provides thousands of goals and team-level metrics, and shows current multi-robot and VLM baselines achieve only 16–41% SR, highlighting substantial room for advancement.Link
MUCO: A Mani-Unit-Structured Manipulation Framework for Compositional Long-Horizon Robot TasksDefines reusable Manipulation Units (MUs)—each with local goals and verifiable success/failure—and composes them into long-horizon tasks via MUCO. Visual verification and a frozen capability registry allow retries and cross-task reuse without joint training, improving complete-task success and containing failure propagation on RLBench, RoboTwin 2.0, and real setups.Link
Coordi-pi: Coordination-Aware Dual-Hand Action Representation for Bimanual Dexterous ManipulationShanghai Jiao Tong UniversityIntroduces Coordi-π, an action-token representation that interleaves left and right arm–hand commands with explicit hand-identity embeddings and RoPE factorization across sequence and time. This structured bimanual encoding yields large success gains on all DexJoCo tasks and multiple real bimanual dexterous manipulation scenarios.Link
F2A-ObjectNav: An Adaptive Agentic Architecture for Object-Goal NavigationBuilds F2A-ObjectNav, an adaptive object-goal navigation system with a rule-based supervisor that orchestrates exploration, detection, validation, semantic guidance, and memory. It dynamically switches strategies, triggers recovery, and leverages mission memory, achieving strong SR/SPL across AI2-THOR, RoboTHOR, and ProcTHOR with environment-dependent intervention patterns.Link
Cracking Mobile Manipulation: From Failure Diagnosis to Agentic Robot SystemChinese Academy of Sciences; Peking UniversityDiagnoses why strong subtask VLAs fail when composed into long-horizon mobile manipulation, identifying task planning, navigation/base positioning, and local manipulation as distinct failure regimes. Augments a frozen VLA with failure-guided planning, visual memory, base adjustment, and end-effector refinements, more than doubling success on benchmarks like RoboCasa365 and BEHAVIOR-1K.Link
