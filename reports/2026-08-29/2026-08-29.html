Executive Summary

  • Strong motion-planning and control advances span kinodynamic tree search (DFT*), real-time navigation (RTNav), certified safe driving trajectories, and soft/continuum actuator control.
  • Manipulation-focused work ranges from thin-object and soft-gripper grasping to compact and history-aware VLA policies, rapid on-robot learning for dynamic skills, and robust failure recovery.
  • Foundation-model-style world and action models (Riemann-1.0, CLAP, WALL-SS, CLIPPER, CLAP world models, TemporalFlow-VLA) push long-horizon prediction, multi-embodiment simulation, and planning.
  • Humanoid and legged locomotion, plus safe goal-conditioned RL, move toward long-horizon, safety-aware whole-body and navigation behaviors with minimal supervision.
  • Multi-agent navigation and MAPD in constrained warehouses receive new guarantees and efficiency via haven-based reservation and adaptive retreat-planning schemes.
  • Selected works from MIT, UC Berkeley, Stanford, and UC San Diego highlight institution-aligned progress in kinodynamic planning, instruction-conditioned control, continual robot learning, and low-latency VLA inference.

Dispersive Forward Tree Search for Optimal Control: Coverage, Complexity, and ComputationMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyIntroduces DFT*, a kinodynamic motion planner for differentially flat nonlinear systems that builds a forward tree of dispersive control commands with deterministic finite-sample near-optimality guarantees. The method uses cost-conditioned dominance pruning to keep tree size polynomial in the horizon, maps naturally to parallel hardware, and outperforms state-of-the-art planners on unicycle, trailer car, and quadrotor problems, including receding-horizon deployment in dynamic environments.Link
Decoupling Planning and Control for Instructable AgentsUC BerkeleyProposes Instruct-to-Act, which couples a VLM-based high-level planner with a fast world-model controller conditioned on sparse language instructions. Through instruction relabeling and joint behavior cloning with reward and world-model objectives, the system achieves better performance than controller-only and direct VLM-action baselines across seven embodied tasks, including multi-agent coordination, while enabling planner modularity and low-latency control.Link
FlashVLA: Streaming Action Decoding for Fast and Asynchronous VLA InferenceUC San DiegoIntroduces FlashVLA, a streaming action-decoding framework for flow-matching VLAs that maintains a multi-chunk action buffer at different noise levels and uses chunk-wise causal attention. This design yields one executable action chunk per inference step, preserving temporal continuity while achieving ≥30 Hz control on a single GPU for smooth, asynchronous real-world manipulation.Link
Memory Anchors for Continual Robot LearningStanford UniversityIdentifies "Memory Anchors"—high-conflict regions where new-task observations overlap old-task representations but require different actions—as critical samples for replay-based continual robot learning. Emphasizing these anchors in the buffer sharply reduces catastrophic forgetting on LIBERO and enables successful multi-task continual learning on real robots.Link

RTNav: Towards Real-Time Zero-Shot Object NavigationAnalyzes how VLM-based zero-shot object navigation methods degrade when wall-clock inference time is counted and environments step asynchronously. Proposes RTNav, an architecture explicitly designed around latency and bounded compute, improving real-time object navigation success and time-weighted metrics on real-time HM3D variants.Link
Barrier Function Conformal Safety Clearance Certification with CVaR for Driving Trajectory SelectionThe Ohio State UniversityPresents a framework that certifies safety clearance for autonomous driving trajectories by combining a differentiable separating-axis barrier margin with CVaR-based risk evaluation and conformal calibration. On nuPlan data, it tightly bounds trajectory clearances while meeting statistical coverage targets, substantially reducing conservative safety margins.Link
Arrive and Survive: Scaling Safe Goal-Conditioned Policy Learning from One-Bit Failure SignalsDiagnoses an overestimation bias in contrastive RL under failure-terminated tasks and shows it can reinforce unsafe behaviors. Introduces Safe-CRL with mass-weighted InfoNCE and a log-survival-mass policy objective, yielding safer and more successful goal-conditioned navigation and locomotion across 12 failure-prone tasks using only binary failure signals.Link
Closing the Loop on the Poppy Humanoid: Bipedal Locomotion with Linear-Quadratic Control and Learned Cost FunctionsSyracuse UniversityDevelops a closed-loop walking controller for the low-cost Poppy humanoid based on LQR tracking of nominal trajectories with a learned quadratic cost. Experiments show significantly improved reliability over open-loop playback, providing an accessible framework for stable bipedal locomotion on standard Poppy hardware.Link
SOLO: Stable Omni-terrain Long-Horizon Perceptive Humanoid LocomotionUniversity of Science and Technology of ChinaIntroduces SOLO, a perceptive humanoid locomotion framework combining a Query Reconstructor for sharp terrain encoding with a trajectory-aware distillation loss that propagates future disagreement penalties back in time. SOLO achieves near-perfect traversal on challenging terrains and zero-shot transfers to 1.5 km outdoor and indoor routes using only a chest depth camera and proprioception.Link
Task-space model-based control of pneumatic soft actuatorsVanderbilt UniversityPresents a discrete elastic rod–based dynamic model for planar pneumatic soft actuators combined with a quasi-static inverse model, task-space PI control, and a dynamic observer. The framework enables real-time, high-precision task-space control with millimeter-level RMSE across writing and trajectory-tracking tasks under sparse sensing.Link
Rapid On-Robot Learning for Dynamic Manipulation Skills: Robot JugglingUniversity of CambridgeDemonstrates a bimanual robot that learns multiple three-ball juggling patterns directly on hardware within minutes via regularized memory-based online learning that combines a global prior model with locally fitted models. A mutually reachable set construction ensures safety between throws and catches, enabling continual, stable on-robot learning of highly dynamic skills.Link
Active Surface-Driven Reconfigurable Gripper: Robust Grasping and Sequential Manipulation of Thin ObjectsKernel Co., Ltd.; Zhejiang UniversityIntroduces an underactuated gripper with an active-surface thumb and compliant fingers that can robustly grasp and reposition thin objects like books without complex arm motion. Kinematic and physical modeling guide parameter and strategy optimization, yielding high success across flat and vertically packed thin items and reliable long sequential grasp-place tasks.Link
Relaxation-Aware Multimodal Sensing of Soft Gripper Driven by Structure-Perception-LearningKernel Co., Ltd.; Zhejiang UniversityPresents a variable-stiffness soft gripper with onboard vision and IR thermography, plus a physics-informed learning model that couples temperature to viscoelastic force decay. The system reconstructs and compensates grasp force over long holds, maintaining target force with very low error and highlighting co-design of mechanism and learning for stable soft grasping.Link
