Executive Summary

  • UC Berkeley and ETH Zurich feature prominently today, with new methods for humanoid safe stopping, surgical dexterity, and multi-view surgical perception benchmarks.
  • Mobile and autonomous driving motion planning advances include unified diffusion+search planning (DiffuSearch), risk-aware diffusion RL for driving (DiDrive), and better trajectory scoring datasets.
  • Manipulation and whole-body control see progress in uncertainty-aware active mapping (MS-MEM), torque-sampling MPPI for compliant manipulation, and local-geometry-based dexterous transfer (DemoMimic).
  • Humanoid and legged locomotion are pushed by world-model-augmented visual locomotion on constrained footholds (WM-LOCO) and reliability-weighted proprioceptive odometry (FOCUS).
  • World models and VLA-centric control for manipulation are strengthened with sparse residual world models for object-centric pushing, 3D-aware world action models (SA-WAM), and cross-embodiment VLA transfer (ZETA).
  • Navigation and multi-robot coordination contributions span efficient vision-language navigation (LookStep), terrain-adaptive greenhouse navigation, UAV task coordination with reactive avoidance (TriSAR), and evaluation frameworks for future-aware planners (PDT).

Humanoid Safe Stop via Learned Stoppability ValueUC BerkeleySafe-Stop casts emergency stopping for humanoids as a reach-avoid problem, pairing a learned stop policy with two complementary "stoppability" estimators: one learned from stop outcomes and one from Hamilton–Jacobi reachability. At runtime, the system commits to stopping only when both estimators indicate feasibility, otherwise handing off to a fall policy, yielding task-agnostic, transferable safety behavior across upstream controllers.Link
MACAW: Reliable And Efficient Surgical Debridement Using Monocular Adaptive Compact Attention WindowsUC BerkeleyMACAW combines image-plane visual servoing with a new monocular depth-control method using adaptive compact attention windows to automate surgical debridement on the da Vinci platform. The system reduces pixel offset from 37 to under 5 pixels in under 0.4 s and achieves over 90% success in both single-arm and bimanual debridement, significantly improving throughput versus procedural and learned baselines.Link
MV-dVRK: A Multi-Viewpoint Benchmark for Spatial Surgical PerceptionETH ZurichMV-dVRK is the first ex-vivo surgical dataset with synchronized multi-stereo viewpoints, accurate surface geometry, and camera poses, enabling rigorous evaluation of 3D reconstruction in endoscopic conditions. Benchmarking shows multi-stereo and optimization-based multi-view methods substantially outperform current feed-forward foundation models in coverage and accuracy, and dynamic sequences support future work on deformable-tissue perception.Link

MS-MEM: Multi-Skill Manipulation-Enhanced Mapping via Uncertainty- and Disturbance-Aware Action SelectionKarlsruhe Institute of TechnologyMS-MEM is an evidential framework that jointly reasons over active viewpoint selection, pushing, and grasping to reduce semantic-metric map uncertainty in cluttered shelves. A full-evidential grasp estimator models grasp affordance and orientation uncertainty, while a collateral disturbance constraint penalizes unnecessary changes to confident regions, yielding more accurate maps with less scene disturbance than single-skill or unconstrained baselines.Link
DiffuSearch: How Hybrid Trajectory Planning Benefits from Aligned Objectives in Diffusion and Action SpaceDiffuSearch unifies objectives between a guided diffusion model and a Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) planner for autonomous driving, using shared differentiable costs for safety, comfort, and progress. The diffusion stage generates scene-consistent trajectory proposals steered by these costs, while MCTS refines them with the same reward, achieving state-of-the-art performance and reduced collisions on nuPlan and interPlan benchmarks.Link
Real-Time Dynamics-Based Torque-Sampling MPPI for Compliant and Force Aware ManipulationSchool of Electrical Engineering, Korea University, 02841 Seoul, South KoreaThis work introduces a model predictive path integral (MPPI) controller that samples in torque space and explicitly solves full rigid-body dynamics within the MPC loop for manipulators. GPU-parallelized sampling allows 166 Hz updates with a 0.18 s horizon while enforcing safety constraints, enabling compliant, force-aware interaction in unstructured environments on a 7-DoF arm.Link
One Demonstration, Many Objects: Generalizing Manipulation via Local Contact GeometryDemoMimic learns dexterous manipulation policies that focus on local contact geometry rather than global object shape, enabling transfer across objects that share similar contact structures. Contact-centric rewards encourage precise contact and improve sim-to-real consistency, yielding a single policy that achieves 71% success across 16 real objects, four tasks, and two robot-hand embodiments.Link
LookStep: Efficient Vision-Language Navigation with Linguistic Foresight and Event Driven MemoryLookStep proposes a resource-efficient VLN agent that predicts coarse future navigation states for each candidate action and maintains an event-driven rolling memory with semantic roles. By replacing heavy cognitive maps and long history buffers with language-centric future modeling and selective memory writes, it improves success on VLN-CE tasks while reducing data and memory demands.Link
Do Better Imagined Rollouts Mean Better Robot Control? A Controlled Study of World-Model Evaluation Under FeedbackGeorgia Institute of TechnologyThis study compares offline world-model evaluation metrics to actual closed-loop performance on a differential-drive path-tracking task with biased odometry and intermittent sensing. It shows that replay-based error with measurement updates correlates better with closed-loop tracking than long open-loop rollouts, and that prediction horizon and measurement schedule must match deployment conditions for meaningful evaluation.Link
From Proxy Learning to Driving Decisions: A Transfer-Based Framework for Evaluating Future-Aware Autonomous Driving PlannersThe Proxy-to-Decision Transfer (PDT) framework dissects how improvements in proxy objectives (e.g., prediction losses) for future-aware driving planners translate—or fail to translate—into actual decision quality. Through detailed decomposition of value loss and stringent reliability checks on NAVSIM, it reveals that many apparent gains in learned components do not survive end-to-end decision robustness tests.Link
HINT: Human-Intent Inception for Long-Horizon Robot ManipulationShanghai Jiao Tong University; Zhejiang UniversityHINT separates sparse semantic intent updates from continuous visual control for long-horizon manipulation, invoking language-based reasoning only at manipulation-pattern transitions. It then grounds and tracks the chosen subtask via multi-view perception and communicates it to a frozen action policy through visual interfaces (highlighting and attention priors), boosting success and robustness on complex multi-step tasks.Link
Advancing Accessible Underwater Robotics: The Mini-Girona I-AUV at RAMI 2025The Mini-Girona I-AUV is a relatively low-cost underwater intervention platform that integrates a 5-DOF manipulator, stereo vision, and onboard AI for autonomous navigation and manipulation. Its performance at the RAMI 2025 competition demonstrates strong vision-based perception and intervention capabilities, highlighting its promise as an accessible research and education platform for underwater mobile manipulation.Link
Pre-Lane-change Signal in Transitional Autonomous Vehicles: Results from Controlled ExperimentsAnalyzing 150 controlled lane changes from a production transitional AV, this work formalizes a pre-lane-change signal time and models how the vehicle chooses to merge ahead of or behind target-lane traffic. A Firth logistic regression using relative position and speed at this signal achieves 0.89 accuracy and supports a two-stage lane change view: longitudinal preparation followed by lateral execution.Link
