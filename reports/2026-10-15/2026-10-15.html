Executive Summary

  • Strong emphasis on contact-rich and dexterous manipulation: multiple works introduce new action/latent representations, tactile prediction and conditioning, and sim-to-real data engines for fine control.
  • Long-horizon and whole-body control are recurring themes, spanning mobile manipulation, humanoid locomotion, quadruped navigation, and hierarchical benchmarks for extended task sequences.
  • World models and world-action models continue to mature, with several papers proposing lighter or more structured futures (boxes, occupancy, SE(3) states, latent predictions) to support planning and policy optimization.
  • Navigation research focuses on robust long-horizon operation under uncertainty: new SLAM, memory and mapping schemes, chained-goal and interactive navigation, and safety-aware conformal semantics.
  • Agentic and foundation-model-based robotics are increasingly prominent, with coding agents, VLA/world-model hybrids, and self-improvement frameworks that adapt policies post-deployment.
  • Benchmarks and datasets targeting robustness—to hardware perturbations, changing environments, or complex assemblies—are expanding evaluation for both manipulation and navigation systems.

OneDemo: Unified Policy Learning and Data Generation from a Single DemonstrationStanford UniversityOneDemo treats editing a single successful demonstration as a structured RL problem, learning when to switch object-centric segments and how to geometrically adapt them. The learned editor serves both as a task policy and as a reusable demonstration generator, achieving high success on Meta-World and DexMimicGen while dramatically amplifying data from one demo.Link
I3L: Iterative Interactive Imitation Learning for Whole-Body Mobile ManipulationStanford UniversityI3L combines a low-cost whole-body correction interface (JoyLo+) with iterative DAgger-style refinement to close the gap between teleoperated training and reliable deployment in long-horizon mobile manipulation. Across multiple robots and tasks, four rounds of HG-DAgger with I3L boost autonomous success by over 68 percentage points while reducing human takeover time.Link
Force-Modulated Dexterity: Unified Compliance for Contact-Rich ManipulationCarnegie Mellon UniversityFOMO-Dex defines a unified compliance scheme that modulates actions in task/contact-aligned coordinates, correcting motion based on measured forces only along selected directions. In card picking, cutting, screwdriving, and nut-and-bolt assembly, it reduces excessive forces, improves demo quality, and yields better downstream policies than stiff teleoperation control.Link
Task-Space Motion-Force Nonlinear Model Predictive Control for Precise and Compliant Control under UncertaintiesNew York UniversityThis work proposes a unified NMPC formulation that directly plans joint torques under coupled task-space motion and force objectives, with intrinsic impedance properties analyzed for stability and passivity under unmodeled interactions. Hardware results on a redundant arm and a quadruped demonstrate precise hybrid motion–force control and compliant locomotion with hard constraints and real-time operation.Link
RoboSimStudio: An Interactive Toolkit for Human and Agent Rapid Real2Sim of Robot Manipulation TasksUC BerkeleyRoboSimStudio exposes contact models, solvers, and physical parameters as editable elements atop the Newton physics engine, enabling rapid, interactive real-to-sim alignment by humans or coding agents. Experiments with visual and force-based tuning, plus agentic Real2Sim2Real loops, highlight its utility for building more faithful manipulation simulations.Link
Learning Robot Policies for Whole-Body Mobility Assistance via Dynamic Human Motion GenerationCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors build a simulation framework where a learned human policy, trained with an adversarial motion prior, responds realistically to a mobile manipulator providing sit-to-stand and lift-and-transfer assistance. Using these interactions, they train 3D diffusion policies conditioned on point-cloud humans, showing successful whole-body assistance in simulation and preliminary sim-to-real transfer.Link
The Human--VLA Communication Gap: What Humans Leave Implicit and What Policies Cannot RecoverStanford UniversityAnalyzing how people naturally specify tasks versus what VLA policies like π₀.₅ can infer, this study separates failures due to missing task semantics/grounding from those due to execution sensitivity. It finds humans increasingly rely on implicit context as tasks grow, while current VLAs cope with grounding dependence but struggle when semantics or event structure must be reconstructed.Link
From Orbit Matching to Docking: A Hierarchical Reinforcement-Learning Approach to Multiphase Rendezvous Trajectory DesignMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThis work trains five phase-specific RL policies for spacecraft rendezvous (orbit matching through docking) and a high-level manager that selects among them based on state. The hierarchical controller handles the full multiphase RPO sequence and outperforms fixed heuristic switching in trajectory quality and transition behavior.Link
MIRROR: Reflecting Hidden Symmetries from Offline Robot Interaction DataCarnegie Mellon UniversityMIRROR learns how predefined symmetry groups act jointly on observations and actions by adversarially matching transformed transition tuples and enforcing group axioms. On tabletop manipulation, it recovers high-quality symmetry operators and, when used for frame-averaged behavior cloning, improves success over vanilla BC without manual symmetry engineering.Link
Declarative Reactive Robot Behaviors from Imperative Language ModelsETH ZurichUsing the Playful reactive scripting language, this paper shows how an LLM-based coding agent can synthesize declarative, event-driven robot behaviors from under-specified natural-language descriptions. Reusable guidelines steer the agent away from imperative patterns toward robust reactive compositions, jointly designing ROS-based perception/actuation modules and their declarative orchestration.Link
REFLEX: A Learned On-Board Reflex for Robust Autonomous Collision Recovery of Soft QuadrotorsETH ZurichREFLEX is a recurrent proprioceptive policy running entirely on the flight controller that stabilizes a soft quadrotor after high-speed wall impacts, without external sensing. Trained in simulation from real post-impact states, it enables reliable recovery from ~5–6 m/s collisions, far outperforming a stock autopilot and allowing multiple consecutive recoveries in a single flight.Link
Walk Like This: Stylized Locomotion for Physics-Based Characters through Example-Driven DiffusionETH ZurichThis framework uses a style encoder and autoregressive diffusion model to generate kinematic references that a RL controller tracks, enabling real-time humanoid locomotion with independently controllable velocity and style. Trained on unstructured motion data, it generalizes to unseen styles and runs both in simulation and on hardware.Link
RoboBugBench: Benchmarking Automated Repair of Real Robot Software Under an Audited Behavioral OracleStanford UniversityRoboBugBench collects 33 real defects in a ROS 2 navigation stack and evaluates LLM-generated patches by compiling and running closed-loop simulations instead of unit tests. The work highlights how behavioral oracles admit counterfeit fixes and proposes audited criteria and distributions to better separate true repairs from artifacts, with current models fixing roughly a quarter to a third of bugs.Link

Interaction Latents as Actions for Contact-Rich ManipulationILA learns latent action codes that jointly encode motion, contact, and force evolution, serving as the policy’s output space and target for flow-matching training. A shared decoder maps these latents to executable actions, and a tactile-conditioned expert model uses them for offline improvement, yielding 21–36.5 point success gains across real contact-rich tasks.Link
ForceWAM: Decoupling Future and Response Learning from Demo and Play Data for Contact-Rich ManipulationForceWAM separates prediction of task-compatible force/visual futures (from expert demos) from learning how local actions affect contact responses (from both demos and play). At deployment, an inverse dynamics model tracks predicted futures reactively, achieving 85–95% success on several real-robot contact-regulation tasks and demonstrating the value of play data for force-aware control.Link
Contact Trajectory Prompting: In-Context Transfer of Contact-Rich Behaviors from a Single DemonstrationChinese Academy of SciencesCTP encodes a single demonstration’s contact and motion history into an ordered “contact trajectory prompt” that the policy consults during execution. Using this prompt and online feedback, the robot transfers wiping, rotation, and insertion behaviors one-shot to new objects, controlling not only motion but also contact intensity and direction.Link
EgoMatrix: Physically Faithful Dexterous Robot Demonstrations from Egocentric Human VideosShanghai Jiao Tong University; The University of Hong KongEgoMatrix turns monocular egocentric human videos into bimanual dexterous trajectories and synchronized robot-view observations suitable for replay on real hands. It emphasizes accurate relative hand–object geometry and contact, using RL-based retargeting and diverse background rendering to scale high-fidelity training data for dexterous manipulation.Link
GreenWAM: A World Foundation Model for RoboticsGreenWAM trains a large world foundation model across 20 robot families with a shared “anatomical” action space, supporting both world prediction and policy extraction. It achieves state-of-the-art world-modeling metrics and competitive control performance on multiple benchmarks and transfers better to a real humanoid than competing foundation models.Link
Steertuning Policies from a Few Human VideosSteertuning pretrains a 400M diffusion policy on 100 hours of egocentric human interaction, then adapts to a new task from just five human demos with ~2 minutes of finetuning. During inference it steers sampling using the closest demonstration frame, achieving 71% average success over 12 real tasks with under 5 minutes from data collection to deployment.Link

Conditioning the action expert on these latent futures improves manipulation generalization, achieving strong results on RoboTwin, LIBERO suites, and real-robot tasks.Link
