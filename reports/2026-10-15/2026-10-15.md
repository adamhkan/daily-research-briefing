## Executive Summary

- Strong emphasis on contact-rich and dexterous manipulation: multiple works introduce new action/latent representations, tactile prediction and conditioning, and sim-to-real data engines for fine control.
- Long-horizon and whole-body control are recurring themes, spanning mobile manipulation, humanoid locomotion, quadruped navigation, and hierarchical benchmarks for extended task sequences.
- World models and world-action models continue to mature, with several papers proposing lighter or more structured futures (boxes, occupancy, SE(3) states, latent predictions) to support planning and policy optimization.
- Navigation research focuses on robust long-horizon operation under uncertainty: new SLAM, memory and mapping schemes, chained-goal and interactive navigation, and safety-aware conformal semantics.
- Agentic and foundation-model-based robotics are increasingly prominent, with coding agents, VLA/world-model hybrids, and self-improvement frameworks that adapt policies post-deployment.
- Benchmarks and datasets targeting robustness—to hardware perturbations, changing environments, or complex assemblies—are expanding evaluation for both manipulation and navigation systems.
