## Executive Summary

- CMU and collaborators push long-horizon manipulation reliability and safety, with subtask-focused value functions (SeeQ), subtask RL fine-tuning (PARTS), and lifecycle safety evaluation (SafeStage).
- New frameworks strengthen whole-body and legged/humanoid control in clutter and low-friction settings, including LIMBO for safe humanoid whole-body control and FootQuery/SABER for perceptive, semantically aware locomotion.
- Motion planning and trajectory optimization advance with GPU-parallel contact-rich planning (CoMET), probabilistic swept-volume models for chance-constrained planning, and a square-root Gauss-Newton iLQR formulation.
- Navigation research spans audio-guided search-and-rescue, social and pedestrian-aware VLN, off-road traversability with VLMs, and path planning under temporary obstacles and quadrotor 3D Gaussian-splat maps.
- VLA and world-action models see rapid refinement around geometry, contact, and failure prediction, including FOCAL-VLA, PSR/ForeTac-VLA, and VLA-Scope, plus multiple new sim-to-real and data-generation pipelines.
- Digital-twin and mapping foundations mature with ForceTwin’s force-informed articulated-object twins, Gaussian-/NeRF-style reconstruction for demos and mapping, and a text-only Scene Language Model for persistent scene memory.

| SAGE: Safety-Aligned Gradient Enforcement for Human--Robot Collaboration | Carnegie Mellon University | SAGE aligns multi-agent RL policies with control-barrier-function safety by internalizing safety projections via a differentiable finite-penalty layer and enforcing team-consistent updates with a Lyapunov-based correction. In multi-humanoid collaborative scenarios, it maintains low collision rates while significantly improving task success and reducing discrepancies between proposed and safety-filtered actions. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21130 |
| Dynamics-Induced Commitment in Learning-Based Robotic Penalty Kicks | Carnegie Mellon University | This work analyzes when a hierarchical humanoid-quadruped penalty system becomes dynamically committed to a shot direction and models the resulting reduced zero-sum game. Using a DIC-Map analysis, they locate commitment about 0.29 s before contact and show that improving the responder’s estimator architecture yields much higher save rates than purely delaying the response. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21100 |
| Constraint-Unified MPC for Over-Actuated Surface Vehicles with Post-Detection Fault Reconfiguration | ETH Zurich | This deployed MPC framework optimizes per-thruster commands for over-actuated surface vessels in a single QP, handling trajectory tracking, allocation, and per-thruster limits while reconfiguring after detected faults. Fielded on Lake Zürich and in Venice performances, it tracks trajectories with centimeter-scale RMS error and remains within tens of centimeters of reference even with multiple thruster failures, outperforming a cascaded control baseline. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21046 |

Results in MuJoCo and hardware show that lateral torso motion and a higher COM improve speed and energy efficiency on low-friction surfaces, while the trend reverses at higher friction, providing design guidance for bipedal locomotion on slippery terrain. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21185 |
