## Executive Summary

- Carnegie Mellon leads several works on language-driven robot control, including closed-loop code replanning for manipulation and benchmarks for robust trajectory prediction.
- Multiple papers advance manipulation robustness and safety, from multimodal intent preservation and residual policy adaptation to plug-and-play force control and specification-grounded safety evaluation.
- Navigation and planning see progress via Dijkstra-as-oracle online SSP planning, scale-consistent monocular SLAM, hybrid terrain-aware planning, and efficient embodied VLN with VLMs.
- Whole-body and humanoid control are pushed forward by sim-to-real loco-manipulation, brachiation via waypoint-guided RL, and stereo-augmented humanoid VLA architectures.
- New benchmarks, datasets, and frameworks (ManiGuard, PROBE, PRISM, LIBERO-VIFO, ControlledShifts, PDDL-ART, TGL) focus on standardized evaluation, safety, and scalable long-horizon skill acquisition.
- Several works target safety and reliability at deployment time, including predictive safety shields with JEPA, recovery-aware GRPO variants, test-time augmentation diagnostics, and calibrated risk modeling.
