Executive Summary

  • Navigation and motion planning dominate today’s papers, with new world models, diffusion planners, and curriculum methods all targeting safer, more reliable trajectories in complex environments.
  • Social and crowd navigation see notable advances via predictive human cost fields and conformal, certified planning for legged robots.
  • A cluster of works pushes vision-language(-action) navigation, including zero-shot object-goal navigation, dialogue-grounded interactive navigation, and efficient aerial VLN with automated preference optimization.
  • Whole-body and mobile-manipulation style control is advanced through hierarchical frameworks for underwater vehicle–manipulator systems and humanoid loco-manipulation via learned latent skills.
  • Dexterous and force-aware manipulation receives attention through new visuo-tactile grasping, low-impedance gripper hardware, tactile/force-learning surveys, and fingertip designs that expand thin-object grasping capabilities.
  • CMU and Stanford contribute institution-matched work on safe autonomy verification, cooperative driving VLAs, human-demonstration capture, and speed-optimized manipulation policies.

SpeedTuning: Speeding Up Policy Execution with Lightweight Reinforcement LearningStanford UniversityIntroduces SpeedTuning, an RL framework that learns a separate policy to select execution speeds for existing manipulation controllers. It accelerates imitation-learned policies by over 2.4× on tasks like pouring and throwing while maintaining comparable success rates and requiring no new demonstrations.Link
Agentic Harnesses: LLM-Driven Verification Layers for Robot AutonomyCarnegie Mellon UniversityProposes an LLM-based verification middleware that judges high-level robot plans before execution, using an ensemble of "LLM-as-a-Judge" experts with chain-of-thought reasoning. The harness achieves about 85% precision across approval classes and blocks 97% of adversarial tasks while introducing minimal false positives/negatives between accept and reject.Link
High Fidelity Capture, Reconstruction, and Transfer of Human Demonstrations for Robot-Assisted BathingCarnegie Mellon UniversityPresents a contact-centric pipeline to capture, reconstruct, and transfer clinician bathing demonstrations, including synchronized motion, shape, contact, and force data. The dataset supports controlling a soft dexterous hand for bathing tasks on a mannequin using both open- and closed-loop strategies, targeting safer pHRI in clinical care.Link
CMU-Drive and V2V-VLA: Cooperative Multi-agent Unified Driving with Reasoning Benchmark and Vehicle-to-Vehicle Vision-Language-Action ModelsCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces CMU-Drive, a closed-loop benchmark for cooperative multi-vehicle autonomous driving, and V2V-VLA, a VLA model that jointly generates ego actions, waypoints, verbal reasoning, and inter-vehicle communication. This establishes a first baseline for multi-agent, end-to-end cooperative driving with explicit language and coordination.Link

Energy-Structured Latent World Models with Neural Time Fields for Physically Constistent Open-World Motion PlanningProposes an Energy-Structured Latent World Model whose latent state explicitly encodes energy and momentum, ensuring causal, physically consistent dynamics. Integrated into Physics-Conditioned Neural Time Fields via the Eikonal equation, it yields navigation policies with higher success, SPL, and lower collision and Eikonal residual than prior latent and time-field planners.Link
Entanglement-Free Trajectory Planning for Tethered Mobile Robots with a Slack TetherDelft University of TechnologyDevelops a three-stage motion planner for tethered mobile robots with slack cables that explicitly models tether entanglement. By building a topological model of the entanglement-free configuration space and solving a homotopy-constrained trajectory problem, it generates dynamically feasible paths that avoid tether tangling in cluttered environments.Link
Hierarchical Fast-Slow ReAct Agent for Zero-Shot Object-Goal NavigationIntroduces a hierarchical navigation agent that combines a fast frontier-based controller with a slow deliberative layer grounded in coordinate-anchored semantic memory. The system reuses prior observations, filters detections via a VLM, and selectively deliberates, achieving state-of-the-art zero-shot object-goal navigation success on HM3D and MP3D.Link
Graph-Guided Safe Diffuser: Topological Graph Guidance for Safe Diffusion PlanningPresents G2SD, a hierarchical diffusion-based planner that uses a learned latent topological graph for high-level planning and conditioned diffusion for low-level trajectory generation. The approach theoretically reduces manifold-rupture-induced constraint violations and empirically boosts collision-free success in Maze2D navigation from ~40–50% to 98%.Link
SAIN: Structure-Aware Interactive Navigation with Active Dialogue Grounding for Mobile RobotHarbin Institute of TechnologyTargets interactive instance-goal navigation under ambiguous language by converting dialogue into persistent structured memories over values, rooms, graphs, and objects. This zero-shot framework uses those memories for frontier ranking and target approach, improving SR and SPL over prior dialogue-enabled baselines without task-specific policy training.Link
Latent World Models with Monotone Planning Costs for Image-Goal NavigationDrexel UniversityTrains a latent world model on top of a frozen DINO encoder with an autoregressive rollout loss plus a Monotone Cost Ranking objective that enforces consistent cost ordering over perturbed action sequences. The resulting planner achieves state-of-the-art image-goal navigation on GNM data and transfers zero-shot to real robots in indoor and outdoor scenes.Link
Hierarchical Topology-Aware Planning and Control of Underwater Vehicle-Manipulator Systems in Confined EnvironmentsProposes MANTA, a three-layer framework that first finds traversable base corridors, then jointly optimizes base–arm trajectories, and finally tracks them via a learned Gaussian-process MBRL policy. It improves success, clearance, and arm-effort metrics over sampling-based baselines for underwater intervention in caves and narrow tubes.Link
Curriculum Generation under Structured Parametric Environments for Robust Navigation PoliciesCornell UniversityIntroduces a gradient-based curriculum generator over continuous environment parameters, with a distribution-shift regularizer for richer latent features. On CarRacing and BipedalWalker variants, it consistently outperforms manual and automated curricula (SPRL, ALP-GMM, reverse curricula) in robustness under parameter variation.Link
MPPI Planning with Gaussian-Based Human Cost Function for Social NavigationAugments MPPI with Predictive Gaussian Interaction Fields that roll out pedestrian forecasts and encode them as anisotropic, speed-scaled motion cones. This yields closed-form, parallelizable costs that reduce collision rates to 0% across crowd densities while preserving real-time performance.Link
Drone-Assisted UAV-UGV Collaboration for Autonomous Navigation in Snow-Covered TerrainDemonstrates a UAV–UGV system where an overhead drone performs snow-robust road segmentation using an efficient U-Net with synthetic snow augmentation and tracks the UGV via YOLOv5 and depth sensing. Fused into a dynamic planner, the setup enables robust ground navigation on snow-covered roads with sub-metre UAV localization accuracy.Link
