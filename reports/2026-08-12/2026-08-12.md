## Executive Summary

- Navigation and motion planning dominate today’s papers, with new world models, diffusion planners, and curriculum methods all targeting safer, more reliable trajectories in complex environments.
- Social and crowd navigation see notable advances via predictive human cost fields and conformal, certified planning for legged robots.
- A cluster of works pushes vision-language(-action) navigation, including zero-shot object-goal navigation, dialogue-grounded interactive navigation, and efficient aerial VLN with automated preference optimization.
- Whole-body and mobile-manipulation style control is advanced through hierarchical frameworks for underwater vehicle–manipulator systems and humanoid loco-manipulation via learned latent skills.
- Dexterous and force-aware manipulation receives attention through new visuo-tactile grasping, low-impedance gripper hardware, tactile/force-learning surveys, and fingertip designs that expand thin-object grasping capabilities.
- CMU and Stanford contribute institution-matched work on safe autonomy verification, cooperative driving VLAs, human-demonstration capture, and speed-optimized manipulation policies.
