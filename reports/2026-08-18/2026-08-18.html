Executive Summary

  • Strong focus on safe, anticipatory aerial and ground navigation: new temporal-barrier CBFs for multi-UAV collision avoidance and tube-based certificates for articulated trucks.
  • Learning-based planners extend from informative Mars exploration and pixel-goal navigation to self-play pursuit-evasion and control-informed racecar planning.
  • Navigation is increasingly semantics- and language-aware, with graph-based object reasoning and evidence-preserving belief memories for object- and language-guided tasks.
  • Manipulation work pushes dexterous skill learning from human data and task-structured flat-object handling, along with dynamic, reaction-critical VLA control and vibration-aware collaborative manipulation.
  • Several papers refine general embodied control and evaluation: LTL-guided chunked manipulation policies, smoother asynchronous VLA control, active-perception for disambiguation, and coverage-aware failure discovery.
  • Autonomous driving and mixed-traffic control continue to adopt RL with curriculum and knowledge-data fusion, while mobile communication agents are repositioned via more tractable spectral-graph proxies.

A Temporal Barrier Framework for Collision Avoidance in Multi-Agent Autonomous Aerial VehiclesMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyIntroduces adversarial time-to-collision (aTTC) as a risk metric embedded in a control barrier function defined directly in time, enabling anticipatory multi-UAV collision avoidance. A neural surrogate makes aTTC usable in real-time QP-based controllers, yielding significantly improved waypoint progress and reduced collisions versus distance-based CBFs in 3D simulations.Link

Across driving, manipulation, and legged locomotion, it finds up to 2× more diverse and severe failures than random or standard active-learning baselines.Link

OccPlanner: Goal-Aware Occupancy-Conditioned Diffusion Planner for Pixel-Goal NavigationPresents a diffusion-based planner that grounds pixel-specified goals into egocentric metric space using learned local 3D occupancy features. Trained with automatically reconstructed L3ROcc labels from monocular RGB videos, it achieves large gains in success rate on pixel-goal navigation and shows initial sim-to-real transfer on a Unitree Go2.Link
PILOT: Privileged Imitation Learning for End-to-End Motion Planning of Autonomous UAVs under Partial ObservabilityIntroduces a privileged imitation-learning framework that distills optimal-control experts into a vision-based end-to-end UAV motion planner. A temporal-convolutional fusion of past depth and odometry plus trajectory parameterization yields safe, dynamically consistent flight in clutter with >80% computational savings and successful zero-shot field deployment.Link
Spatiotemporal Tube-Based Safety-Certificate for Autonomous Navigation of Articulated VehiclesDevelops a spatiotemporal tube-based planner that certifies routes for articulated vehicles, ensuring that towed trailers remain within a traffic corridor under bounded corrections. By exploiting articulated kinematics and sway constraints, it generates safe paths for complex truck–trailer configurations in simulation.Link
Expected Free Energy-based Informative Path Planning for Robotic Mars ExplorationEindhoven University of TechnologyApplies the expected free energy (EFE) objective from active inference to informative path planning under hard budget constraints. Using a Gaussian-process map of an unknown field, the planner jointly balances exploration and exploitation, outperforming information-theoretic baselines in finding high-value regions while keeping travel costs bounded.Link
