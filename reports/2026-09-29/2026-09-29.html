Executive Summary

  • LLM- and VLA-based policy generation and refinement continue to mature, spanning whole-body humanoid loco-manipulation, executable manipulation programs, and planning–execution alignment.
  • Contact-rich and dexterous manipulation sees strong advances in impedance modulation, tactile phase retrieval, blind grasping, and co-training from human and robot demonstrations.
  • Navigation and motion planning work covers socially steerable diffusion planners, GPU-accelerated multi-robot and space–time RRT variants, and new mapping/localization stacks for construction, off-road, and UAV settings.
  • Whole-body and mobile manipulation are pushed in both humanoid and aerial domains, combining onboard perception, imitation/RL, and model-predictive control for challenging real-world tasks.
  • New mapping, VIO, and open-vocabulary scene-graph systems tighten the perception–control loop for legged robots, drones, and service platforms in resource-constrained or visually ambiguous environments.

Representation-Guided Generation and Integration of Executable Programs for Robot ManipulationMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyRIVET uses a shared object-centric representation (6D poses plus relation graph) to guide VLMs in generating interoperable perception, relation-inference, rendering, and planning code for manipulation tasks. Once authored for a domain, these executable programs are reused across new configurations without regenerating code, achieving 83% real-world success on stacking and assembly benchmarks.Link
See to Reach, Feel to Grasp: Learning A Blind Grasp Reflex for Anthropomorphic Robotic HandsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThis work separates global arm motion from local hand control to learn a proprioception-only "blind grasp reflex" on an anthropomorphic hand. A reinforcement-learning policy, conditioned solely on hand joint sensing, robustly closes and stabilizes grasps across objects and arm controllers, with a learned stability score aligning closely to a physics-based grasp metric.Link
HuGo: LLMs as Whole-Body Policy Code Designers for Humanoid Loco-ManipulationUC BerkeleyHuGo leverages an LLM to synthesize high-level policy code atop a frozen low-level whole-body controller for humanoid loco-manipulation. Using rollout feedback (trajectories and video), the system iteratively refines task logic without task-specific rewards or demonstrations, approaching demo-based performance in simulation and transferring zero-shot to hardware with further real-world refinement.Link
Precision at Speed: Sample-Efficient Online Model-Based Reinforcement Learning for Hydraulic Excavator ControlETH ZurichThe authors present an online model-based RL framework that learns a probabilistic dynamics ensemble for sampling-based MPC directly on a 11.5-ton hydraulic excavator. With a precision-gated contouring objective, the system attains sub-centimeter path tracking in 40 minutes of real interaction, matching or exceeding prior controllers that require much longer data collection.Link
Design and Characterization of a Variable-Length Continuum Mechanism with Force LockingStanford UniversityThis paper introduces a rod-driven helical continuum mechanism that combines variable length with a force-locking mode to boost stiffness for high-force tasks. Experiments show 180° bending with <10% distal error and demonstrate anisotropic stiffness gains, tunable via rod tension, enabling both flexible navigation and rigid manipulation behaviors.Link
Policy-Calibrated DAgger: Offline Calibrated Noise Injection for Imitation LearningCarnegie Mellon UniversityPolicy-Calibrated DAgger estimates a generative policy’s action noise offline from its own diffusion action spread and closed-loop error, removing manual noise tuning in dataset aggregation. In cluttered reaching tasks, it matches the best hindsight noise level and outperforms no-noise aggregation, without risking unsafe on-robot exploration.Link

Generate, Track, Improve: Perceptive Multi-Skill Humanoid Locomotion with RL-Fine-Tuned Motion GeneratorsA two-layer locomotion stack couples a depth-based flow-matching motion generator with a perceptive tracking policy trained via control-guided RL for humanoids. An off-policy fine-tuning loop using advantage-weighted regression significantly improves terrain consistency and skill composition, enabling a Unitree G1 to robustly walk, run, jump, and traverse stairs outdoors.Link
dRVG: Quadtree-Guided, Resolution-Complete Online Motion Planning for Polygonal Robots in Unknown EnvironmentsdRVG incrementally builds rotation-stacked visibility graphs guided by a quadtree over sensed space, planning translations and rotations for polygonal robots without a fixed grid. Under mild geometric assumptions it is resolution-complete relative to full-map RVG, and experimentally solves all tested cases with sub-second median planning on challenging maps.Link
Transformer-based Monte Carlo Localization in Construction MeshesThe authors propose a LiDAR-based global relocalization system for construction sites that learns an observation model over building meshes via PointNet++ and a transformer decoder. Embedded in an MCL framework with uncertainty-aware decoding and hypothesis injection, it outperforms diffusion and ScanContext++ baselines while running at 18 ms per update.Link
Quadruped Obstacle Avoidance and Footstep Planning with Distributed Low-cost Time-of-Flight SensorsUniversity of OxfordThis work equips a quadruped with multiple low-cost ToF sensors distributed around its body to replace or augment depth cameras for near-field perception. Despite higher noise and lower resolution, the architecture delivers centimeter-level local mapping that suffices for obstacle avoidance and footstep planning, at significantly reduced cost and power.Link
SoGuDiff: Socially Guided Diffusion for Steerable, Norm-Grounded Robot NavigationSoGuDiff is a diffusion-based navigation planner whose social behavior is parameterized along continuous style axes such as passing distance, yielding side, and group deference. A feasibility projection layer enforces kinematic and collision constraints, and experiments reveal style–safety tradeoff curves that strictly dominate fixed-behavior baselines.Link
Memory-Aware Multi-Sensor Perception for Efficient and Safe Navigation in Dynamic EnvironmentsThe authors propose a navigation framework that fuses LiDAR and RGB into an online learned distance-field representation, separating static structure from dynamic obstacles. A modulated control-barrier-function QP controller exploits this persistent memory to safely squeeze through narrow passages and efficiently reach goals in cluttered dynamic scenes.Link
DGT-Map: Directional Global Traversability Mapping Utilizing Multi-Task Learning for Heterogeneous VehiclesDGT-Map learns vehicle-conditioned, heading-dependent costmaps from RGB-D and proprioception via a shared backbone and per-vehicle heads, capturing anisotropic and platform-specific traversability. Integrated with Hybrid A*, it improves success on off-road navigation tasks like asymmetric slopes and vehicle-selective ridges over geometric and isotropic learned baselines.Link
NavGen: Visual Generative Models as a Scalable Data Engine for Embodied 3D NavigationNavGen uses text-to-video generative models to synthesize ~400k diverse vision-language navigation episodes for UAVs across indoor/outdoor settings, including style-diversified long-tail scenarios. Policies trained on this synthetic data scale favorably and transfer to real UAV flights within a world-action-model framework, achieving 75% success on varied navigation tasks.Link
Learning Vision-Based Agile Gap Traversal: Differentiable Simulation with a Warm-Started CriticA two-stage RL pipeline trains quadrotor visuomotor policies for agile gap traversal using quasi-analytical policy gradients that avoid backpropagating through rendering. A privileged expert and critic are first trained with gap geometry, then a visual policy is learned with a warm-started critic, yielding higher success and efficiency than BPTT-based or cold-start baselines and transferring to hardware.Link
VkVIO: Cross-platform GPU Acceleration for Visual-Inertial Odometry with VulkanVkVIO is a vendor-agnostic GPU-accelerated VIO pipeline implemented on Vulkan, targeting diverse hardware from workstations to low-cost SBCs. It provides causal, real-time state estimates with state-of-the-art accuracy and outperforms CUDA-based systems on the same platforms, enabling low-latency, low-power odometry for navigation and XR.Link
