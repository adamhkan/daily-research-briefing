## Executive Summary

- LLM- and VLA-based policy generation and refinement continue to mature, spanning whole-body humanoid loco-manipulation, executable manipulation programs, and planning–execution alignment.
- Contact-rich and dexterous manipulation sees strong advances in impedance modulation, tactile phase retrieval, blind grasping, and co-training from human and robot demonstrations.
- Navigation and motion planning work covers socially steerable diffusion planners, GPU-accelerated multi-robot and space–time RRT variants, and new mapping/localization stacks for construction, off-road, and UAV settings.
- Whole-body and mobile manipulation are pushed in both humanoid and aerial domains, combining onboard perception, imitation/RL, and model-predictive control for challenging real-world tasks.
- New mapping, VIO, and open-vocabulary scene-graph systems tighten the perception–control loop for legged robots, drones, and service platforms in resource-constrained or visually ambiguous environments.
