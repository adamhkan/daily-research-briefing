Executive Summary

  • New whole-body and shape-control methods span rigid humanoids (BRIDGE) and continuum soft arms, targeting more human-like and constraint-aware motion.
  • Navigation and driving see several end-to-end world-model and VLA advances, from quadruped parkour and air–ground VLN to multi-style autonomous driving and efficient surround-view WMs.
  • Robotic manipulation work ranges from compact and robust VLA policies (MINERVA, WISE, EGR) to task-adaptive grasping, bimanual household skills, and tool-specific gripper design.
  • World-model research is rapidly maturing, with applications from spacecraft rendezvous (Stanford) to goal-conditioned planning and safety-critical decision making.
  • New evaluation and infrastructure papers (FailBench, R2S-Eval, miniature driving platform, RoboTok) target data, benchmarking, and internet-scale supervision for dexterous learning.
  • Mapping, state estimation, and safety monitoring advances (semantic ontology mapping, adaptive radar–LiDAR odometry, predictive zonotope reduction, robust pose registration) aim to harden autonomy stacks under uncertainty.

BRIDGE: An Open-Source Humanoid Platform via Morphology-Control Co-Design for Physical AICarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces a data-driven morphology–control co-design framework for humanoids that jointly optimizes body design and whole-body control to better match human motion. A new morphology-fidelity metric combines kinematic retargeting and dynamic tracking, and the resulting 88 cm BRIDGE platform achieves SOTA performance in locomotion, balance, and dynamic maneuvers with open-source hardware and control.Link
GPU-Accelerated Astrodynamics World Models for Spacecraft Rendezvous and Proximity OperationsStanford UniversityPresents a JAX-based, GPU-accelerated ISS docking environment and a transformer world model tailored to spacecraft rendezvous with visual and kinematic inputs. The learned model supports planning for docking under keep-out constraints, improves success and generalization over RL baselines, and detects anomalous objects with high accuracy, offering a template for world-model-based space robotics.Link

FWBC-VLA: Force-Aware Whole-Body Compensation for Contact-Rich Loco-ManipulationProposes a framework that couples a VLA action generator with a force-aware whole-body compensation layer for wheeled-legged robots performing contact-rich loco-manipulation. A sensorless residual-torque estimator infers contact forces, which are injected as tokens into the VLA and a compensation generator, yielding improved robustness in tasks like whiteboard wiping and door opening.Link
Real-Time Shape Control of Multi-Segment Soft Robotic Arms Using Koopman Operators with Global and Local ObservablesIntroduces a Koopman-based MPC framework for real-time shape control of multi-segment soft arms that combines global and local observables. Demonstrated on 3–10 segment robots, it achieves accurate, disturbance-robust shape tracking at up to 0.6 m/s and scales to high segment counts, enabling constrained-space manipulation and inspection.Link
Adaptive Vision-Language Grasping via Composable Foundation Priors and Generalizable Grasp SynthesisAdaRoboVLG decouples task understanding from physical grasp synthesis by combining a generalizable grasp policy with modular foundation-model priors. Explicit kinematic mapping and force-closure-based evaluation generate feasible grasps, while composable spatial, cognitive, and temporal priors adapt them to context without retraining, improving grasping in cluttered and dynamic scenes.Link
Robot Aware Computational Design of Object Specific Passive Grippers for Additive ManufacturingPresents an end-to-end pipeline that turns an object mesh, pose, and robot model into an additively manufacturable, passive object-specific gripper. The method jointly reasons over contact sampling, wrench feasibility, capture mechanisms, robot kinematics, and topology optimization, yielding printable grippers traceable to their originating design assumptions.Link
Scaling Bimanual Household Manipulation from 1,500 hours of Demonstrations to On-Policy CorrectionsPeking UniversityReleases a 1,500-hour dataset of diverse bimanual household manipulation and introduces XR-2, a VLA model trained with a high-throughput pipeline. Systematic scaling experiments with additional demonstrations and DAgger-style corrective data show steady improvements in success, highlighting strong scaling properties for generalist bimanual policies.Link
MINERVA: How Small Can a Manipulation Policy Be and Still Solve LIBERO?University of TokyoStudies the capacity floor of LIBERO by designing compact visuomotor policies; a 0.54M-parameter model reaches ~95% success, close to billion-parameter VLAs while running in milliseconds on a CPU. Results expose task memorization effects in LIBERO instructions and motivate capacity-aware policy design and distillation for deployment-efficient manipulation.Link
WISE: World-model-guided Imagination Scheduling for Efficient Post-training of Vision-Language-Action ModelsTsinghua UniversityWISE coordinates when and how to use world-model rollouts to refine VLA policies for manipulation, selectively invoking imagination near critical interaction states and bounding rollout horizons. Applied to π0 and π0.5, it improves robustness and generalization under distribution shifts while cutting GPU compute by about 80% compared with naive full imagination.Link
GIFT: Guided Intermediate Feature Training via Action-Oriented Structural Supervision for Robotic ManipulationChinese Academy of SciencesGIFT introduces control-structured intermediate feature training that aligns visual features with geometry, affordances, and goals for manipulation policies. Plugged into VLA and world-action models, it yields sizeable gains on LIBERO-Plus and RoboCasa, especially for articulated objects and precise tasks under novel visual/spatial perturbations.Link
Sensing Which Modality Matters: Evidence-Gated Regularization for Robust VLA PoliciesMitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories; University of North Carolina at Chapel HillProposes Evidence-Gated Regularization, which learns per-frame, per-sensor relevance signals and enforces invariance on low-evidence modalities while ensuring sufficiency of high-evidence ones. Without inference-time overhead, it substantially improves VLA robustness to sensor corruption, occlusions, and distractors in both simulation tasks and real bi-manual and tactile setups.Link
MulDP: Multimodal Diffusion Policy for Autonomous Quadruped Parkour Navigation across Complex TerrainsIntroduces MulDP, a diffusion policy that fuses vision, proprioception, and goal inputs to generate anticipatory velocity commands for quadruped robots performing parkour. Trained on a new Quadruped Parkour Navigation Dataset, it achieves robust long-horizon autonomous traversal over complex terrains in simulation and real-world experiments.Link
