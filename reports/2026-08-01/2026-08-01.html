Executive Summary

  • Cross-embodiment transfer emerges as a theme, with new methods for sharing behaviors and policies across robot bodies and even between arms and UAVs.
  • Multiple works strengthen manipulation robustness: from zero-shot skill transfer across object geometries to failure-aware training and targeted data repair.
  • Tactile and contact-rich manipulation capabilities advance via new high-speed curved tactile sensors and mechanics-aware world models.
  • Navigation research spans social leg-aware local planners, agricultural VLN with traversability checks, and robust 4D radar odometry under adverse conditions.
  • Several system-level frameworks (RoboBRIDGE, World Action Planner) focus on turning strong policy models into reliable, generalizable robotic agents.

SemAnCorr: Semantic Anchored Correspondence for Zero-Shot Manipulation Skill TransferCarnegie Mellon University; UC BerkeleySemAnCorr provides a training-free way to build dense, semantically consistent, and geometrically coherent correspondences across functionally similar but geometrically diverse objects. By jointly optimizing for pose and correspondence to select anchor regions and propagating them via functional maps, it significantly outperforms prior descriptor-based methods and enables reliable zero-shot skill transfer from a single demo to unseen objects in real manipulation.Link
Cross-Embodiment Transfer via Behavior-Aligned RepresentationsStanford UniversityThis work studies which intermediate representations help vision-language-action models transfer skills between different robot embodiments. It finds that behavior-aligned signals such as end-effector traces substantially improve cross-embodiment and sim-to-real transfer, especially when large prior datasets are available, boosting real-robot task progress by 28% over baseline policies.Link

PAC-MAN: Perception-Aware CBF-RL for Whole-Body Safety in Humanoid DodgeballCalifornia Institute of Technology; Technology Innovation InstitutePAC-MAN integrates control barrier functions with reinforcement learning under realistic onboard sensing to achieve whole-body safe evasive behavior on a humanoid playing dodgeball. The study dissects how perceptual observability affects usable barrier structure and deploys a lightweight link-level policy on a Unitree G1, achieving 95% evasion success despite imperfect perception.Link
X-NavDP: Generalizing Navigation Diffusion Policy to Novel Behavior and Embodiments with Group Q-score Reweighted MatchingX-NavDP post-trains a pretrained navigation diffusion policy via a new RL framework, GQRM, which stabilizes gradients and improves exploration by behavior perturbation and group Q-score normalization. Trained across heterogeneous robot bodies, the method achieves strong cross-embodiment visual navigation gains, with large success-rate improvements in both simulation and challenging real-world cases.Link
UniCross: Unified Cross-Skill Dexterous Manipulation SynthesisUniCross proposes a single formulation that unifies grasping, relocation, in-hand rotation, and in-hand translation within a shared state-action and objective space. This allows distilling one cross-skill policy that performs well across all skills, generalizes to new objects and hand morphologies, and chains skills seamlessly for long-horizon dexterous tasks.Link
Learning Social Robot Navigation By Sensing Human LegsThe authors introduce CALF, an end-to-end policy that reasons directly over leg-level LiDAR returns using convolution and attention to produce socially compliant navigation commands. Trained in a JAX-based simulator with a gait model, the policy transfers zero-shot to a TurtleBot 4, yielding smooth, human-aware trajectories among pedestrians.Link
FA-RDP: A Frequency-Adaptive Reactive Diffusion Policy for Contact-Rich ManipulationShanghai Jiao Tong UniversityFA-RDP tackles the trade-off between pre-contact trajectory multimodality and post-contact reactivity in diffusion policies. A multi-frequency visual-force Transformer and learned multimodality indicator switch between low-frequency multi-step sampling and high-frequency one-step inference, while a manifold-consistency distillation aligns actions to the robot manifold, leading to higher success on contact-rich tasks.Link
TEA-AgriVLN: Traversability Estimation Alarm for Agricultural Vision-and-Language NavigationTEA-AgriVLN augments agricultural vision-and-language navigation with a traversability estimation module that flags misalignments between proposed actions and terrain safety. Integrated into AgriVLN, this alarm-driven reconsideration improves success rate and navigation error on the A2A benchmark, addressing ambiguous traversability in crop fields and outdoor farm scenes.Link
FasTac: A Curved Multispectral Vision-Based Tactile Sensor for High-Speed High-Precision 3D Shape and Force PerceptionFasTac is a curved fingertip sensor that combines multispectral photometric stereo, tailored depth reconstruction, and FPGA acceleration to deliver fast, precise 3D contact geometry and three-axis force estimates. It attains sub-0.05 mm depth MAE and low force NMAE, enabling high-speed feedback for dexterous grasping and dynamic contact sensing.Link
TacWAM: Anchor-Guided World Action Model with Mechanics-Aware Tactile PredictionTacWAM extends world action models by incorporating rich tactile futures without leaking privileged information to the action branch. A spatially aligned tactile encoder, tactile history, and anchor-guided tri-modal attention use predicted tactile sequences purely as supervision, yielding large gains (up to 37.5 percentage points) on diverse real contact-rich manipulation tasks.Link
RaDiVe: Robust 4D Radar Odometry with Distance-Bounded NDT and Velocity-Discrepancy Point UncertaintyRaDiVe improves 4D radar odometry by constraining NDT registration to near-distance voxel pairs and weighting points via a velocity-discrepancy model that compares measured and expected Doppler. Combined with SDF-based surface extraction, this yields markedly lower translational and rotational ATE than prior methods while remaining real-time.Link
Static In, Dynamic Out: Counterfactual Action Augmentation for Moving Object ManipulationSIDO turns static-object demonstrations into supervision for moving-target manipulation by counterfactually displacing objects and morphing action chunks to preserve hand-object relative pose. With a learned object motion predictor at test time, the resulting goal-conditioned policies significantly improve performance on moving-object tasks in simulation and two real-world setups without sacrificing static performance.Link
