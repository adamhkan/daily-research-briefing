## Executive Summary

- Cross-embodiment transfer emerges as a theme, with new methods for sharing behaviors and policies across robot bodies and even between arms and UAVs.
- Multiple works strengthen manipulation robustness: from zero-shot skill transfer across object geometries to failure-aware training and targeted data repair.
- Tactile and contact-rich manipulation capabilities advance via new high-speed curved tactile sensors and mechanics-aware world models.
- Navigation research spans social leg-aware local planners, agricultural VLN with traversability checks, and robust 4D radar odometry under adverse conditions.
- Several system-level frameworks (RoboBRIDGE, World Action Planner) focus on turning strong policy models into reliable, generalizable robotic agents.

| SemAnCorr: Semantic Anchored Correspondence for Zero-Shot Manipulation Skill Transfer | Carnegie Mellon University; UC Berkeley | SemAnCorr provides a training-free way to build dense, semantically consistent, and geometrically coherent correspondences across functionally similar but geometrically diverse objects. By jointly optimizing for pose and correspondence to select anchor regions and propagating them via functional maps, it significantly outperforms prior descriptor-based methods and enables reliable zero-shot skill transfer from a single demo to unseen objects in real manipulation. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28382 |
