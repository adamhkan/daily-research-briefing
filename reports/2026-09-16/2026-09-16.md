## Executive Summary

- CMU introduces GeomVLA, a 3D vision-language-action model that jointly reasons about scene geometry, motion, and actions, achieving SOTA manipulation without robot-action pretraining.
- NYU proposes VFAL, fusing foundation-model vision with force-based admittance control to achieve millimeter-precision peg insertion into moving targets using failure-aware online adaptation.
- Mobile manipulation and navigation see multiple advances in long-horizon memory, world-model-based planning, and costmap/CBF safety layers, targeting more robust operation in clutter and under uncertainty.
- Dexterous and whole-body control progresses via tactile-driven and predicted-touch manipulation, cross-embodiment humanoid control foundations, and force-aware wheeled-legged loco-manipulation.
- Safety, verification, and reachability are emphasized in new neural HJ reachability solvers, invariant-set computation, and hybrid safety-filter architectures for humanoids and mobile robots.
- MIT and Stanford contribute new testbeds and planners for safety-critical navigation in space and autonomous driving, including chance-constrained belief-space maneuvering and scalable reachability value learning.

| Vision-Force Admittance Learning for Peg Insertion into a Movable Hole | New York University | VFAL combines asynchronous visual pose estimates from vision foundation models with high-frequency force-based admittance control to handle peg insertion into moving or uncertain targets. | ResSafe: Learning Safety Filtering with Residual Reinforcement Learning for Humanoids | UC Berkeley | ResSafe splits humanoid control into a task-focused nominal policy and a residual safety policy that learns corrective actions, effectively acting as a learned safety filter. This residual RL formulation improves the performance–safety trade-off and reduces the need for delicate multi-objective reward tuning in a single monolithic controller. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15988 |
| DreamSat-Bench: Development and Initial Testing of a Testbed for AI-Based Pose Estimation from 3D Reconstruction | Massachusetts Institute of Technology | DreamSat-Bench is a modular rendezvous and proximity-operations testbed that couples MuJoCo/Isaac/LeRobot simulation with robotic-arm motion to benchmark AI-based relative navigation. | Gradient-Free Neural Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability for Scalable Safety-Critical Control | Stanford University | This work learns discrete-time HJ reachability value functions and safety policies without computing spatial gradients, instead exploiting bang-bang structure to turn adversarial optimization into supervised policy learning. A windowed temporal curriculum stabilizes long-horizon value propagation, enabling scalable safety filters up to 80D systems and real-time deployment on an F1-tenth car with zero-shot generalization to new tracks. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14087 |
| Chance-Constrained Belief-Space Maneuver Planning for Autonomous Collision Avoidance Under Uncertainty | Stanford University | The authors frame satellite conjunction response as chance-constrained belief-space planning over Gaussian orbital uncertainties using Monte Carlo tree search that anticipates future tracking updates. Their planner decides when to wait for better information versus commit to a maneuver while enforcing a terminal collision-risk bound, reducing unnecessary maneuvers yet avoiding risk violations on historical conjunction cases. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13428 |
