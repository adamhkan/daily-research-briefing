Executive Summary

  • Certified and optimization-based planning tools (CSymPlan, OpenSCvx, GCS-Bridging) advance safety and scalability for high-DOF and convex-region motion planning.
  • Multiple works integrate language, vision, and geometry for robust navigation and manipulation, spanning SLAM (SuperMap), semantic navigation (OptiSight, RACO), and mobile manipulation (TONAV, DreamMimic).
  • World/vision-language-action models continue to mature, with new interfaces, memory, and supervision schemes (Pointing-VLA, UniMem, CounterAlign, SCVC, DELE-w0.5, WAM-OPD) improving robustness and long-horizon control.
  • Whole-body and quadrupedal control (DreamMimic, DELTA, GuRO) achieve better locomotion and loco-manipulation via world models, terrain-aware encoders, and curvature-aware optimization.
  • Robustness to uncertainty, faults, and dynamics mismatch is a key theme, with works on fault-tolerant navigation, physics-filtered feedback, force/torque-based adaptation, and improved Sim2Real.
  • Benchmarks and system architectures (InstructMove, The Imitator Game, GOLEM, Physical Agentic AI) emphasize evaluation and modular orchestration for real-world, multi-robot, and humanoid deployments.

CSymPlan: Certified Symbolic Planning and Control for High-DOF ManipulatorsNew York UniversityIntroduces CSymPlan, a certified reach-avoid planning-and-control framework for high-DOF arms that jointly reasons about dynamics, disturbances, and safety. It provides both offline precomputed feedback policies and online symbolic synthesis, achieving zero safety violations on Franka FR3 tasks by refusing to execute uncertified commands.Link
OpenSCvx: An Open-Source Modular and Extensible Nonlinear Trajectory Planning PackageUniversity of WashingtonPresents OpenSCvx, a Python framework that lifts high-level trajectory-optimization problem descriptions into solver-ready formulations. It supports continuous-time constraints, temporal/logical specs, batching, and modular backends, easing development and comparison of advanced motion-planning algorithms.Link
UniMem: Unifying Multimodal Memory and Control for Vision-Language-Action ModelsStanford UniversityProposes UniMem, a single-backbone VLA framework where an event classifier and keyframe encoder maintain multimodal memory directly within the policy. It improves non-Markovian manipulation/navigation tasks in sim and hardware while simplifying training compared to hierarchical or multi-model memory pipelines.Link
In-Situ Reconstruction of the International Space Station Using 3D Gaussian Splatting and AstrobeeUC BerkeleyUses 3D Gaussian Splatting to reconstruct high-fidelity 3D maps of the ISS Kibo module from Astrobee grayscale images. Demonstrates that a few hundred in-situ images are sufficient to build fast-rendering, high-quality maps suitable for free-flyer navigation and interior mapping updates.Link
MIVIFI: Bridging Perspective and Fisheye Domains for Training Multi-View Fisheye Image Generation ModelsETH ZurichIntroduces MIVIFI, a cross-domain generative framework that leverages perspective datasets to train multi-view fisheye image generators for surround perception. By combining KITTI-360 fisheye and nuScenes data via equirectangular projections, it synthesizes photorealistic fisheye views for rare events and varied conditions.Link

GCS-Bridging: Restoring Connectivity of Disconnected Convex Sets for Graph-of-Convex-Sets Motion PlanningAddresses disconnected Graph-of-Convex-Sets (GCS) maps by inflating collision-free point paths into new convex regions that reconnect start and goal components. Achieves 99.8% connectivity restoration in simulation and validates on a Franka arm, strengthening reliability of convex-optimization-based motion planning.Link
TONAV: Task-Oriented Navigation and Action-Velocity Chunk Learning for Articulated Object Quadrupedal Mobile ManipulationSchool of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Hunan University, China.Proposes TONAV, a quadruped mobile-manipulation framework that fuses vision-language-based task-oriented navigation with velocity-supervised action chunks for stable contact-rich manipulation. It closes the gap between base positioning and manipulation readiness, boosting success on real articulated-object tasks.Link
DreamMimic: Learning Visuomotor Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation via World ModelTsinghua UniversityIntroduces DreamMimic, which distills privileged teacher policies into vision-based humanoid controllers using a world model as a predictive latent space and supervision signal. Auxiliary prediction heads and performance-conditioned guidance stabilize learning of long-horizon loco-manipulation behaviors from images.Link
OptiSight: Bridging Semantic Reasoning and Geometric Control for Embodied NavigationCombines VLM-based semantic reasoning with geometric visual servoing in a finite-state "Chain-of-Thought" navigation framework. Grounded-SAM provides open-vocabulary target localization and pure-geometry control executes continuous navigation, yielding reliable zero-shot indoor navigation in Habitat under tight VRAM budgets.Link
SuperMap: A Spatio-Temporal SLAM System for Visual-Language NavigationPresents SuperMap, a 4D spatio-temporal mapping system that fuses high-rate geometric SLAM with asynchronous open-vocabulary perception. It maintains stable object identities and prunes outdated content, exposing a queryable 4D scene graph tailored for language-guided navigation in dynamic environments.Link
Triplet2Track: A Hierarchical System with Object-Centric Representations for Reliable Long-Horizon ManipulationShanghai Jiao Tong UniversityIntroduces the Triplet-to-Track System, a closed-loop imitation framework that encodes subgoals as instance-grounded semantic triplets and converts them to continuous tracks for execution. Using human videos and online progress monitoring, it achieves strong reliability on long-horizon manipulation with object-level and compositional generalization.Link
DELTA: Deformable Elevation-Based Local Terrain Attention Encoder for Sparse-Terrain Quadrupedal LocomotionKorea Advanced Institute of Science and TechnologyProposes DELTA, a deformable attention encoder that samples state-conditioned elevation patches to build fixed-size terrain tokens independent of map resolution. It matches dense attention encoders in performance while enabling higher-resolution maps and better sparse-terrain quadrupedal locomotion, including sim-to-real transfer on RAIBO2.Link
