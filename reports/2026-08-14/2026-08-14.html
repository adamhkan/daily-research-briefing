Executive Summary

  • ContactIPM introduces a faster, structure-exploiting interior-point solver for contact-implicit trajectory optimization, advancing whole-body motion planning with contacts.
  • New datasets and generators (DaViNCi, RoadWeaver, TrafficDiffuser, D3D-GEN, HandEdit) broaden realistic evaluation for navigation, driving, and dexterous manipulation.
  • Several works (RIFT, World Tokens, MiDAS, StellaVLA, G0.5) refine how future prediction, demonstrations, and world modeling are used in VLA control, improving efficiency and OOD robustness.
  • Safety and reliability for autonomous driving and navigation are strengthened via neuro-symbolic safety guards, hybrid behavior planners, risk-sensitive routing, and adversarial test generation.
  • Embodied agent infrastructure (Thea, SHAPER) and failure monitoring for OpenVLA highlight the growing role of non-parametric harnesses and diagnostics in robust robot deployment.

Early Warning Signals for OpenVLA Failure under Visual Distribution ShiftNew York UniversityAnalyzes whether internal activations of a frozen OpenVLA policy contain early-warning signals before failures caused by visual distribution shifts. Linear probes fitted on intermediate layers retrospectively separate failing from successful trajectories well, but also trigger frequent false alarms in clean runs, highlighting the gap between retrospective discriminability and practically useful online warning behavior.Link

ContactIPM: A Structure-Exploiting Interior-Point Solver for Contact-Implicit Trajectory OptimizationProposes ContactIPM, a primal–dual interior-point method tailored to contact-implicit trajectory optimization with complementarity constraints. By identifying complementary inequality pairs, using barrier-coupled relaxations, and exploiting optimal-control structure via Riccati recursions, it achieves 2–9× speedups and higher robustness over prior MPCC solvers on contact-rich manipulation and transport benchmarks.Link
Learning Loco-Manipulation From SMPC Demonstrations With Sparse Offline-to-Online RLTechnical University of MunichCombines sample-based MPC in simulation with sparse-reward offline-to-online RL to learn integrated locomotion and manipulation skills. The high-level RL agent, paired with a low-level stability controller, surpasses its MPC teacher and transfers sim-to-real on both an arm-equipped quadruped and a humanoid for complex loco-manipulation.Link
DaViNCi: A Dataset Towards Outdoor Vision-and-Language Navigation with Continuous Actions and Dynamic ElementsShanghai Jiao Tong UniversityIntroduces DaViNCi, an outdoor vision-and-language navigation dataset with continuous control and dynamic elements such as moving agents. Results show substantial performance drops compared to prior discrete-graph VLN datasets, underscoring the challenges of realistic outdoor navigation and the value of DaViNCi for advancing VLN research.Link
Scalable Multi-Agent Maze Traversal with Local CommunicationPresents a distributed algorithm for many robots to explore and traverse unknown maze-like graphs using only local communication and leader–follower coordination. The method is complete, near-optimal in makespan with respect to full-knowledge strategies, and empirically scales to hundreds of agents while reducing total fuel usage vs. independent exploration.Link
IoT-Enabled Autonomous Maritime Navigation in Smart Ports: A Curriculum-Guided Shared Policy Learning FrameworkNanyang Technological University; National Taiwan UniversityDevelops a curriculum-guided RL framework with a shared recurrent policy for autonomous maritime navigation in congested smart ports under partial observability. Centralized offline training with fully onboard execution improves collision avoidance, navigation reliability, and scalability across varying traffic densities.Link

The approach reduces wind-induced return failures and boosts mission success rates compared to static or deterministic routing models.Link
