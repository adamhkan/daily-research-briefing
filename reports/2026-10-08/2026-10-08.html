Executive Summary

  • Mobile manipulation and humanoid loco-manipulation see multiple advances in data generation, policy training, and robustness (MobileVISTA, VOMMI, Humanoid Horizon, iGPC, BiGym 2.0).
  • Navigation and motion planning papers span agile UAV flight, sensor-layout-agnostic navigation, urban driving with learned world models, and safe crowd/crowd-like environments (NMPP, Sensor-Layout-Agnostic Navigation, RIWANav, risk-sensitive crowd navigation).
  • World-action/world-model work continues to unify video prediction and control, with frameworks for composable WAMs, planning efficiency, and robust latent-space decision making (OpenWAM, SufficientPlan, ACG-WAM, DepthWorld, robust latent disturbances).
  • Safety and control under uncertainty feature prominently: context-conditioned reachability, adaptive CBFs, risk-sensitive MPC, and new collision-cone and scenario-based guarantees (Context-Conditioned HJ Reachability, LBA-CBF, SC3BF, SH-MPC under mode uncertainty).
  • Multi-robot and collaborative settings gain richer planning and control tools, from stochastic multi-robot skills and density-based collaborative transport to decentralized search and communication network formation.
  • Institution-aligned work from CMU, Stanford, Berkeley, MIT, ETH Zurich, UCSD, and UW emphasizes legged and humanoid control, safety certificates, world-action models, locomotion planning, and efficient VLA architectures.

OpenWAM: An Open Framework for Composable World-Action ModelsStanford UniversityDefines a common causal robot-video backbone with configurable video–action interaction, enabling systematic comparison of WAM designs and counterfactual dynamics learning. Shows strong gains on LIBERO bimanual benchmarks and real robot tasks, and demonstrates how counterfactual transitions improve inverse and forward dynamics beyond demonstration-only training.Link
Model-Based Geometry-Aware Generative Optimization for Constrained Locomotion PlanningCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces 2GO, a model-based diffusion-style planner that embeds active constraint geometry into denoising for quadruped and humanoid constrained locomotion. By shaping scores with normal-induced metrics and adaptive diffusion/flow schedules, it improves success, reduces constraint violations, and enhances execution compatibility over prior constrained MBD methods.Link
EigenDEXplore: Structured Exploration for Dexterous Manipulation with Human PriorsStanford UniversityUses eigenvectors from human hand motion to structure exploration noise while keeping the native joint action space, improving discovery of coordinated behaviors in RL and trajectory optimization. Across dexterous hands and tasks (grasping, in-hand reorientation, contact-rich skills), the method outperforms joint-space and learned low-dim action baselines, including sim-to-real deployments.Link
MobileVISTA: Generative Data Augmentation for Pose Generalization in Mobile ManipulationStanford UniversityPresents a data-generation framework that perturbs robot base poses and jointly augments egocentric visuals plus retargeted actions, targeting humanoids where cameras ride the kinematic chain. MobileVISTA-trained policies show markedly improved robustness to off-distribution robot poses on both simulation and a Galaxea R1 Pro, without extra demonstrations or learned generative models.Link
RACER: Residual-Adaptive Closed-Loop Estimation for Sampling-Based Planning in Wheeled-Quadruped RacingUC BerkeleyCombines MPPI trajectory optimization with a learned residual dynamics model and a low-level RL velocity tracker for high-speed wheeled quadruped racing. Introduces Low-Rank Residual Adaptation to pretrain in sim and adapt with low-rank constraints on limited real data, yielding better cornering performance and robustness to domain shifts in ground and joint properties.Link
Expressiveness, Equivalence, and Uncertainty in Velocity Obstacles and Closest Point of Approach MetricsUC BerkeleyFormally relates VO, TCPA, and DCPA collision metrics under perfect state and extends them to bounded-uncertainty, convex relative-state sets. Shows that separately computed CPA bounds break the joint conditions underlying VO membership, while uncertainty-aware set-valued VOs preserve a coherent characterization of collision-inducing velocities.Link
Monocular Navigation Relative to Unknown Spacecraft Using a Transformer-Aided Kalman FilterStanford UniversityFuses transformer-based monocular odometry (via SuperPoint/LightGlue features) with an MSCKF to estimate relative orbit elements, attitude, and angular velocity to previously unseen spacecraft. Through attitude maneuvers, the method recovers metric range and achieves low attitude and range errors on held-out targets from a large synthetic spacecraft dataset.Link
Context-Conditioned Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability for Adaptive Safety FilteringStanford UniversityLearns a single HJ reachability value function conditioned on geometry, friction, and disturbance scale, enabling reuse of one certificate across operating contexts. Deployed as a discrete-time CBF filter on a full-scale race car, it maintains lateral containment under varying grip and unseen track geometries with minimal intervention and small lap-time penalties.Link
Feeling Through the Load: Compliant Quadruped Locomotion under Payload InteractionsCarnegie Mellon UniversityDevelops a force-aware locomotion stack for quadrupeds carrying unrestrained payloads that humans may directly manipulate. A compliant load-carrying policy is combined with an estimator-in-the-loop learned force estimator, enabling the robot to maintain balance, yield to payload interactions, and interpret them as motion commands in both sim and real experiments.Link
VLA-ACL: Action-Consistent Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Vision-Language-Action ModelsETH ZurichIntroduces a lightweight token-pruning policy trained to keep VLA actions consistent with a full-context teacher, enabling aggressive visual token reduction without fine-tuning the base model. On LIBERO and real manipulation, it prunes up to 87.5% of visual tokens, cuts compute by up to 75%, and yields 1.5× speedups while preserving task success.Link
Reactive Task-Oriented Robot-Human Handovers via Generative Hypothesis SelectionETH ZurichUses VLM-based image generation to synthesize diverse task-specific human grasp hypotheses for objects, then matches them online to the observed human hand pose to infer suitable robot handover configurations. The method generalizes to unseen object–task pairs and, in user studies, is perceived as understanding tasks better than prior affordance-only approaches.Link
The Robot Is Not Its Description: GaugeBench for Representation Robustness in Morphology-Aware PoliciesUC San DiegoIntroduces GaugeBench to test policy robustness to changes in kinematic description (axis sign, joint order, angle zeros) while keeping the physical robot fixed. Shows that such representation changes can degrade performance more than changing to a genuinely new morphology, and that training across equivalent descriptions restores robustness.Link
Dynamics Modeling of a Multi-UAV Slung Load System Using a Discrete-Link Cable ApproachMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyProposes an Euler–Newton dynamics model for multi-UAV slung-load systems that discretizes flexible cables as rigid-link chains amenable to Featherstone’s articulated-body algorithm. Real-flight validation across maneuver types demonstrates sub-6% load-translation error relative to cable length and provides an open dataset for future cable-model development.Link
Toward Trustworthy Physical AI for Human InteractionStanford UniversityArticulates a conceptual framework for trustworthy physical AI that integrates safety, behavioral intelligibility, and perceptual alignment across embodiment, control, cognition, and design. Argues that human trust emerges from consistent alignment between physical capabilities, observable behavior, and user expectations in human–robot interaction.Link
Lego-Like Stiffness Configuration of Planar Compliant Modules for Task-Specific Flexible InterfacesStanford UniversityPresents stackable planar compliant modules with complementary geometries and tunable parameters to realize task-specific stiffness profiles. A unified stiffness model plus two-stage optimization (GA + SQP) supports designing flexible interfaces such as a compliant wrist, validated experimentally with <6.5% stiffness error and safe 1 m/s contact-rich motion.Link
Fluorescence-enhanced Whisker Array with Vision-based Deformation Analysis for Underwater Source LocalizationStanford UniversityIntroduces a UV-excited fluorescent whisker array whose deflections are imaged and processed with a CNN to localize underwater flow sources without directly imaging them. Pool experiments achieve ~88 mm mean localization error over a 600 mm, ±30° region and real-time tracking of moving thrusters, suggesting utility for low-light underwater robots.Link
Behavioral Cloning MysteryUC BerkeleyIntroduces OCBench, a GPU-accelerated manipulation benchmark with scripted policies that mimic key properties of human demonstrations, allowing controlled study of puzzling BC behaviors. Reproduces phenomena like overfitting-driven performance gains and the importance of action chunking, and uses the benchmark to test and refute candidate explanations.Link
Physical Twins: Accelerating and Enabling Robot Learning with Phantom PlatformsUC San DiegoDescribes an improved phantom upper-limb device that mimics ball-and-socket plus scapular and protraction motions to stand in for humans in physically interactive experiments. Demonstrates a Franka arm performing ADL-like and pHRI tasks with the device, enabling repeatable, safe RL-style testing before moving to real human subjects.Link
VeriFine: Scaling Verification for Self-Improvement in Embodied ReasoningStanford University; UC BerkeleyProposes VeriFine, a dual-loop framework where both policy and judge co-evolve: a rubric-based judge guides curriculum and optimization, and when progress stalls, human-in-the-loop refinement improves the judge itself. Experiments on driving and navigation show that adaptive verification enables continued performance gains as failure modes shift.Link

NMPP: Nonlinear Model Predictive Planning for Agile UAV Flight in Cluttered EnvironmentsPresents a nonlinear model predictive planner that treats obstacles as hard constraints and outputs full-state references for an SE(3) controller, enabling agile quadrotor flight in clutter. Compared to linear MPC and polynomial corridor planners, it cuts position RMSE by ~40–70% and achieves markedly higher success rates in dense “forest” environments and real-world flights up to 5.5 m/s.Link
RIWANav: Recursive World-Action Models with Self-Improvement for Urban NavigationIntroduces a recursive self-improvement loop between a world-action model and navigation policy for urban driving, using imagined visual rollouts for policy optimization and policy-driven self-curricula for model refinement. Demonstrates improved long-horizon navigation performance over imitation and prior world-model baselines, with real-world trials confirming applicability.Link
Pareto-Optimal Entropy-Regularized Trajectory OptimizationGeorgia Institute of TechnologyProposes PER-DDP, an entropy-regularized DDP framework that maintains a population of trajectories, performs prior-guided sampling, and uses Pareto filtering to preserve diverse, constraint-satisfying candidates. Across many systems and environments, it achieves higher success rates and solves instances that defeat other sampling-augmented TO methods, improving robustness in nonconvex motion planning.Link
SC3BF: Shifted Collision Cone Control Barrier Function for Dynamic Obstacle AvoidanceIntroduces the shifted collision-cone CBF, which relaxes classical collision-cone conservatism by allowing distance- and speed-dependent approach rates while maintaining forward invariance. Implemented via a QP for a kinematic bicycle among up to 100 moving obstacles, SC3BF increases goal reachability and reduces deviation from nominal inputs compared to three velocity-space baselines.Link
Sensor-Layout-Agnostic Navigation via Geometric Observation CanonicalizationCanonicalizes depth from arbitrary multi-camera layouts into a unified spherical range image plus validity mask, letting a single RL policy generalize across sensor configurations on a quadrotor. Policies trained with aggressive camera randomization achieve high zero-shot success on unseen layouts and transfer to real-world flights, remaining robust to sudden sensor dropouts.Link
VOMMI: Collecting and Leveraging Portable Demonstrations for Mobile ManipulationHong Kong University of Science and TechnologyProposes a portable RGB-only demo interface with synchronized body/hand views and an R2-VO pipeline that reconstructs trajectories and outputs causal motion tokens to condition a VLA’s base branch. Policies post-trained solely on these portable demos outperform robot-demo baselines in base-velocity error while matching end-effector accuracy, and deliver higher success on real mobile-manipulation tasks.Link
iGPC: Generative Motion Priors for Object-Aware Humanoid InteractionMohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial IntelligenceExtends a generative pretrained human-motion controller into interaction experts conditioned on affordances and privileged state, then distills them into a perception-driven humanoid policy. The resulting whole-body controller leverages large-scale motion priors to execute reaching, support-grasping, and object-pushing tasks in contact-rich scenes.Link
Reactive Exploration of Unknown Environments for Redundant Robots using Virtual Model ControlUniversity of BolognaCombines voxel-level information-gain scoring with Virtual Model Control to drive eye-in-hand redundant arms through confined unknown spaces without explicit path replanning. Both sim and real-robot tests reach >90% mapping coverage within 120 s while avoiding collisions, showing effective whole-body exploration in constrained environments.Link
Navigation with RF Cues: Embodied Perception Action under Multipath UncertaintyHong Kong University of Science and Technology; University of Science and Technology of ChinaBuilds a Habitat–Sionna RT benchmark that co-simulates RF and visual observations and proposes a multimodal policy that estimates target direction and uncertainty from RF, vision, and history. On unseen scenes, the approach boosts success rate and SPL over vision- or RF-only baselines, highlighting RF’s utility for inspection under occlusion and multipath.Link
OntoPlan: An Ontology-Grounded Scene Representation and Agentic Framework for Scalable Robot Task PlanningYonsei UniversityIntroduces an ontology-aligned scene representation for objects, spaces, and relations plus an agentic LLM framework that retrieves only task-relevant facts and composes symbolic plans. Across 150 tasks over multiple indoor scales, OntoPlan attains ~0.89 task success with ~5.6× fewer tokens than the best baseline and more robust behavior as environment size grows.Link
