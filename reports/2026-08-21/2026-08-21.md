## Executive Summary

- Dexterous and deformable-object manipulation see multiple new benchmarks (LabDex, SoftVTBench) and training pipelines (ADEPT, RoboEdit, Dream2Reward) targeting long-horizon, high-DoF control and rich visuo-tactile feedback.
- Navigation and motion planning advances span depth-guided collision-aware indoor navigation, autonomous agricultural tractors, and grounded LLM-based or world-model-based trajectory planning for driving.
- Whole-body and underactuated control methods progress via a behavior world model for humanoids and a control-constrained DDP MPC framework enabling underactuated quadruped balancing and dynamic gaits.
- Task and layout planning papers address concurrent navigation–fetch coordination for assistive robot dogs and backward layout search for sequence-constrained assembly with explicit motion feasibility checks.
- Institution-matched work from UC Berkeley and UC San Diego explores LLM-based agentic solutions for contact-rich manipulation and task-agnostic control strategies for soft exosuits.
