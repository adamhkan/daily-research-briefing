## Executive Summary

- Strong motion-planning and control advances appear across domains: constrained optimal control (MAGMA, LEAP, A-SLS), safe multi-agent planning under legal and communication constraints, and robust delay-aware MPPI for manipulators.
- Mobile manipulation and long-horizon control see new agentic frameworks and memory mechanisms (MoCaP, Mem4Act, LTM, VaM), plus neuromorphic and topological navigation systems for resource-limited or unmapped settings.
- Dexterous and whole-body manipulation capabilities are pushed forward via large-scale tactile pretraining, supervised play, whole-body grasping on humanoids, contact-aware impedance control, and new generalization benchmarks.
- Navigation safety and certification are emphasized from micro- to macro-scales: monocular collision cones, BubbleCBF for second-order systems, human-aware UAV navigation with onboard LLMs, and bio-inspired/ergodic search for aerial and marine robots.
- Vision-language(-action) model tooling for robotics matures rapidly, including efficient token compression and one-shot task-context inference, causal/mechanistic analyses, verification and scheduling of slow modules, and instruction-following diagnostics.
- New benchmarks and datasets (LIBERO-PeRM, Evo-DexBench, MuSTMM, CRIA, SparkArena, LIN-SLAM, OccEVT-Bench) target personalization, sound-triggered mobile manipulation, anomaly anticipation, tactile pretraining, and robust SLAM.
