Executive Summary

  • Strong motion-planning and control advances appear across domains: constrained optimal control (MAGMA, LEAP, A-SLS), safe multi-agent planning under legal and communication constraints, and robust delay-aware MPPI for manipulators.
  • Mobile manipulation and long-horizon control see new agentic frameworks and memory mechanisms (MoCaP, Mem4Act, LTM, VaM), plus neuromorphic and topological navigation systems for resource-limited or unmapped settings.
  • Dexterous and whole-body manipulation capabilities are pushed forward via large-scale tactile pretraining, supervised play, whole-body grasping on humanoids, contact-aware impedance control, and new generalization benchmarks.
  • Navigation safety and certification are emphasized from micro- to macro-scales: monocular collision cones, BubbleCBF for second-order systems, human-aware UAV navigation with onboard LLMs, and bio-inspired/ergodic search for aerial and marine robots.
  • Vision-language(-action) model tooling for robotics matures rapidly, including efficient token compression and one-shot task-context inference, causal/mechanistic analyses, verification and scheduling of slow modules, and instruction-following diagnostics.
  • New benchmarks and datasets (LIBERO-PeRM, Evo-DexBench, MuSTMM, CRIA, SparkArena, LIN-SLAM, OccEVT-Bench) target personalization, sound-triggered mobile manipulation, anomaly anticipation, tactile pretraining, and robust SLAM.

Safe and Fuel-Efficient Motion Planning for Lunar Propulsive DronesStanford UniversityPresents a vision-guided optimal-control framework for hopping drones on the Moon that plans parabolic hop trajectories between waypoints while checking landing safety via semantic segmentation and reachable-terrain analysis. A diversion maneuver and MPC tracking ensure safe, fuel-efficient hops in a high-fidelity lunar simulator.Link
Whole-Body DexterityUC San DiegoIntroduces a framework for humanoids to use the entire body as a manipulation surface, generating whole-body grasps in simulation and training RL experts on any-to-any object repositioning. A distilled policy transfers zero-shot to a Unitree G1, achieving 90% success on complex box and ball repositioning tasks, far outperforming baselines.Link
CLIS-MPPI: Closed-Loop Model Predictive Path Integral Control With Initial-State Sampling Under Execution DelayUniversity of WashingtonAddresses execution delay in MPPI by sampling a distribution over plausible execution-time initial states and adding rollout-based local feedback. On a UR5e performing a narrow-passage task, CLIS-MPPI eliminates collisions seen with standard MPPI and improves tracking smoothness and efficiency.Link
Manifold Generative Annealing for Contact Constraint-aware Impedance Control with Reinforcement Learning PriorCarnegie Mellon UniversityProposes MGA, which organizes pretrained RL policies into structured proposal sequences and refines them via model-based rollouts on a contact-aware manifold. The method improves safety and success for contact-rich tasks like surface scanning, peg insertion, and humanoid pushing under changing contact conditions.Link
MoCaP: Mobile Code-as-Policies for Zero-Shot Mobile Manipulation from Passive Human VideosNew York UniversityIntroduces an agentic pipeline that extracts spatial memory and approach priors from passive human videos to guide a code-as-policy mobile manipulation controller. On a real mobile manipulator, MoCaP achieves 80% success on two indoor tasks and substantially boosts navigation and base-pose finding versus baselines.Link
Perceive, Predict, and Plan: a Unified World Action Model for Egocentric Dexterous ManipulationETH ZurichPresents P³-WAM, a unified video-diffusion-based model that jointly handles 3D perception, action-conditioned future prediction, and goal-directed planning for egocentric hand-object manipulation. Unified training across these modes improves 3D hand reconstruction, video prediction, and instruction-following manipulation over specialized baselines.Link
Auditable and Deployable Multi-Agent Motion Planning via Posetal Games from Legal GuidelinesMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyFormulates multi-agent motion planning under partially ordered legal guidelines as posetal games, allowing agents to reconcile private objectives with shared legal priorities. Belief play and a distributed best-response scheme recover equilibria without sharing private preferences, demonstrated on multi-vessel maritime navigation scenarios.Link
Illumination-Aware Active Perception for Spacecraft InspectionNew York UniversityReplaces line-of-sight visibility metrics with a learned information measure from a classifier VAE to plan spacecraft inspection viewpoints under challenging orbital lighting. In simulation, the planner obtains significantly better-illuminated observations than geometric baselines while maximizing its own objective.Link
Asking Smarter Questions: Targeted Active Preference Learning for Underspecified RewardsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyShows how robots can detect underspecified reward features from initial teaching data and then target preference queries toward them using an informed prior plus explanations. Simulation and user studies demonstrate up to 30.8% improved reward recovery in low-query regimes over standard active preference learning.Link

Spark-0: Scaling Spatially Aligned Tactile Pretraining for Robot Dexterous ManipulationIntroduces Spark-0, a large VTLA model that uses 1,000 hours of full-hand tactile human data to supervise visual tokens and a tactile pathway for contact-rich dexterous manipulation. Dexterity from Supervised PlayUniversity of Maryland, College ParkDSP trains a short human-play behavior-cloned policy to autonomously collect hours of contact-rich data, then uses a world model for MPC planning and self-play policy distillation. On multifingered hands, it achieves large success gains over hindsight BC and matches RL with orders-of-magnitude less interaction data.Link
Evo-DexBench: Benchmarking Generalization across Related Tasks for Dexterous ManipulationShanghai Jiao Tong UniversityDefines the Core-Gen principle and releases Evo-DexBench, 30 dexterous tasks grouped into six functional families with 1,248 multimodal teleoperated demos. Existing methods achieve only 38.5% average success under few-shot Gen tasks, highlighting the difficulty of functional generalization even when source tasks are related.Link
Mem4Act: Learning Effective Latent Memory for Long-Horizon Robotic ManipulationMem4Act learns a fixed-size latent memory optimized for long-horizon manipulation, combining multi-scale temporal aggregation, QA-style semantic supervision, and visual key-frame reconstruction. It attains state-of-the-art success on memory-dependent benchmarks and real tasks, with ablations validating each component.Link
TS-WAM: Temporal Latent Actions and World-Action Coupling for Vision-Language Robot ControlTS-WAM couples a VLM-JEPA backbone with delta latent world modeling to jointly learn latent actions, visual dynamics, and control from both robot and action-free human videos. The EMA teacher is only used during training, yielding a lightweight deployed policy that shows strong manipulation performance and robustness in sim and on Franka/RevoTron.Link
MOSS: Mode-Oriented Source Shaping for Robot Flow Matching PoliciesNanyang Technological University; Tsinghua UniversityMOSS exploits the mode-dependent structure of deterministic flow trajectories by constructing mode-specific Gaussian sources in the noise space, enabling explicit behavior selection at inference. Across simulated and real manipulation tasks, it achieves near-perfect guidance to desired modes and recovers minority behaviors under severe dataset imbalance.Link
MAIA: Anticipating Interaction Anomalies in Contact-Rich Manipulation via Multimodal World ModelsShanghai Jiao Tong UniversityMAIA trains a multimodal vision-action-force world model with an anomaly head that predicts impending contact anomalies before they manifest. On the CRIA dataset and real tasks, it achieves earlier and more accurate anomaly detection than baselines and raises manipulation success by using predictions for anomaly-aware action selection.Link
CQDC-Warp: GPU-Accelerated Differentiable Quasi-Dynamic Simulation for Contact-Rich ManipulationPeking University; Tsinghua UniversityCQDC-Warp is a differentiable quasi-dynamic simulator on GPU tailored for batched MPC in contact-rich manipulation, encouraging intended contacts via soft costs without fixed contact schedules. It substantially improves success and reduces trajectory-generation time over prior planners on Allegro in-hand rotation and scales to multi-hand and whole-body cases.Link
