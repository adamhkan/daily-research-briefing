Executive Summary

  • Language- and vision-language-action models continue to dominate manipulation research, with new work on explicit reasoning (R^3, LM-X), retrieval-based test-time adaptation (RA-VLA), and geometry/world modeling (GaussVLA, GaussianDream++).
  • Multiple methods tackle contact-rich and dexterous manipulation via fast grasp generation, visuo-physical deformation feedback, tactile-conditioned streaming control, and new dexterous hand hardware.
  • Planning and control advances span anytime tensor motion planning, bimanual MPC with projection, convex-decomposition-based packing, and compliant whole-body humanoid control (LAC).
  • Navigation and exploration papers address challenging settings from indoor image-goal navigation and social navigation with explicit memory to orchards, multi-floor buildings, and safe multirotor flight.
  • New datasets, simulators, and toolkits (CRESSim-Neo, VirTooS, ConfAL-WM-style world model adaptation) support large-scale learning and evaluation for both manipulation and mobile platforms.

$R^3$: Training Robots to Reason in Natural Language via Reinforcement LearningCarnegie Mellon UniversityR^3 post-trains off-the-shelf vision-language models into robotic "reasoners" that produce free-form language traces to guide low-level manipulation policies. Mid-training on expert reasoning traces plus rubric-based RL yields better exploration and generalization on long-horizon tasks like Language Table and bimanual grocery packing than instruction-only imitation learning.Link

Anytime Global Tensor Motion PlanningThis work generalizes Global Tensor Motion Planning to accept arbitrary local planners and introduces two anytime variants: a random-restart GTMP that probabilistically covers all homotopy classes and an asymptotically optimal informed expansion scheme. On manipulation benchmarks it matches state-of-the-art performance and on 2D navigation produces batches of topologically diverse solutions rather than concentrating on a few classes.Link
PRISM: Projection-Integrated Sampling-Based MPC with Bayesian Cost Tuning for Bimanual ManipulationPRISM is a GPU-accelerated sampling-based MPC framework for dual-arm manipulation that projects sampled joint-velocity trajectories into a QP-defined feasible set before physics rollouts. A custom ADMM/Bregman QP solver and Bayesian cost tuning enable real-time, robust execution on contact-rich bimanual tasks such as obstacle-constrained transport and handovers, with successful sim-to-real transfer on dual UR5e arms.Link
Leveraging Inter-object Affordances for Efficient Planning in Contact-rich TasksThe paper augments a Unified Task-and-Motion Planning (U-TAMP) framework with VLM-derived abstractions of inter-object affordances, such as grasp and support relations, to encode physical interaction constraints. In simulated kitchen organization scenarios, the enriched domain significantly boosts planning success and reduces planning time by one to two orders of magnitude compared to standard U-TAMP and other VLM-based planners.Link
LAC: Linear and Angular Compliance for Humanoid Whole-body ControlLAC learns a whole-body controller that realizes commanded linear and angular compliance to external wrenches on a humanoid’s upper body. Using large-scale data synthesis and teacher–student reinforcement learning, the policy produces tunable compliant responses over the full stiffness range and supports teleoperated loco-manipulation tasks in both simulation and hardware.Link
Fast Generative Grasping via Lie Group-Constrained MeanFlowThe authors propose a MeanFlow-based generative model on SO(3)×R^3 for rapid sampling of grasp poses, combining algebraic consistency with Riemannian conditional flow matching. On ACRONYM, it matches diffusion/flow grasp quality while requiring ≤5 network evaluations (up to 39× faster), and transfers directly to real-world grasping under sensor noise without extra training.Link
VISTA: Visually Inferred Spatial ConTact Attention for Contact-Rich ManipulationVISTA-Policy uses a 3D Visual Deformation Field of a compliant gripper as high-dimensional visuo-physical feedback for imitation learning in contact-rich tasks. With physics-aware encoding, denoising, and deformation-augmented policy networks, it outperforms strong vision-only and tactile baselines on tasks like cap unscrewing and calligraphy, and generalizes to unseen object scales and disturbances.Link
TacForcing: Streaming Action Generation with Execution-Time Tactile FeedbackTacForcing replaces chunked action prediction with streaming action generation conditioned on tactile signals observed during execution, avoiding stale feedback. Its Execution-Aware Tactile Attention focuses conditioning on imminent actions, yielding 65% and 69% average success in simulated and real contact-rich tasks, surpassing existing tactile-reactive and VLA baselines.Link
MA-VLA: Multi-Arm Vision-Language-Action Model for Collaboration and Compositional GeneralizationMA-VLA decomposes multi-arm collaboration into arm-specific atomic action prompts and introduces an Arm Shuffle scheme to train role-agnostic coordination. It generalizes to unseen multi-arm collaboration patterns where prior VLAs fail, demonstrating that structured per-arm instruction assignment enables compositional generalization in multi-arm manipulation.Link
LM-X: Explainable Action Modeling with Progress, Event, and Uncertainty Prediction for Generalist Robot ManipulationLM-X augments VLA policies with three explicitly supervised heads—return-to-go, event-to-go, and heteroscedastic action variance—that both condition and explain action generation. Trained on over 20,000 hours of robot data, it substantially outperforms GR00T N1.7 on RoboTwin2.0 and real-robot tasks, while its signals provide interpretable views of task progress and control reliability.Link
GaussianDream++: Efficient 3D Gaussian World Modeling for Robotic ManipulationChinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of SciencesGaussianDream++ injects compact current and future 3D Gaussian world modeling into a VLA backbone via world state and prediction tokens and a training-only reconstruction head. At inference it retains only lightweight tokens, yet achieves strong LIBERO and LIBERO-Plus performance and significantly improves real-robot success and robustness to camera/layout shifts without online Gaussian rendering.Link
