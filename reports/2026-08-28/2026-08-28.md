## Executive Summary

- Language- and vision-language-action models continue to dominate manipulation research, with new work on explicit reasoning (R^3, LM-X), retrieval-based test-time adaptation (RA-VLA), and geometry/world modeling (GaussVLA, GaussianDream++).
- Multiple methods tackle contact-rich and dexterous manipulation via fast grasp generation, visuo-physical deformation feedback, tactile-conditioned streaming control, and new dexterous hand hardware.
- Planning and control advances span anytime tensor motion planning, bimanual MPC with projection, convex-decomposition-based packing, and compliant whole-body humanoid control (LAC).
- Navigation and exploration papers address challenging settings from indoor image-goal navigation and social navigation with explicit memory to orchards, multi-floor buildings, and safe multirotor flight.
- New datasets, simulators, and toolkits (CRESSim-Neo, VirTooS, ConfAL-WM-style world model adaptation) support large-scale learning and evaluation for both manipulation and mobile platforms.
