## Executive Summary

- Foundation-model-style VLAs and world-action models continue to advance manipulation, memory, and safety, with several works targeting long-horizon, failure-robust control.
- Humanoid and legged loco-manipulation are a major theme, including test-time reward evolution, whole-body safety/coordination, and reusable motion capabilities.
- Navigation and motion planning see progress in uncertainty-aware belief-space planning, glass-aware mapping, LiDAR BEV perception, multi-robot exploration, and execution-interface adaptation.
- Dexterous and continuum manipulation benefit from better retargeting, Jacobian modeling, and data-efficient trajectory-guided RL, plus new teleoperation and co-training pipelines.
- Task-and-motion planning and high-level reasoning are increasingly coupled to LLMs, with applications from spacecraft rendezvous to legible plans and stable construction sequences.
- Selected CMU, Berkeley, ETH, MIT, Stanford, and Washington–adjacent work emphasizes bridging sim-to-real gaps, long-horizon memory, and unified SE(3) representations for motion.

| Reconstruct, Practice, Go Real: Guided Self-Improvement for Embodied Agents | UC Berkeley | RPG is a practice-in-simulation framework that improves a robot’s manipulation stack without updating network weights. It mines skills from offline data, constructs simulated practice tasks, diagnoses failures using privileged state and videos, then revises symbolic skills and prompts; on 22 manipulation tasks, success rises from 28.6% to 95.0%, and the frozen system transfers to 30/30 successes on real hardware. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02204 |
| GlassGuard: Verified Glass Plane Mapping for Robot Navigation | Carnegie Mellon University | GlassGuard fuses LiDAR and vision to explicitly reconstruct planar glass surfaces that standard LiDAR SLAM misses, emphasizing both high glass coverage and low free-space contamination. On 2.1 km of real-robot traversal, it achieves up to 82–85% glass coverage while producing 5–17× fewer false voxels than baselines, enabling planners to correctly treat glass as collision boundaries. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02110 |
| ECoMEM: Explicit Concept Memory for Memory-Dependent Robot Control | Stanford University | ECoMEM separates ‘what to remember’ from ‘how to act’ by maintaining an explicit library of grounded task concepts updated by an evidence-based Writer and consumed by a Reader that conditions a vision-language-action policy. On 16 RoboMME tasks, it wins on 15 and boosts real-robot memory-dependent task success from 8.6% (no memory) to 86.1% with reusable concept memories. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00801 |
| World Motion Models: Flexible Sequence Modeling of SE(3) Trajectories | UC Berkeley | World Motion Models treat dynamic scenes as sets of rigid SE(3) trajectories and learn a unified flow-matching model over these pose sequences. A context-token mechanism supports any-to-any conditioning, enabling future prediction, motion infilling, MPC, inverse kinematics, cross-embodiment retargeting, and policy learning within a single architecture across diverse 3D vision and robotics tasks. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01742 |
| Continual Learning for 6-DoF Grasp Synthesis via Experience and Demonstrations | ETH Zurich | This work introduces a continual-learning 6-DoF grasping framework that adapts via a non-parametric memory in embedding space rather than by finetuning the network. Grasp outcomes reweight future candidates and user demonstrations are stored and transferred across scenes, enabling over 90% success on challenging object categories after ~50 online trials while limiting catastrophic forgetting. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01301 |
| OrbitTAMP: Grounding Language Models for Task and Motion Planning in Spacecraft Rendezvous | Stanford University | OrbitTAMP grounds LLM-based mission specification for spacecraft rendezvous in a library of reusable behaviors plus domain planners and trajectory optimization. The LLM maps natural-language intent to partial specs, while structured planners fill in admissible details, yielding 98% exact recovery of partial mission specifications and much higher reliability than direct free-form LLM planning. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01093 |
| When Reasoning Helps Action: Monitoring and Steering Chain-of-Thought in Vision-Language-Action Policies | Stanford University | The authors define correctability and actionability for chain-of-thought (CoT) in VLA policies and propose TRUST, a value model that predicts reasoning correctness to steer CoT at inference. On a driving VLA, TRUST improves reasoning correctness to 90% and reduces collisions and trajectory error, while on a manipulation VLA it fixes reasoning without clear task gains, highlighting that better CoT does not always improve embodied performance. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00601 |
| ScaffoldM3C: A Multimodal Sequential Monte Carlo Framework for Generative Stable Construction Planning | Stanford University | ScaffoldM3C frames block construction as probabilistic next-block generation conditioned on multimodal prompts (text, sketches, images) and explicitly models scaffold blocks for intermediate stability. A lightweight autoregressive model proposes candidates and SMC maintains multiple assembly hypotheses, achieving comparable or better structural stability than larger baselines with 4× fewer parameters and 5–20× faster inference. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00487 |
| CLoSeR: Closing the Loop for Long-Context Streaming Reconstruction | ETH Zurich | CLoSeR augments feedforward 3D reconstruction foundation models with loop-closure by detecting loop candidates, estimating relative poses, and optimizing frame poses on SE(3) under sequential and loop constraints. Exploiting globally consistent scale from the backbone, it achieves markedly reduced drift and consistent kilometer-scale reconstructions, outperforming prior long-range mapping methods. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01927 |
| Extreme Length Generalization in a Compact Recurrent Architecture for One-Shot Exploration | UC Berkeley | FRANK is a 507k-parameter recurrent architecture combining gated recurrence, content-addressable memory, and a reflex pathway, trained on short sequences yet generalizing to sequences 100,000× longer with perfect accuracy in most seeds. A sim-trained FRANK policy also controls a physical ground vehicle to waypoints through obstacles, illustrating its potential for long-horizon onboard policies under tight compute budgets. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01105 |
