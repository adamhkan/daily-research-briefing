Executive Summary

  • University-linked work spans mega-scale RL for control, microscopic-language-to-motion mapping, execution safety gates, cross-embodiment world models, and high-dimensional safe RL.
  • Mega-scale parallel RL with adaptive sampling (SGS) enables previously unsolved quadruped locomotion and contact-rich manipulation, with sim-to-real transfer via RGB policies.
  • Microscale task-conditioned trajectory integration from language substantially improves geometric accuracy and coverage for microscopic robots with minimal new labels.
  • Safety and reliability at the control interface are addressed through execution gates (PIER) and feasibility-aware safety filtering (FAITH) that separate task performance from safety constraints.
  • Cross-embodiment latent action spaces (LAC-WM) and world models unify control priors across heterogeneous robots, improving adaptation to unseen embodiments without action-space redesign.

A Balanced Data Diet: Addressing the Exploration Bottleneck in Mega-Scale RL for Robot ControlUniversity of WashingtonIntroduces Success Guided Sampling (SGS), an adaptive reset-sampling strategy that concentrates mega-scale parallel RL on task configurations at the frontier of current capability. This drastically improves exploration efficiency, enabling solutions to challenging multi-terrain quadruped locomotion and contact-rich assembly tasks, with RGB policy distillation for zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on real assembly hardware.Link
From Language to Motion: Task-Conditioned Focal-Stack Trajectory Integration for Microscopic RobotsCarnegie Mellon UniversityMaps natural-language instructions for microscopic robots into geometric operators and integrates multiple focal-plane trajectories via confidence-weighted dynamic programming. The method cuts trajectory RMSE by over 50% versus image-first fusion and improves physical coverage in dispensing experiments while requiring no new part-specific labels beyond an existing open-vocabulary perception stack.Link
PIER: An Evidence-Gated Execution Interface for Robotic ManipulationUniversity of WashingtonDefines PIER, a deterministic execution-authorization gate that separates evidence checks and provenance tracking from low-level hardware control in manipulation tasks. Through synthetic traces it characterizes how re-observation budgets and thresholding affect rates of valid denials and unsafe proceeds, exposing contracts and limitations of evidence-based gating without claiming superiority over calibrated rule logic.Link
FAITH: Feasibility-Aware Safety-Filtered RL for High-Dimensional SystemsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyProposes FAITH, a model-free safety filter that learns a state–action safety value function and amortizes minimal-intervention filtering via a neural network, removing safety terms from the task-policy gradient. It achieves high safety rates with near-unfiltered return on a 29-DoF humanoid and handles infeasible states by steering toward actions with minimum predicted peak harm, with policies validated on a real Unitree G1.Link
Cross-Embodiment Robot Foundation World Models with Latent ActionsStanford UniversityIntroduces LAC-WM, a robot world model that operates in a unified latent action space shared across heterogeneous embodiments to support cross-robot generalization. Compared to explicit action conditioning, LAC-WM improves downstream performance by up to 46.7% on dexterous manipulation and scales positively with the number of pretraining embodiments instead of degrading.Link
Scalable LEO Conjunction Screening using Adaptive Synthetic-Covariance ThresholdsStanford UniversityPresents an uncertainty-aware conjunction-screening sieve for large LEO catalogs, using object-specific, time-varying altitude thresholds derived from propagated radial covariance plus a binary-search altitude sweep. On 33k tracked objects it reduces candidate pairs by ~29% over a fixed-threshold filter and >70% over brute-force all-pairs, while retaining all close approaches in reference sets.Link

Dex-One2Many: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from a Single Human DemonstrationSeoul National University; University of Maryland, College ParkProposes a real-to-sim-to-real pipeline that converts a single human video into sequential scene graphs used to guide RL for dexterous multi-stage tasks. The relation-based graphs generate diverse reset states and dense rewards, enabling broad generalization to unseen poses and grasps and zero-shot transfer to a real multi-fingered hand with large gains in unseen configurations.Link
Generative Neural Retargeting for Human-to-Robot Dexterous ManipulationIntroduces Generative Neural Retargeting, a flow-matching model that samples dynamically feasible robot trajectories conditioned on human demonstrations, instead of solving per-trajectory MPC or RL optimizations. It attains higher success than MPC with under 10% of its samples and scales to a 223k-demo dexterous dataset with dense contact-force labels.Link
SkillWeave: Weaving Heterogeneous Demonstrations into Long-Horizon Manipulation SkillsCombines teleoperation for coarse motion with kinesthetic teaching for contact-rich segments to build long-horizon dexterous skills. An object-mask-conditioned diffusion policy and successor-aware terminal steering address visual mismatch and distribution shift between sub-policies, yielding strong real-world performance on multi-stage dexterous tasks.Link
TACROSS: An Efficient and Low-Cost Scalable Human Touch System Across Heterogeneous Tactile Sensors for Dexterous Robot LearningThe University of Hong Kong; University of Hong Kong ffiliation:Presents a USD 10.86, 285-taxel human tactile glove plus canonicalizers and residual adapters that align heterogeneous human and robot tactile streams at the event level. A robot-grounded policy learning scheme uses robot demos for action supervision and human data for tactile representation learning, cutting demo collection time by 3.5× and hardware cost by ~96% on contact-rich tasks.Link
OmniDex: Scaling Dexterous Hand Grasping to Diverse Cluttered ScenesConstructs a large-scale dexterous grasping benchmark with 2.6M cluttered scenes and 0.4B scene-specific grasp labels via a seed-and-filter strategy that avoids expensive scene optimization. The OmniDex model tackles multimodal grasps and precision via Soft WTA learning and physics-driven ranking, achieving state-of-the-art grasp success and generalization without slow post-optimization.Link
LiteNWM: Efficient Latent World Models for Onboard Visual Navigation in the WildIntroduces LiteNWM, a latent navigation world model that jointly predicts future representations for multiple candidate trajectories and scores them for selection. It significantly reduces trajectory error and yields a 128× speedup over a prior NWM while more than doubling real-robot navigation success in unseen indoor and outdoor environments.Link
ManiUnit: A Manipulation Skill Dataset and Benchmark for Long-Horizon TasksRe-factors 50 BEHAVIOR-1K activities into 137k skill segments with explicit subtask labels, perturbation robustness tests, and local success criteria to diagnose long-horizon mobile manipulation. Skill policies trained on ManiUnit improve local success and, when coordinated with a planner, quadruple full-task success compared to monolithic task policies.Link
SimVLA: Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real VLA Learning for Mobile ManipulationYonsei UniversityDevelops SimVLA, a VLA policy trained entirely in simulation from composed atomic skills (SimAction) and rich spatial and subtask-level supervision (SimVQA, SimDeploy) without teleoperation. The resulting model transfers zero-shot to real mobile manipulation tasks such as restocking and cleaning and outperforms policies trained on 50 in-domain real demos.Link
UNITAS: A 3D-Native World Action Model for Embodied ManipulationChinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen; Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen ffiliation:; The Chinese University of Hong KongPresents UNITAS, a 3D-native world-action model that represents both human hands and robot grippers as 3D point trajectories and predicts action-conditioned 3D scene flow in a shared metric frame. It achieves large reductions in displacement error versus 3D baselines on RoboTwin and near-perfect success on LIBERO, with strong real-world manipulation performance.Link
A Physics-Informed Collision Learning Framework for Collaborative Robot Motion GenerationIntroduces PI-UDF, a unified differentiable distance predictor for body-to-body collision between articulated arms, combining analytical kinematics with learned link-geometry embeddings and boundary-focused training. Integrated into nonlinear MPC on dual Frankas, it supports fast, close-proximity collision-aware motion such as dynamic dual-arm swapping and evasion.Link
