Executive Summary

  • Planning efficiency: FLASH-CBF-RRT reportedly matches RRT-Connect's 100% success across 689 MotionBenchMaker problems while reducing median planning time by 3.82x. VQ-WM reports 100% success on physical Tower of Hanoi manipulation using only short-play data; evaluated foundation models succeeded only when training data contained the solution trace. (arXiv:2610.24149; arXiv:2610.25600)
  • Long-horizon manipulation: DreamCan reports 91.2% success on LIBERO-Long with a 0.1B-parameter low-level policy and demonstrates real-world bimanual folding and packing. Separately, a quadruped system reports three autonomous hardware solves of a giant Rubik's Cube, each taking about 30 steps over 8-9 minutes. (arXiv:2610.24981; arXiv:2610.24610)
  • Real-world adaptation and personalization: PIA reports reliable few-shot adaptation on a full-size autonomous race car with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction. ChairNav reports increasing mean distance between user interventions from 29.2 m to 57.4 m, with autonomous operation for 93.4% of total driving time on kilometer-scale urban sidewalk routes. (arXiv:2610.26757; arXiv:2610.24012)
  • Contact-rich manipulation remains challenging in the evaluated settings: a new benchmark reports that vision-only policies struggle with contact events and visual-tactile policies do not meaningfully improve performance on six tasks. ACTS reports qualitative human-to-robot contact transfer without fine-tuning, but less robust visual transfer, including predictions below frame copying on Motherboard. (arXiv:2610.24512; arXiv:2610.24285)
  • Warehouse policy search: the authors report 58.9% higher throughput than a calibrated proprietary solution in simulated warehouses. Their collision-freedom and completion guarantees apply to accepted, unchanged goals under stated assumptions, rather than constituting unconditional deployment guarantees. (arXiv:2610.25946)
  • Productivity and workforce implications require caution: a small humanoid pick-and-stack study reports execution times of 12.87 s for robot-specific motion versus 25.03 s for human-derived motion, but does not establish broader productivity gains. Separately, robotics-exposure estimates concentrate in physically intensive, lower-wage occupations; the authors characterize these as upper bounds on potential automation exposure, not readiness, cost, or adoption measures. (arXiv:2610.24312; arXiv:2610.24267)

FLASH-CBF-RRT: Fast Lipschitz-Adaptive Safe Horizon CBF Steering for Certified Motion PlanningUC San DiegoThe authors introduce FLASH-CBF-RRT, which accelerates CBF steering using clearance-based time horizons and adaptive step sizes. The authors report matching RRT-Connect's 100% success rate across 689 MotionBenchMaker problems while reducing median planning time by 3.82x; they also report being 3.88x faster than fixed-step CBF-RRT. On a 17-DoF mobile manipulator, the authors report 2.2x faster planning and 4.2x fewer evaluations.Link
Solving a Giant Rubik's Cube with a Quadruped RobotETH ZurichThe authors combine onboard RGB-D perception and planning with reinforcement-learned whole-body skills distilled into depth-and-proprioception policies. The authors report three autonomous hardware solves of a giant Rubik's Cube using a quadruped's feet, each taking about 30 steps over 8-9 minutes.Link
Action-Aligned Latent Planning for Versatile Humanoid Loco-ManipulationCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford UniversityThe authors introduce FIBER, using a frozen behavioral foundation model's executable latent space to unify planning across interaction skills. Across 128 variations per task in simulation, the authors report 42.0% mean success versus 11.6% for projected latent replay and 3.5% for kinematic planning. The authors report real-world sim-to-real transfer, skill chaining, and conditional steering with the unified planner.Link
Vector-Quantized World Model: Latent Feedback Motion Planning for Robot ManipulationNew York UniversityThe authors introduce VQ-WM, combining discrete latent search with continuous visual feedback control. On physical robot Tower of Hanoi manipulation, the authors report 100% success using only short-play data, while the evaluated foundation models succeed only when their training data contain the solution trace. The authors report outperforming the strongest world-model baselines by 13.3--85.3 percentage points on simulation benchmarks spanning manipulation and locomotion.Link
Long-Horizon Multi-Object Rearrangement via Distilled Tree Search over Continuous ActionsCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford UniversityThe authors introduce DISCO, which periodically distills physics-simulation MCTS plans into a policy-value model that can run without simulator-based search at test time. The authors report outperforming behavior cloning, reinforcement learning, and search-based baselines on simulated and real-world rearrangement tasks.Link
Policy Search in Code Space for Multi-Agent PathfindingMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThe authors investigate human-directed policy search in code space, with coding agents refining algorithms through simulation feedback and explicit correctness verification. On a challenging competition map, the authors report that a two-day search exceeds the stronger locally reproduced entry by 35.0% on the densest instance and generalizes to unseen map instances. In simulated warehouses, the authors report 58.9% higher throughput than a calibrated proprietary solution, and prove collision freedom and completion of accepted, unchanged goals under stated assumptions.Link
Simulation Priors for Rapid Adaptation in Model-Based RL for Autonomous RacingUC San DiegoThe authors introduce PIA, which pretrains a world model on heterogeneous simulated trajectories and adapts it through limited target-domain interaction and uncertainty-regularized planning. In autonomous-racing simulation, the authors report faster adaptation across track and surface shifts, lower lap times, and fewer failures than strong baselines. On a real full-size autonomous race car, the authors report reliable few-shot adaptation with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction.Link
DexOAK: Object-Aware Kinematic RetargetingETH ZurichThe authors introduce DexOAK, an object-aware kinematic retargeting method that learns from joint hand-object graph solutions and refines references with contact-related losses. On the Inspire hand, the authors report success increasing from 39.5% to 48.4% on OakInk-v2 and ADD-AUC increasing from 62.7% to 77.9% on ARCTIC. The authors report 46/56 successful open-loop hardware replays across five tasks.Link
CoopUQ: Robust Multi-Robot Social Navigation via Uncertainty-Aware Cooperative ForecastingNew York UniversityThe authors introduce CoopUQ, combining cross-robot occupancy forecasting with uncertainty-guided constrained multi-agent reinforcement learning. The authors report improved prediction and safety over representative baselines in Unity3D simulations covering in-distribution and OOD settings. The authors also report robustness across various scenes in real-robot experiments.Link
Ropedia W0: Scaling Loco-Manipulation World Models across Embodiments with Human ExperienceUC San DiegoThe authors introduce Ropedia W0, a cross-embodiment egocentric loco-manipulation world model with a unified representation of locomotion and manipulation. The authors report state-of-the-art performance on five cross-embodiment benchmarks and held-out human video, across zero-shot, few-shot, and full-data post-training settings. The authors report that distillation enables real-time, minute-long rollouts supporting live VR interaction.Link
GOFusion: Real-Time Gaussian-Occupancy Submap Fusion for Fisher-Informed Autonomous Robot NavigationETH ZurichThe authors present GOFusion, which anchors Gaussian primitives to local occupancy maps for navigation. The authors report better mapping quality than several baselines in evaluations on public datasets and real-world drone flights. The authors demonstrate autonomous drone exploration for map refinement using their Fisher-informed heuristic.Link
SWAP: Test-Time Steerable Humanoid Whole-Body Collision AvoidanceCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors propose a hierarchical diffusion-policy and reinforcement-learning controller that separates collision-aware motion proposals from balance control. The authors report reliable static- and dynamic-obstacle avoidance in simulation and on a real humanoid. The authors report that test-time steering improves targeted rewards by 34% on average while maintaining a high collision avoidance rate.Link

Dream What I Can: Sparse Visual Planning for Long-Horizon Robot ManipulationStanford UniversityThe authors introduce DreamCan, pairing a video-model planner with a visuomotor policy to generate sparse visual subgoals balancing reachability and task utility. The authors report outperforming language, dense-video, and uniform-subgoal planners, with 91.2% success on LIBERO-Long using a 0.1B-parameter low-level policy. The authors also report real-world bimanual box folding and packing with unseen object combinations.Link
SteerAct: Unlocking Action Models through Few-Shot Temporal SteeringMassachusetts Institute of Technology; Stanford UniversityThe authors introduce SteerAct, using temporal descriptors of sparse-feature dynamics for offline feature selection and online activation steering. The authors report approximately 10% improvement in task success relative to unsteered models using 24 outcome-labeled rollouts per task, across the listed model, benchmark, and real-world evaluations without action-model weight updates.Link

In evaluations using dynamic simulation and standard image-based benchmarks, the authors report 89.1% average success and over 16% relative improvement over the strongest competing method in highly cluttered scenes. The authors report 88.8% success when deploying CLARO on a robot arm across diverse cluttered scenes.Link
Vision as Proprioception: Estimating Humanoid Robot Pose from VideoETH ZurichThe authors introduce HumanoidPose to recover robot joint configurations from video by combining pretrained human-pose features with robot kinematics. ACTS: Learning Visuo-Tactile World Models from Human Physical InteractionMassachusetts Institute of Technology; Stanford University; UC Berkeley; University of WashingtonThe authors introduce ACTS, a visuo-tactile world model trained only on human interaction data that jointly predicts scene, wrist, and tactile observations. On held-out human data, the authors report scene-view prediction improvements of 0.7-2.0 dB PSNR over the stronger of two comparison models trained from scratch on React. The authors report qualitative contact transfer without fine-tuning in inspected robot rollouts, but less robust visual transfer, including predictions below frame copying on Motherboard.Link
MOMO: Human Motion from Moving SoundsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThe authors introduce MOMO, conditioning motion diffusion on semantic features and dynamic spatial cues from audio. The authors report that MOMO outperforms existing baselines in motion fidelity, audio-motion alignment, and physical plausibility.Link
VaDar: Implicit Goal Conditioning via Value DisaggregationCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors introduce VaDar, which trains a subgoal-independent policy using a linear combination of per-subgoal critic values. The authors report that VaDar is consistently competitive with, and frequently outperforms, natural baselines such as goal-conditioning, while succeeding on non-sequential goals and multiple objectives.Link

The authors report that robotics exposure concentrates in physically intensive work and is higher in occupations with lower wages and education requirements. The authors caution that these measures are an upper bound on potential automation exposure, not measures of readiness, cost, or adoption.Link
