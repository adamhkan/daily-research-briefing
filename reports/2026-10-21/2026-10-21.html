Executive Summary

  • Planning efficiency: FLASH-CBF-RRT reportedly matched RRT-Connect's 100% success across 689 MotionBenchMaker problems while reducing median planning time by 3.82x. DISCO reports that distilling simulation search into a policy-value model enables deployment without simulator-based search and outperforms evaluated baselines on simulated and real-world rearrangement tasks. (arXiv:2610.24149; arXiv:2610.25785)
  • Long-horizon manipulation: DreamCan reports 91.2% success on LIBERO-Long using a 0.1B-parameter low-level policy, plus real-world bimanual folding and packing demonstrations. PhysWord separately reports 99.6% on LIBERO-Long and 90.3% on UniVTAC, alongside gains on real-world manipulation tasks; these summaries do not establish a controlled head-to-head comparison. (arXiv:2610.24981; arXiv:2610.26854)
  • World-model results include important transfer limits: VQ-WM reports outperforming its strongest world-model baselines by 13.3--85.3 percentage points on simulation benchmarks. ACTS, trained only on human interactions, reports contact-consistent tactile predictions in inspected robot rollouts without fine-tuning, but mixed visual transfer, including worse-than-frame-copying predictions and hallucinated structure. (arXiv:2610.25600; arXiv:2610.24285)
  • Adaptation and deployment: PIA reports reliable few-shot adaptation on a full-size autonomous race car with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction. ChairNav reports increasing mean distance between user interventions from 29.2 m to 57.4 m after post-training, with autonomous operation for 93.4% of driving time on kilometer-scale urban sidewalk routes. (arXiv:2610.26757; arXiv:2610.24012)
  • Multi-robot coordination: Human-directed code-space policy search reports 58.9% higher throughput than a calibrated proprietary solution in simulated warehouses. Its collision-freedom and finite-time-completion proofs depend on stated assumptions and apply to accepted goals that are not withdrawn or replaced. CoopUQ separately reports improved prediction and safety in Unity3D evaluations and robustness in real-robot experiments. (arXiv:2610.25946; arXiv:2610.26852)
  • Humanoid control: A hierarchical diffusion-and-reinforcement-learning framework reports static- and dynamic-obstacle avoidance in simulation and on hardware; test-time steering improved targeted rewards by 34% on average while maintaining a high collision avoidance rate. DUET demonstrates one locomotion checkpoint with two vision-language-action models on hardware, while its no-falls result is reported in simulation. (arXiv:2610.25711; arXiv:2610.23872)

FLASH-CBF-RRT: Fast Lipschitz-Adaptive Safe Horizon CBF Steering for Certified Motion PlanningUC San DiegoThe authors introduce FLASH-CBF-RRT, accelerating CBF steering with a safe constant-velocity horizon computed from closed-form collision-clearance bounds. Across 689 MotionBenchMaker problems, the authors report matching RRT-Connect's 100% success rate while reducing median planning time by 3.82x and running 3.88x faster than fixed-step CBF-RRT. On a 17-DoF mobile manipulator, the authors report 2.2x faster planning and 4.2x fewer evaluations.Link
Action-Aligned Latent Planning for Versatile Humanoid Loco-ManipulationCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford UniversityThe authors propose FIBER, projecting task-specific policies into a frozen behavioral foundation model's executable latent space and training a unified perceptive planner offline. Across 128 variations per task in simulation, the authors report 42.0% mean success, compared with 11.6% for projected latent replay and 3.5% for kinematic planning. The authors report real-world demonstrations of sim-to-real transfer, skill chaining, and conditional steering with the unified planner.Link
Vector-Quantized World Model: Latent Feedback Motion Planning for Robot ManipulationNew York UniversityThe authors introduce VQ-WM, combining discrete latent-state search with continuous visual feedback control to correct execution deviations. For physical Tower of Hanoi manipulation, the authors report 100\% success using only short-play data, while the compared foundation models succeed only when their training data contain the solution trace. The authors report outperforming the strongest world-model baselines by 13.3--85.3 percentage points on simulation benchmarks spanning manipulation and locomotion.Link
Long-Horizon Multi-Object Rearrangement via Distilled Tree Search over Continuous ActionsCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford UniversityThe authors propose DISCO, using progressive-widening MCTS in physics simulation and periodically distilling its plans into a policy-value model. The authors report that the learned model can run without simulator-based search at test time and outperforms behavior cloning, reinforcement learning, and search baselines on simulated and real-world rearrangement tasks.Link
Policy Search in Code Space for Multi-Agent PathfindingMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThe authors investigate human-directed code-space policy search, in which coding agents refine algorithms using simulation feedback and correctness verification. On a challenging competition map, the authors report that a two-day search produced a policy exceeding the stronger locally reproduced entry by 35.0% on the densest instance and generalizing to unseen map instances. In simulated warehouses, the authors report 58.9% higher throughput than a calibrated proprietary solution; under stated assumptions, they also prove collision freedom and finite-time completion of accepted goals that are not withdrawn or replaced.Link
Simulation Priors for Rapid Adaptation in Model-Based RL for Autonomous RacingUC San DiegoThe authors propose PIA, pretraining a world model on heterogeneous simulated trajectories and adapting it through limited target-domain interaction with uncertainty-regularized planning. In autonomous racing simulation, the authors report faster adaptation across track and surface shifts, lower lap times, and fewer failures than strong baselines. On a real full-size autonomous race car, the authors report reliable few-shot adaptation with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction.Link
DexOAK: Object-Aware Kinematic RetargetingETH ZurichThe authors introduce DexOAK, using joint hand-object interaction geometry to seed a feed-forward retargeting network refined with contact, non-penetration, and self-collision losses. On the Inspire hand, the authors report success increasing from 39.5% to 48.4% on OakInk-v2 and ADD-AUC increasing from 62.7% to 77.9% on ARCTIC. On hardware, the authors report 46/56 successful open-loop replays across five tasks.Link
CoopUQ: Robust Multi-Robot Social Navigation via Uncertainty-Aware Cooperative ForecastingNew York UniversityThe authors propose CoopUQ, combining cross-robot occupancy forecasting with constrained multi-agent reinforcement learning that penalizes predicted occupancy and uncertainty. The authors report improved prediction and safety over representative baselines in Unity3D simulations covering in-distribution and OOD settings. The authors also report robustness across various scenes in real-robot experiments.Link
Ropedia W0: Scaling Loco-Manipulation World Models across Embodiments with Human ExperienceUC San DiegoThe authors introduce Ropedia W0, a cross-embodiment loco-manipulation world model using camera poses for locomotion and projected skeletons for manipulation. The authors report state-of-the-art performance across five named cross-embodiment benchmarks and held-out human video, spanning zero-shot inference, few-shot adaptation, and full-data post-training. After distillation, the authors report real-time, minute-long rollouts supporting live VR interaction.Link
GOFusion: Real-Time Gaussian-Occupancy Submap Fusion for Fisher-Informed Autonomous Robot NavigationETH ZurichThe authors introduce GOFusion, anchoring Gaussian primitives to local occupancy maps and using an online Fisher approximation to guide pruning and next-best-view planning. On public datasets and real-world drone flights, the authors report better mapping quality than several baselines and real-time, uncertainty-aware mapping at scale. The authors demonstrate autonomous drone exploration for Gaussian map refinement using their Fisher-informed heuristic.Link
SWAP: Test-Time Steerable Humanoid Whole-Body Collision AvoidanceCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors propose a hierarchical collision-avoidance framework combining diffusion-based motion proposals with a whole-body reinforcement-learning controller for tracking and balance. The authors report reliable static- and dynamic-obstacle avoidance in simulation and on a real humanoid. The authors report that test-time steering improves targeted rewards by 34% on average while maintaining a high collision avoidance rate.Link
ForagerSim: Evaluating Embodied Map-Based Reasoning in Large-Scale Dynamic WorldsCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors introduce ForagerSim as a simulation platform for embodied map-based reasoning, covering search-and-rescue and foraging scenarios in mazes and large open areas. The authors report that, with simplified sensing, learned policies perform well in simple topologies but trail humans in larger or more complex environments where map-based baselines perform well.Link
Dream What I Can: Sparse Visual Planning for Long-Horizon Robot ManipulationStanford UniversityThe authors introduce DreamCan, a hierarchical policy pairing a video-model-based sparse visual planner with a low-level visuomotor policy. The authors report outperforming language, dense-video, and uniform-subgoal planners, with 91.2% success on LIBERO-Long using a 0.1B-parameter low-level policy. The authors also report real-world bimanual box folding and packing with unseen object combinations.Link
SteerAct: Unlocking Action Models through Few-Shot Temporal SteeringMassachusetts Institute of Technology; Stanford UniversityThe authors introduce SteerAct, which uses temporal descriptors of sparse-feature dynamics to select features offline and control activation steering online. The authors report approximately 10% higher task success relative to unsteered models using only 24 outcome-labeled rollouts per task, across the listed model, benchmark, and real-world manipulation evaluations, without updating action-model weights.Link
SSIO: Self-Supervised Inertial OdometryCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford University; UC BerkeleyThe authors train inertial odometry from short-window IMU integration supervision, then refine it using sequence-consistent pose-graph targets. The authors report that, across six datasets covering pedestrians and multiple robot types, training without velocity or position labels comes within 0.07~m/s average velocity error of a same-architecture supervised baseline.Link
Physical Words: A Vocabulary for Structuring Latent Reasoning in Robot ManipulationUC BerkeleyThe authors introduce PhysWord, which structures latent physical-dynamics reasoning using atomic action words and aligns their hidden states with future-observation targets. The authors report state-of-the-art success rates of 90.3% on visuotactile UniVTAC and 99.6% on vision-based LIBERO-Long, alongside better performance than competing methods on real-world bimanual-gripper and dexterous-hand tasks.Link
CusADi2: Latency-Optimized Batch Computation of Robot Kinematics, Jacobians, and Hessians on the GPUUC BerkeleyThe authors introduce a GPU library that reduces batch-evaluation latency by parallelizing computation within each batch instance. The authors report lower latency than all evaluated baselines at batch sizes of 64 to 4096, with matching latency at larger batch sizes.Link
CLARO: Clutter Aware Robot Orchestrator for Open-Vocabulary Grasping using VLM AgentsNew York UniversityThe authors introduce CLARO, a VLM agent that coordinates perception, grasping, and non-prehensile pushing to retrieve language-specified targets in clutter. In their simulation and image-benchmark evaluation, the authors report 89.1% average success and a relative improvement of over 16% over the strongest competing method in highly cluttered scenes. The authors report 88.8% success when deploying CLARO on a robot arm across diverse cluttered scenes.Link
Vision as Proprioception: Estimating Humanoid Robot Pose from VideoETH ZurichThe authors introduce HumanoidPose to recover full-body joint configurations from video, combining pretrained human-pose features with robot kinematics. The authors report reducing joint-angle error by 18-32% relative to the best-performing evaluated baseline on each of three real humanoid platforms.Link
ACTS: Learning Visuo-Tactile World Models from Human Physical InteractionMassachusetts Institute of Technology; Stanford University; UC Berkeley; University of WashingtonThe authors introduce ACTS, a visuo-tactile world model trained only on human interactions, using sensor motion augmented by force change as a shared human–robot action space. On held-out human data, the authors report scene-view prediction gains of 0.7-2.0 dB PSNR over the stronger of two visuo-tactile world models trained from scratch on React. Without fine-tuning, the authors observe contact-consistent tactile predictions in inspected robot teleoperation rollouts, but report mixed visual transfer, including worse-than-frame-copying predictions on Motherboard and blurred or hallucinated wrist-view structure.Link
MOMO: Human Motion from Moving SoundsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThe authors introduce MOMO, a motion-diffusion framework conditioned on audio semantics and dynamic spatial cues estimated from binaural audio rather than ground-truth source coordinates. The authors report that MOMO outperforms existing baselines in motion fidelity, audio-motion alignment, and physical plausibility.Link
VaDar: Implicit Goal Conditioning via Value DisaggregationCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors introduce VaDar, which uses per-subgoal critic values to train a single policy that does not require subgoal information at deployment. In their experiments, the authors report competitiveness with, and frequent improvements over, natural baselines such as goal-conditioning, alongside success on non-sequential goals and multiple objectives.Link
