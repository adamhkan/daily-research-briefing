Executive Summary

  • Long-horizon manipulation and rearrangement see multiple planning advances, from tree-search distillation in continuous action spaces (DISCO) to latent and visual-subgoal world models that reliably execute multi-step tasks in real and simulated settings.
  • Humanoid loco-manipulation and whole-body control are pushed forward via hierarchical VLA and latent-planning frameworks (SWAP, FIBER, HAF-VLA, DUET, Humanoid SRL), with several results validated on Unitree G1 hardware.
  • Vision-language(-action) foundations continue to expand in scale and robustness for manipulation and navigation, with structured reasoning primitives (PhysWord), temporal/action efficiency (PaceVLA, VLA-Rolling, SPAR, AutoPruner, CondenseVLA), and improved robustness to perturbations and depth noise (PICA-WAM, NISE, LIBERO-Pro2.0/Dynamic).
  • Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation gain new large-scale benchmarks (HandsOn, BiDex-100, T(R,O) Grasp++, ContactBench, HandsOn/Gen2Dex/DexOAK) and methods that leverage visuo-tactile feedback and human data for world modeling and retargeting.
  • Navigation, mapping, and motion planning receive GPU-accelerated, safety-certified, and monocular or egocentric solutions (FLASH-CBF-RRT, GOFusion, SpaRV, ESCAPE, InJEPA, ScaleNav, ChairNav), including multi-robot and social settings with uncertainty and reactivity (CoopUQ, RCRA, CAOS-SLAM).
  • Fleet and lifelong learning paradigms (CEL, SARAM, TILDE, PGPG) and value/feedback modeling benchmarks (RoboValue, PhysRSI-eval) target scalable deployment, alignment, and evaluation of foundation-model-based robot policies.

Long-Horizon Multi-Object Rearrangement via Distilled Tree Search over Continuous ActionsCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford UniversityDISCO combines progressive-widening MCTS in physics simulation with a learned continuous policy-value model, periodically distilling search-generated plans back into the network. The final policy executes long-horizon multi-object rearrangement without test-time search, outperforming behavior cloning, RL, and search baselines in simulation and on real robots.Link
SWAP: Test-Time Steerable Humanoid Whole-Body Collision AvoidanceCarnegie Mellon UniversitySWAP uses a hierarchical architecture where a perception-conditioned diffusion policy proposes collision-aware whole-body motions for a humanoid, and a low-level RL controller maintains dynamic stability. Hindsight-labeled supervision and Monte Carlo test-time steering enable safe, customizable avoidance in clutter on a Unitree G1.Link
Vector-Quantized World Model: Latent Feedback Motion Planning for Robot ManipulationNew York UniversityVQ-WM discretizes latent states with a vector-quantized world model and plans over them using a first-passage-time quasi-metric aligned with the controller's capabilities. A continuous visual feedback controller then tracks the planned latent waypoints, achieving 100% success on physical Tower of Hanoi and large gains over prior world models in diverse benchmarks.Link
CusADi2: Latency-Optimized Batch Computation of Robot Kinematics, Jacobians, and Hessians on the GPUUC BerkeleyCusADi2 is a GPU library that parallelizes both across batch instances and within each instance to accelerate evaluations of forward kinematics, Jacobians, and Hessians. Benchmarks on 67 MuJoCo Menagerie robots show substantially lower batch-evaluation latency than MuJoCo XLA/Warp, frax, CusADi, and CasADi, enabling faster sampling-based control and trajectory optimization.Link
Dream What I Can: Sparse Visual Planning for Long-Horizon Robot ManipulationStanford UniversityDreamCan turns pre-trained video models into high-level planners that output sparse visual subgoals, while a learned low-level visuomotor policy executes between them. Subgoal utilities and reachability are estimated from offline action-free videos via dynamic programming, yielding strong performance on LIBERO-Long and real bimanual tasks like box folding and packing.Link
Action-Aligned Latent Planning for Versatile Humanoid Loco-ManipulationCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford UniversityFIBER uses the executable latent space of a behavioral foundation model as a shared representation for diverse humanoid loco-manipulation skills, projecting task policies into latent commands that preserve local feedback. An adaptive-horizon BFM and offline training over aggregated latent rollouts yield a unified perceptive planner that substantially outperforms kinematic and latent-replay baselines.Link
ACTS: Learning Visuo-Tactile World Models from Human Physical InteractionMassachusetts Institute of Technology; University of WashingtonACTS trains a visuo-tactile world model solely on human-operated GelSight interactions, predicting future scene, wrist, and tactile observations from motion-plus-force actions. Without robot data, it transfers contact dynamics to robot teleoperation rollouts, suggesting that human-collected visuo-tactile data can supervise contact-rich manipulation models.Link
FLASH-CBF-RRT: Fast Lipschitz-Adaptive Safe Horizon CBF Steering for Certified Motion PlanningUC San DiegoFLASH-CBF-RRT accelerates control-barrier-function-based steering within RRT-Connect by computing adaptive safe horizons that allow larger steps in free space and smaller ones near obstacles. Across 689 MotionBenchMaker problems and 17-DoF mobile-manipulator hardware, it matches RRT-Connect’s success while running up to ~3.8× faster than fixed-step CBF-RRT.Link
Ropedia W0: Scaling Loco-Manipulation World Models across Embodiments with Human ExperienceUC San DiegoRopedia W0 is a foundation-scale egocentric loco-manipulation world model trained on 10k hours of human and 3k hours of robot data with unified annotations and kinematic representations. It achieves SOTA across five cross-embodiment benchmarks and, after distillation, enables real-time minute-long rollouts for tasks like policy evaluation and model-based planning.Link
CLARO: Clutter Aware Robot Orchestrator for Open-Vocabulary Grasping using VLM AgentsNew York UniversityCLARO is a VLM-based orchestrator that chains open-vocabulary detection, segmentation, grasping, and pushing skills to find and retrieve language-specified targets in heavy clutter. With action memory and skill-agnostic 6-DoF targets, it reaches ~89% success in simulation and ~89% on real arms, outperforming competing open-vocabulary grasping agents.Link
Vision as Proprioception: Estimating Humanoid Robot Pose from VideoETH ZurichHumanoidPose treats video as proprioception by fusing human-pose features with robot kinematics to predict full-body joint angles and positions in world space for multiple humanoid embodiments. Trained on synthetic retargeted data and adapted with self-training on online videos, it cuts joint-angle errors by 18–32% over baselines and enables recovery of robot motion from video-only data.Link
GOFusion: Real-Time Gaussian-Occupancy Submap Fusion for Fisher-Informed Autonomous Robot NavigationETH ZurichGOFusion anchors 3D Gaussians to local occupancy submaps to provide unified geometry and appearance for navigation, with Fisher-information-based sensitivity guiding Gaussian pruning and next-best-view exploration. It achieves real-time, uncertainty-aware mapping on drones and outperforms baselines in mapping quality and autonomous exploration efficiency.Link
Simulation Priors for Rapid Adaptation in Model-Based RL for Autonomous RacingUC San DiegoPIA pretrains world models on heterogeneous, biased simulation data and adapts them online with uncertainty-regularized planning, treating simulators as priors rather than ground truth. In high-speed autonomous racing, including on a full-size race car, it adapts to new tracks and surfaces with under an hour of interaction, outperforming strong baselines in lap time and reliability.Link
VaDar: Implicit Goal Conditioning via Value DisaggregationCarnegie Mellon UniversityVaDar trains separate critics per subgoal but a single policy that maximizes a linear combination of their values, eliminating goal conditioning at deployment. On long-horizon RoboCasa tasks, it outperforms goal-conditioned and multi-task baselines, solving problems even when starting from behavior-cloned policies with almost zero initial success.Link

DexOAK: Object-Aware Kinematic RetargetingINRIADexOAK retargets human-hand motion to robot hands by optimizing a joint hand–object graph to preserve interaction geometry, then amortizing this mapping with a neural network fine-tuned under contact and collision losses. It improves dynamic retargeting success and geometric accuracy on OakInk-v2 and ARCTIC and transfers zero-shot across datasets and to hardware.Link
Robot Learning with Vision-Language-Action Models for Agricultural ManipulationUsing a MuJoCo-based tomato environment, this work adapts Octo and π₀.₅ VLA policies to agricultural reaching and grasping via joystick teleoperation in simulation, followed by fine-tuning with limited real-robot demos. π₀.₅ shows better robustness to viewpoint and spatial shifts, demonstrating the feasibility of leveraging generalist VLAs for agricultural manipulation tasks.Link
HAF-VLA: Hierarchical Action Flow Framework for Humanoid Vision-Language-Action ControlHAF-VLA refactors a pretrained flow-matching VLA into staged whole-body action generation, first producing locomotion/head commands, then waist refinement, then manipulation, with cross-stage KV-cache conditioning. On real humanoid loco-manipulation tasks, this hierarchical refinement significantly improves stability and task success over monolithic VLA control.Link
Learning Geometry-Aware Planning Representations for Monocular Flight with Cross-Modal SupervisionThe authors distill planning-relevant features from a depth-based expert into a monocular RGB planner using paired RGB-D observations and cross-modal feature supervision. Solving a Giant Rubik's Cube with a Quadruped RobotThis system integrates onboard RGB-D perception, planning, and RL-trained whole-body skills to let a quadruped reorient and rotate a large Rubik’s Cube solely with its feet while maintaining balance. Distilled pedipulation policies achieve robust, multi-step mobile manipulation, completing full cube solves in 8–9 minutes without external tracking.Link
PrismPlan: A Shared GPU Substrate for Motion Planning Across ParadigmsPrismPlan implements batched per-sphere FK and collision checking once as a GPU substrate and builds sampling-, search-, and optimization-based planners atop it, enabling fair, backend-identical comparisons. Experiments on 1,800 MotionBenchMaker problems show sampling remains most reliable, while parallel search often offers lower latency, especially for 7-DoF arms.Link

Policies transfer zero-shot to hardware, achieving safer, more energy-efficient traversal in clutter, on slopes, and outdoors than planner–controller baselines.Link
