Executive Summary

  • Planning and computation: FLASH-CBF-RRT reportedly matched RRT-Connect's 100% success across 689 MotionBenchMaker problems while reducing median planning time by 3.82x. CusADi2 reportedly lowered GPU evaluation latency versus all tested baselines at batch sizes of 64 to 4096, but only matched them at larger batch sizes. (arXiv:2610.24149; arXiv:2610.25476)
  • Long-horizon manipulation: DreamCan reports 91.2% success on LIBERO-Long using sparse subgoals from action-free videos and a 0.1B-parameter low-level policy, plus real-world bimanual demonstrations. FIBER reports 42.0% mean simulation success across 128 variations per task, versus 11.6% for projected latent replay and 3.5% for kinematic planning; its hardware evidence consists of demonstrations. (arXiv:2610.24981; arXiv:2610.24675)
  • Hardware demonstrations show distinct capabilities, with different evaluation scopes: a quadruped completed three autonomous Rubik's Cube solves using only its feet, each taking about 30 steps over 8-9 minutes. CLARO reports 88.8% robot-arm retrieval success in clutter, while DexOAK reports 46/56 successful open-loop hardware replays across five tasks. (arXiv:2610.24610; arXiv:2610.24016; arXiv:2610.26762)
  • Adaptation and deployment: PIA reports reliable few-shot autonomous-racing adaptation with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction. ChairNav reports increasing mean distance between user interventions from 29.2 m to 57.4 m after post-training and autonomous operation for 93.4% of total driving time on kilometer-scale urban sidewalk routes. (arXiv:2610.26757; arXiv:2610.24012)
  • Evaluation exposes important limitations: on six ContactBench tasks, evaluated vision-only policies struggled with contact events, and visual-tactile policies did not meaningfully improve performance. ForagerSim's learned policies fell short of humans in larger or topologically complex environments under simplified 2D sensing. ACTS showed contact-consistent tactile predictions in inspected robot rollouts, but mixed visual transfer, sometimes worse than frame copying. (arXiv:2610.24512; arXiv:2610.26395; arXiv:2610.24285)
  • Workforce implications remain conditional: the robotics-exposure study reports higher exposure in physically intensive occupations and those with lower wages and education requirements. Its authors explicitly characterize these measures as an upper bound on potential exposure—not evidence of deployment readiness, cost competitiveness, or adoption. (arXiv:2610.24267)

FLASH-CBF-RRT: Fast Lipschitz-Adaptive Safe Horizon CBF Steering for Certified Motion PlanningUC San DiegoThe authors introduce FLASH-CBF-RRT, using closed-form collision-clearance bounds to choose how long a CBF-filtered velocity can remain constant. Across 689 MotionBenchMaker problems, the authors report matching RRT-Connect's 100% success rate while reducing median planning time by 3.82x; they also report being 3.88x faster than fixed-step CBF-RRT. On a 17-DoF mobile manipulator, the authors report 2.2x faster planning and 4.2x fewer evaluations.Link
Solving a Giant Rubik's Cube with a Quadruped RobotETH ZurichThe authors combine onboard RGB-D perception and planning with reinforcement-learned whole-body skills distilled into policies using onboard depth and proprioception. The authors report three autonomous hardware Rubik's Cube solves by a quadruped using only its feet, each taking about 30 steps over 8-9 minutes.Link
Action-Aligned Latent Planning for Versatile Humanoid Loco-ManipulationCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford UniversityThe authors propose FIBER, projecting task-specific policies into a frozen behavioral foundation model's executable latent space and training a unified perceptive planner offline. Across 128 variations per task in simulation, the authors report 42.0% mean success, compared with 11.6% for projected latent replay and 3.5% for kinematic planning. The authors report real-world demonstrations of sim-to-real transfer, skill chaining, and conditional steering with the unified planner.Link
Vector-Quantized World Model: Latent Feedback Motion Planning for Robot ManipulationNew York UniversityThe authors introduce VQ-WM, combining discrete latent search with continuous visual feedback control and using estimated first-passage time to align the latent abstraction with controller capabilities. For physical-robot Tower of Hanoi manipulation, the authors report 100\% success using only short-play data, contrasting with evaluated foundation models that succeed only when the solution trace is in their training dataset. The authors report outperforming the strongest world-model baselines by 13.3--85.3 percentage points on simulation benchmarks spanning manipulation and locomotion.Link
Long-Horizon Multi-Object Rearrangement via Distilled Tree Search over Continuous ActionsCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford UniversityThe authors introduce DISCO, which uses progressive-widening MCTS in physics simulation and periodically distills search-generated plans into a policy-value model deployable without simulator-based search. The authors report outperforming behavior cloning, reinforcement learning, and search-based baselines across simulated and real-world rearrangement tasks.Link
Policy Search in Code Space for Multi-Agent PathfindingMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThe authors study human-directed policy search in code space, with coding agents refining algorithms through simulation feedback and correctness verification. On a challenging competition map, the authors report that a two-day search found a policy exceeding the stronger locally reproduced competition entry by 35.0% on the densest instance, with generalization to unseen map instances. In simulated warehouses, the authors report 58.9% higher throughput than a calibrated proprietary solution; under stated assumptions, they also prove collision freedom and finite-time completion of accepted goals that are not withdrawn or replaced.Link
Simulation Priors for Rapid Adaptation in Model-Based RL for Autonomous RacingUC San DiegoThe authors introduce PIA, pretraining a world model on heterogeneous simulated trajectories and adapting it with limited target-domain interaction through uncertainty-regularized planning. In autonomous-racing simulation, the authors report faster adaptation across track and surface shifts than strong baselines, with lower lap times and fewer failures. On hardware, the authors report reliable few-shot adaptation with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction.Link
DexOAK: Object-Aware Kinematic RetargetingETH ZurichThe authors introduce DexOAK, learning object-aware hand retargeting from graph-based seed references and refining the network with contact, non-penetration, and self-collision losses. On the Inspire hand, the authors report success improving from 39.5% to 48.4% on OakInk-v2 and ADD-AUC improving from 62.7% to 77.9% on ARCTIC. The authors report that 46/56 open-loop hardware replays succeed across five tasks.Link
CoopUQ: Robust Multi-Robot Social Navigation via Uncertainty-Aware Cooperative ForecastingNew York UniversityThe authors propose CoopUQ, combining cross-robot occupancy forecasting with constrained multi-agent reinforcement learning that penalizes predicted occupied or highly uncertain regions. The authors report improved prediction and safety over representative baselines in Unity3D simulations covering in-distribution and OOD settings, and robustness across scenes in real-robot experiments.Link
Ropedia W0: Scaling Loco-Manipulation World Models across Embodiments with Human ExperienceUC San DiegoThe authors introduce Ropedia W0, a cross-embodiment egocentric loco-manipulation world model using camera poses for locomotion and projected skeletons for manipulation. The authors report state-of-the-art performance across five named cross-embodiment benchmarks and held-out human egocentric video, covering zero-shot inference through few-shot and full-data post-training. After distillation, the authors report real-time, minute-long rollouts supporting live VR interaction.Link
GOFusion: Real-Time Gaussian-Occupancy Submap Fusion for Fisher-Informed Autonomous Robot NavigationETH ZurichThe authors introduce GOFusion, which anchors Gaussian primitives to occupancy maps and uses an online Fisher approximation to guide pruning and next-best-view planning. The authors report evaluation on public datasets and real-world drone flights, with better mapping quality than several baselines and real-time mapping.Link
SWAP: Test-Time Steerable Humanoid Whole-Body Collision AvoidanceCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors propose a hierarchical framework combining diffusion-based motion proposals with a whole-body RL controller for tracking and balance. The authors report reliable static and dynamic obstacle avoidance in simulation and on a real Unitree G1, with test-time steering improving targeted rewards by 34% on average while maintaining a high collision avoidance rate.Link
ForagerSim: Evaluating Embodied Map-Based Reasoning in Large-Scale Dynamic WorldsCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors introduce ForagerSim, a simulation platform for embodied map-based reasoning, and compare RL baselines, an engineered robotics stack, and a human player on 8 example worlds. The authors report that with simplified 2D sensing, learned policies handle search and well-alternation in simple topologies but fall short of humans in larger or topologically more complex environments, where map-based baselines perform well.Link
ContactBench 1.0: A Benchmark for Everyday Contact-Rich Robotic ManipulationStanford UniversityThe authors introduce ContactBench 1.0, a contact-rich manipulation benchmark with 36 reference tasks and more than 40 object assets. On six benchmark tasks, the authors report that the evaluated vision-only policies struggle with contact events and the evaluated visual-tactile policies do not meaningfully improve performance.Link
Dream What I Can: Sparse Visual Planning for Long-Horizon Robot ManipulationStanford UniversityThe authors introduce DreamCan, a hierarchical policy using sparse visual subgoals selected by dynamic programming from offline, action-free robot videos without manual annotations. The authors report outperforming language, dense-video, and uniform-subgoal planners, including 91.2% success on LIBERO-Long with a 0.1B-parameter low-level policy. The authors also report real-world bimanual box folding and packing with unseen object combinations.Link
SteerAct: Unlocking Action Models through Few-Shot Temporal SteeringMassachusetts Institute of Technology; Stanford UniversityThe authors introduce SteerAct, using temporal descriptors of sparse-feature dynamics for offline feature selection and online activation steering. The authors report approximately 10% improved task success relative to unsteered models using only 24 outcome-labeled rollouts per task, across the listed model, benchmark, and real-world evaluations without action-model weight updates.Link
Physical Words: A Vocabulary for Structuring Latent Reasoning in Robot ManipulationUC BerkeleyThe authors introduce PhysWord, aligning predicted atomic action words' contextual hidden states with latent targets from future observations. The authors report state-of-the-art success rates of 90.3\% on visuotactile UniVTAC and 99.6\% on vision-based LIBERO-Long, as well as outperforming competing methods on real-world bimanual-gripper and dexterous-hand tasks.Link
CusADi2: Latency-Optimized Batch Computation of Robot Kinematics, Jacobians, and Hessians on the GPUUC BerkeleyThe authors introduce CusADi2, which reduces GPU batch evaluation latency by parallelizing computation within each batch instance. For kinematics, Jacobian, and Hessian benchmarks across the MuJoCo Menagerie, the authors report lower latency than all tested baselines at batch sizes of 64 to 4096, and matching them at larger batch sizes.Link
SSIO: Self-Supervised Inertial OdometryCarnegie Mellon University; Stanford University; UC BerkeleyThe authors train inertial odometry from scratch using short-window IMU integration as self-supervision, then refine it with pose graph distillation. Across six datasets covering pedestrians, drones, quadrupeds, and a humanoid, the authors report training without velocity or position labels and coming within 0.07~m/s average velocity error of a same-architecture supervised baseline.Link
CLARO: Clutter Aware Robot Orchestrator for Open-Vocabulary Grasping using VLM AgentsNew York UniversityThe authors introduce CLARO, a VLM agent orchestrating detection, segmentation, grasping, and pushing to retrieve language-specified objects from clutter. In evaluations using a dynamic simulation environment and image-based benchmarks, the authors report 89.1% average success and over 16% relative improvement over the strongest competing method in highly cluttered scenes. The authors report 88.8% success when deploying CLARO on a robot arm across diverse cluttered scenes.Link
Vision as Proprioception: Estimating Humanoid Robot Pose from VideoETH ZurichThe authors introduce HumanoidPose, combining pretrained human-pose features with robot kinematics to recover joint configurations from video alone. The authors report joint-angle error reductions of 18-32% relative to the best-performing baseline on each of three real humanoid platforms.Link
ACTS: Learning Visuo-Tactile World Models from Human Physical InteractionMassachusetts Institute of Technology; Stanford University; UC Berkeley; University of WashingtonThe authors introduce ACTS, a world model trained only on human interaction data that predicts scene, wrist, and tactile observations. On held-out human data, the authors report scene-view prediction gains of 0.7-2.0 dB PSNR over the stronger of two comparison models trained from scratch on React. In inspected robot rollouts without fine-tuning, the authors report contact-consistent tactile predictions, but visual transfer was mixed and sometimes worse than frame copying.Link
MOMO: Human Motion from Moving SoundsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThe authors introduce MOMO, conditioning motion diffusion on audio semantics and dynamic spatial cues estimated from binaural audio rather than ground-truth source coordinates. The authors report that MOMO outperforms existing baselines in motion fidelity, audio-motion alignment, and physical plausibility.Link
VaDar: Implicit Goal Conditioning via Value DisaggregationCarnegie Mellon UniversityThe authors introduce VaDar, training a subgoal-independent policy using a linear combination of per-subgoal critic values, with subgoal information needed only during training. The authors report that VaDar is competitive with, and frequently outperforms, natural baselines such as goal-conditioning, and succeeds with non-sequential goals and multiple objectives.Link
How exposed is work to robotics? Occupational, industry, and geographic measuresMassachusetts Institute of Technology; New York UniversityThe authors construct robotics exposure measures using a capability taxonomy and language-model scores relating capabilities to occupational abilities. The authors report that exposure concentrates in physically intensive work and is higher in occupations with lower wages and education requirements than the pattern for generative-AI exposure. The authors caution that these measures are an upper bound on potential exposure, not measures of readiness, cost, or adoption.Link
