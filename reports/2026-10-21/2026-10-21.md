## Executive Summary

- Planning efficiency: FLASH-CBF-RRT reportedly matched RRT-Connect's 100% success across 689 MotionBenchMaker problems while reducing median planning time by 3.82x. DISCO reports that distilling simulation search into a policy-value model enables deployment without simulator-based search and outperforms evaluated baselines on simulated and real-world rearrangement tasks. ([arXiv:2610.24149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24149); [arXiv:2610.25785](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25785))
- Long-horizon manipulation: DreamCan reports 91.2% success on LIBERO-Long using a 0.1B-parameter low-level policy, plus real-world bimanual folding and packing demonstrations. PhysWord separately reports 99.6% on LIBERO-Long and 90.3% on UniVTAC, alongside gains on real-world manipulation tasks; these summaries do not establish a controlled head-to-head comparison. ([arXiv:2610.24981](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24981); [arXiv:2610.26854](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26854))
- World-model results include important transfer limits: VQ-WM reports outperforming its strongest world-model baselines by 13.3--85.3 percentage points on simulation benchmarks. ACTS, trained only on human interactions, reports contact-consistent tactile predictions in inspected robot rollouts without fine-tuning, but mixed visual transfer, including worse-than-frame-copying predictions and hallucinated structure. ([arXiv:2610.25600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25600); [arXiv:2610.24285](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24285))
- Adaptation and deployment: PIA reports reliable few-shot adaptation on a full-size autonomous race car with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction. ChairNav reports increasing mean distance between user interventions from 29.2 m to 57.4 m after post-training, with autonomous operation for 93.4% of driving time on kilometer-scale urban sidewalk routes. ([arXiv:2610.26757](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26757); [arXiv:2610.24012](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24012))
- Multi-robot coordination: Human-directed code-space policy search reports 58.9% higher throughput than a calibrated proprietary solution in simulated warehouses. Its collision-freedom and finite-time-completion proofs depend on stated assumptions and apply to accepted goals that are not withdrawn or replaced. CoopUQ separately reports improved prediction and safety in Unity3D evaluations and robustness in real-robot experiments. ([arXiv:2610.25946](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25946); [arXiv:2610.26852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26852))
- Humanoid control: A hierarchical diffusion-and-reinforcement-learning framework reports static- and dynamic-obstacle avoidance in simulation and on hardware; test-time steering improved targeted rewards by 34% on average while maintaining a high collision avoidance rate. DUET demonstrates one locomotion checkpoint with two vision-language-action models on hardware, while its no-falls result is reported in simulation. ([arXiv:2610.25711](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25711); [arXiv:2610.23872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23872))
