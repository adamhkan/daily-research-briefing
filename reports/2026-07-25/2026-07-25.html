Executive Summary

  • Multi-robot coordination and navigation see strong advances, from distributed diffusion-based trajectory optimization to socially consistent multi-robot navigation and AV coordination at unsignalized intersections.
  • Manipulation research emphasizes robustness in contact-rich and visuo-tactile settings, scalable data engines and datasets, and unified control frameworks that explicitly couple high-level policies with low-level impedance/admittance behavior.
  • New navigation formulations and systems, including vision-only long-horizon UAV navigation, monocular VLM-based indoor navigation, and capability-aware traversability, target long-horizon, embodiment-aware deployment in unstructured environments.
  • Aerial and multi-drone systems progress with safe, scalable payload transport via CBF-based RL that transfers zero-shot to real hardware and decentralized UAV swarms for target protection in GPS/communication-denied settings.
  • Benchmarking and data generation efforts expand with large-scale tabletop and teleoperation-based manipulation datasets, Gaussian-splatting SLAM for large-scale mapping, and domain-specific datasets for cluttered transparent lab objects and topological road maps.
  • Several works focus on interpretability and compositionality in policies—through mixture-of-experts VLAs, bias-aware compositional evaluation, correctable visual attention, and factorized latent coordination for AVs.

Distributed Model-Based Diffusion For Scalable Multi-Robot Trajectory OptimizationUniversity of MichiganExtends model-based diffusion to multi-robot systems by decomposing the reverse diffusion process into local conditional denoising steps coordinated via a central server. The resulting Distributed MBD framework scales trajectory optimization to high-dimensional, non-convex coordination tasks like multi-floor coverage and rush-hour traffic, solving them in sub-seconds and outperforming existing baselines.Link
Socially Consistent Multi-Robot Navigation Using Decoupled Planning and Trajectory CoordinationPresents a partially decentralized navigation framework that embeds social norms into a modified A* global planner and then coordinates multi-robot trajectories via a mixed-integer convex program. Sharing socially compliant paths across robots builds a social route graph, yielding predictable, human-aligned motion and simplifying downstream multi-robot coordination.Link
VoLN: Vision-Only Long-Horizon Navigation---Paradigm, Benchmark, and MethodDefines Vision-Only Long-Horizon Navigation, shifting guidance from explicit language instructions to locally observable visual cues and goal views. The VoLN-UAV benchmark and VoLN-MLLM baseline expose major challenges in long-horizon evidence integration and cross-view goal matching for aerial robots operating under continuous 3D motion.Link
Robostral NavigateIntroduces an 8B vision-language navigation model that uses only monocular RGB streams to predict image-space waypoints, enabling embodiment-agnostic deployment across wheeled, legged, and aerial platforms. Trained on 2.4M simulated trajectories with a prefix-caching recipe and RL fine-tuning, it sets new SOTA on R2R-CE and RxR-CE while outperforming depth and multi-camera systems.Link
ZONDA: Zero-shot Object Navigation with Dynamic Avoidance in Multi-floor EnvironmentsProposes a zero-shot object-goal navigation framework that unifies heuristic multi-floor planning, multi-view target verification via a VLM, and explicit pedestrian tracking for dynamic obstacle avoidance. Demonstrated on a real biped robot and large-scale simulators, ZONDA enables robust cross-floor, dynamic navigation without task-specific training.Link
Towards Capability-Aware Traversability Navigation for Unstructured EnvironmentsMobile Robotics Group, University of São Paulo, São Carlos, SP, Brazil.Correspondence toIntroduces Capability-Aware Traversability (CAT), which embeds robot-specific physical limits directly into a spatial feature space using SPADE-conditioned semantic terrain maps. The method improves AUROC and AUPRC over baselines on trajectory-aligned data and runs on legged and wheeled robots at 4.8 Hz, yielding embodiment-aware obstacle avoidance in unstructured terrain.Link
Self-Supervised Bio-Inspired Robotic Trajectory Planning with Obstacle AvoidanceExtends a neuro-inspired self-supervised trajectory planning framework to environments with obstacles, leveraging coupled forward and inverse models for internal supervision. Experiments show the planner can generate collision-free trajectories but also reveal exploitation of the learning signal, prompting proposed training and mitigation strategies.Link
A Real-Time Generalized Nash Equilibrium Framework for Interaction-Aware Autonomous Driving in Mixed TrafficModels AV–human-driver interactions as a Generalized Nash Equilibrium Problem with shared safety and geometric constraints, explicitly coupling each agent’s feasible strategies. A custom particle-swarm-based solver achieves sub-50 ms convergence and generates comfortable, human-like trajectories validated on a real autonomous Renault Zoé in mixed traffic.Link
Deep Reinforcement-Learning-Guided Model Predictive Control for Preventing Overtakes in Autonomous RacingNational University of Singapore; University of MichiganFormulates defensive racing as a spatial occupancy regulation problem, combining a SAC-based strategic layer in Frenet coordinates with a nonlinear MPC controller under friction constraints. On a racing circuit, the framework substantially delays overtakes and reduces opponent progress while operating in real time near vehicle dynamic limits.Link
Decentralized UAV Swarms for Ground Target Protection in GPS- and Communication-Denied EnvironmentsComputing and the Ingenuity Labs Research Institute; Czech Technical University in Prague; Queen's UniversityDevelops a decentralized UAV-swarm defense system that relies solely on onboard sensing and relative measurements, without GPS or inter-UAV communication. Kalman filters estimate both hostile and friendly states, and a swarm encirclement strategy adapts to target motion, with real-robot tests showing effective detection, encirclement, and interception of attacking UAVs.Link
