## Executive Summary

- Humanoid and whole-body control sees notable advances, from text-driven diffusion action priors (ADAPT) to a practical architecture for fast loco-manipulation behavior authoring on real humanoids.
- New benchmarks and frameworks target underexplored regimes: aerial manipulation (AM-Bench), high-precision insertion (Peg-in-Bench), and counterfactual evaluation for imitation-learning robustness and negation-aware dexterous grasping.
- Foundation-style models and VLAs are pushed toward contact-rich, long-horizon manipulation via contact-aware action-wrench prediction (Facet-0), reusable skill libraries (REFACTOR-VLA), and adaptive action chunking.
- Navigation and motion planning under uncertainty are strengthened through dual-process neuro-symbolic planners, scalable Rao-Blackwellized POMDP solvers, and verifier-first low-latency VLN.
- Multi-robot and dynamic-environment operation is addressed with connectivity-aware multi-UAV exploration, cooperative drone perception with runtime context planes, and dynamic 3D memories/scene graphs.
- Control and hardware contributions span EPID guarantees for multi-DOF manipulators, non-prehensile throwing with RL, soft and dexterous hardware (variable-transmission fingers, origami actuators), and wearable bidirectional haptics for manipulation.
