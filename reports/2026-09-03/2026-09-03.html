Executive Summary

  • Humanoid and whole-body control sees notable advances, from text-driven diffusion action priors (ADAPT) to a practical architecture for fast loco-manipulation behavior authoring on real humanoids.
  • New benchmarks and frameworks target underexplored regimes: aerial manipulation (AM-Bench), high-precision insertion (Peg-in-Bench), and counterfactual evaluation for imitation-learning robustness and negation-aware dexterous grasping.
  • Foundation-style models and VLAs are pushed toward contact-rich, long-horizon manipulation via contact-aware action-wrench prediction (Facet-0), reusable skill libraries (REFACTOR-VLA), and adaptive action chunking.
  • Navigation and motion planning under uncertainty are strengthened through dual-process neuro-symbolic planners, scalable Rao-Blackwellized POMDP solvers, and verifier-first low-latency VLN.
  • Multi-robot and dynamic-environment operation is addressed with connectivity-aware multi-UAV exploration, cooperative drone perception with runtime context planes, and dynamic 3D memories/scene graphs.
  • Control and hardware contributions span EPID guarantees for multi-DOF manipulators, non-prehensile throwing with RL, soft and dexterous hardware (variable-transmission fingers, origami actuators), and wearable bidirectional haptics for manipulation.

ADAPT: Agile Diffusion Action Priors for Robust and Steerable Online Text-Driven Humanoid ControlETH ZurichADAPT learns a diffusion-based action prior over humanoid state-action trajectories, enabling end-to-end, closed-loop whole-body control directly from text commands. A residual RL layer atop the frozen diffusion controller improves long-horizon robustness, smooth prompt switching, and allows reuse of the same motion prior for downstream task adaptation.Link
AM-Bench: A Modular Simulation Suite and Benchmark for Aerial Manipulation Policy LearningCarnegie Mellon UniversityAM-Bench provides a standardized simulation suite for multirotor-based aerial manipulation, covering diverse embodiments, 12 contact and transport tasks, and configurable disturbances. It supports system-level evaluation across embodiment, low-level control, and policy design, with simulation studies and real-world validation demonstrating its diagnostic value.Link

Facet-0: A Robotic Foundation Model for Contact-Rich Precise ManipulationNanyang Technological UniversityFacet-0 is a foundation model that jointly predicts action chunks and their induced wrist-wrench profiles, aligning wrench histories with vision-language and state. A distributional Action-Wrench Critic and lightweight task-specific actor enable robust sub-millimeter assembly, achieving large gains over baselines on computer-assembly tasks.Link
A System for Fast, Resilient, and Adaptable Loco-Manipulation Behaviors on Humanoid RobotsThe authors present a behavior architecture for humanoid loco-manipulation that fuses affordance templates, tree-structured logic, and runtime-editable perception. A whole-body controller supports concurrent walking and manipulation, enabling rapid authoring and adaptation of tasks like door traversal and object sorting under disturbance.Link
Does Imitation Learning Preserve Temporal Robustness in Dexterous Manipulation? An Expert-Learner Comparison Across Task Execution SpeedsUsing a contact-rich parcel insertion task, this study compares an expert controller and ACT imitation policies across execution speeds. While both succeed at nominal speed, learned policies degrade more under speedup, especially in insertion alignment, highlighting that matching nominal success does not imply temporal robustness.Link

Field trials across varied outdoor sites show 93–96% area coverage with robust docking and recovery for long-duration missions.Link

Non-Prehensile Throwing: A Reinforcement Learning Perspective. Domae are with the National InstituteThe authors develop an RL-based approach for non-prehensile throwing that optimizes joint-jerk trajectories, exploiting sliding and rolling contacts without explicit contact models. A Compact Robotic Finger with 2-DoF MCP Joint Embedding DoF-Selective Passive Continuously Variable Transmission for Wide Force-Speed Operating RangeThis work presents a 2-DoF robotic finger whose MCP joint integrates a passive CVT on the flexion axis while keeping direct transmission for abduction-adduction.
