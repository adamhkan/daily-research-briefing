Executive Summary

  • Mobile manipulation and VLA: New flow-based RL (RLMM-Flow) and verification (CheckVLA) methods significantly boost long-horizon mobile manipulation reliability and efficiency, while compact controllers like TurboVLA reduce inference cost for real-time use.
  • Navigation and localization: BioVLN and embodied agent studies expand visual-language navigation to biomedical labs and zero-shot indoor settings, and a diffusion-based floorplan localizer improves multimodal indoor pose estimation without ray matching.
  • Planning and control theory: Risk-aware motion planning with learned primitives, temporal-logic-conditioned trajectory generation, and new MPC/online-learning and control designs advance safe, optimal motion in uncertain or adversarial environments.
  • Manipulation skill learning and transfer: Frameworks like HERO, Pegasus, ContactFlow, and MoMo focus on self-improving manipulation, cross-embodiment transfer from human videos, embodiment-agnostic contact representations, and controllable motion modes.
  • Specialized manipulation and interfaces: Surgical needle pickup via thread, speech-conditioned grasping, analytic concept guidance for VLA, and robust cable-driven control broaden manipulation in surgical, humanoid, and compliant-robot settings.
  • Institution highlights: Stanford, CMU, UCSD, and MIT report results on latency-robust legged locomotion, autonomous surgical needle pickup, globally stabilizing vehicle parking control, semi-decentralized spacecraft collision avoidance, and compliant multi-robot marine autonomy.

Reinforcement Learning on Cost-Constrained Quadrupedal HardwareStanford UniversityDemonstrates robust RL-based locomotion on the low-cost Mini Pupper 2 despite >50 ms actuator delays, which make the problem partially observable. A time-aware network paired with a learned forward delay model produces a central-pattern-generator-like gait tolerant to large latency perturbations, suggesting temporal self-organization as a viable strategy for cost-constrained whole-body control.Link
Reeling It In: Flexible Needle Pick Up via Thread Manipulation for Autonomous SuturingCarnegie Mellon University; UC San DiegoIntroduces an autonomous suturing framework that uses the suture thread, rather than direct needle contact, to recover dropped or occluded needles. The system reconstructs thread and tissue geometry, plans safe grasp points and bimanual thread-following, and robustly secures the needle on a da Vinci robot, reducing tissue contact and expanding reachable configurations.Link
Global Exponential Stabilization of the Kinematic Bicycle Model of a Car in Polar CoordinatesUC San DiegoReformulates the kinematic bicycle model in polar and range-normalized coordinates to bypass Brockett’s obstruction and enable smooth static feedback stabilization. A tailored backstepping design achieves global exponential stabilization and generates parking trajectories that resemble human-like maneuvers using feedback alone.Link
Semi-Decentralized Multi-Spacecraft Collision Avoidance under Communication ConstraintsStanford UniversityModels multi-spacecraft conjunction avoidance as a Semi-Decentralized POMDP governed by realistic, intermittent ground-station visibility windows. Using an RS-SDA*-based planner, the approach achieves near-centralized maneuver quality with substantially fewer synchronization events than continuous coordination and outperforms rule-based operator heuristics.Link
Multi-Objective Compliance-Integrated Coevolution For Simulated And Real-World Deployment Of Multi-Robot Marine AutonomyMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyPresents MMOCIC, a multi-objective framework that coevolves multi-robot marine behaviors while explicitly integrating compliance with safety and regulatory norms. By decoupling learning from compliance, the system balances team progress and norm adherence in swimmer-rescue missions, scaling to large robot teams in both hardware and simulation.Link

RLMM-Flow: A Flow-based Mobile Manipulation Framework with Latent-Space Reinforcement LearningProposes RLMM-Flow, which learns a flow-based whole-body motion prior for mobile manipulation from demonstrations and then refines it via latent-space RL. A frozen flow policy and a learned latent steering network enable coarse-to-fine adjustment of high-dimensional action chunks, yielding higher success rates, fewer collisions, and smoother trajectories while preserving fast inference.Link
CheckVLA: Execution-Time Verification with Action-Conditioned World Model for Long-Horizon Mobile ManipulationIntroduces CheckVLA, which monitors long-horizon VLA policies using a frozen action-conditioned world model to detect deviations during chunked execution. A conformal risk threshold, latency-aware prefixing, and keyframe-based repair allow timely, low-false-alarm interventions that significantly outperform periodic replanning in mobile manipulation benchmarks.Link
Vision-TL-Action: Neuro-Symbolic Trajectory Generation from Visual Observations and Temporal LogicDevelops Vision-TL-Action, which maps multi-view images and temporal-logic goal graphs directly to action trajectories using cross-attention between visual and TL tokens and a flow-matching generator. It achieves higher or comparable Success@K than oracle-state baselines on Panda and AntMaze tasks, enabling perception-grounded TL-conditioned motion generation without explicit object geometry at test time.Link
Risk-Aware Motion Planning with Learned Trajectory Primitives and Probabilistic Safety AssessmentCombines an RBFN that learns jerk-minimal trajectory primitives with analytic collision probability evaluation and optimization-based refinement for urban autonomous driving. The planner operates in a reduced, dynamically coherent search space and selects candidates via probabilistic risk measures, improving safety and constraint satisfaction relative to baselines.Link
BioVLN: A Simulation Platform for Visual Language Navigation in Biomedical LaboratoriesIntroduces BioVLN, a visual-language navigation simulator tailored to biomedical labs where instruments must be approached from specific operating sides with clearance constraints. It models each instrument with body, clearance, and operation regions for scene generation, evaluation, and safety analysis, and shows that sampling multiple valid operation-area poses improves success and safety over geometric exploration alone.Link
From Uncertainty to Determinism: Coarse-to-Fine Visual Floorplan Localization without Ray MatchingProposes a ray-free visual floorplan localization pipeline that uses an image-conditioned pose diffusion model to capture multimodal global pose hypotheses, followed by a localized refiner for sub-meter accuracy. The method avoids heavy preprocessing and lookup structures while achieving state-of-the-art accuracy and robustness on S3D and ZInD indoor localization benchmarks.Link
Self-Adaptive Learning and Model Predictive Control for Tracking Unknown Dynamics with No RegretUniversity of MichiganPresents an online learning plus MPC framework that simultaneously trains multiple motion predictors and adaptively selects among them to track targets with unknown, switching dynamics. The approach offers finite-time near-optimality and no-regret guarantees under certain conditions and is validated on Crazyflie hardware tracking structured, random, and adversarial trajectories.Link
Embodied Agents Take Control: Minimal-Interface Zero-Shot Agents Rival Industrial-Scale Policies in Vision-and-Language NavigationEvaluates general-purpose software-agent harnesses for zero-shot vision-and-language navigation with only monocular RGB and discrete actions. Despite the minimal interface, certain model–harness combinations achieve success rates competitive with specialized industrial-scale policies, though performance degrades on long horizons due to latency and context limits.Link
Global Sensitive-Based Input Shaping for UAV-Payload Precision Motion ControlDesigns robust global sensitivity-based and Shapley-value-informed input shapers for 3D UAV–payload systems subject to uncertainties in payload mass and rope length. Simulation studies show improved precision and robustness over non-robust, robust, and minimax controllers, advancing uncertainty-aware aerial payload motion control.Link
Time-delay Control Using a New Nonlinear Adaptive Law for Cable-Driven RobotsProposes a TDE-based adaptive fractional-order nonsingular terminal sliding mode controller for cable-driven manipulators, featuring a new nonlinear adaptive gain law with an exponential term. The controller reduces tracking error metrics and chattering compared to baselines and other adaptive laws, while maintaining robustness to disturbances and payload changes.Link
