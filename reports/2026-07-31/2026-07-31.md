## Executive Summary

- Mobile manipulation and VLA: New flow-based RL (RLMM-Flow) and verification (CheckVLA) methods significantly boost long-horizon mobile manipulation reliability and efficiency, while compact controllers like TurboVLA reduce inference cost for real-time use.
- Navigation and localization: BioVLN and embodied agent studies expand visual-language navigation to biomedical labs and zero-shot indoor settings, and a diffusion-based floorplan localizer improves multimodal indoor pose estimation without ray matching.
- Planning and control theory: Risk-aware motion planning with learned primitives, temporal-logic-conditioned trajectory generation, and new MPC/online-learning and control designs advance safe, optimal motion in uncertain or adversarial environments.
- Manipulation skill learning and transfer: Frameworks like HERO, Pegasus, ContactFlow, and MoMo focus on self-improving manipulation, cross-embodiment transfer from human videos, embodiment-agnostic contact representations, and controllable motion modes.
- Specialized manipulation and interfaces: Surgical needle pickup via thread, speech-conditioned grasping, analytic concept guidance for VLA, and robust cable-driven control broaden manipulation in surgical, humanoid, and compliant-robot settings.
- Institution highlights: Stanford, CMU, UCSD, and MIT report results on latency-robust legged locomotion, autonomous surgical needle pickup, globally stabilizing vehicle parking control, semi-decentralized spacecraft collision avoidance, and compliant multi-robot marine autonomy.
