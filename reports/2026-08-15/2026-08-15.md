## Executive Summary

- Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation see new data-efficient learning frameworks, safety monitors, and simulation/benchmarking tools aimed at generalization and robustness.
- Navigation work spans humanoid and aerial VLN, underwater and relay-based localization, and socially compliant crowd navigation via proxemics-aware reward shaping.
- New hierarchical and event-structured world models, teleoperation safety layers, and budget-aware skill practice target long-horizon and constrained robot learning.
- Benchmarks such as RoboSynChallenge, H2R-Bench, HumanTracker, and Hand2Bot systematically evaluate manipulation, tracking, and human-to-robot transfer capabilities.
- Perception advances include adaptive visual bottlenecks, functional affordance exploration, robust VINS and AUV relative pose estimation, and egocentric force/contact inference.
