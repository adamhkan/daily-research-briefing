## Executive Summary

- Strong focus today on safe, long-horizon navigation and exploration, from occlusion-aware barrier functions and cost-sensitive failure prediction to new UAV and ground-robot exploration planners.
- Humanoid whole-body control and loco-manipulation advance via vision-language navigation (TANGO), predictive motion tracking (PGMT, GLoRI, RoboDreamer), and unified interaction priors (CHIP, SkillX).
- Manipulation sees deep work on dexterous hands and VLA integration, including benchmarking (NSF HAND review), contact- and force-aware VLAs (DeCAL, CR-VLA-Force), and hybrid global–local VLA control (GloVLA).
- Multiple CMU, MIT, UCSD, UC Berkeley, NYU and Stanford papers push core capabilities: dexterity benchmarks, spatially conditioned multi-robot manipulation, informative path planning, SLAM with PDA, safe MARL, robust VINS/odometry, and novel human sensing.
- Navigation and mapping pipelines are stress-tested under evolving, uncertain, or learned geometry (EvoNav-Bench, OVMAN, OcclusionCBF, SHIFT, LightSplat), with new active mapping diagnostics and filters for learned occupancy.
- Teleoperation, simulation, and data-generation infrastructures (M3-Tele, SPOT, RoboCousin, mjorbit, IM-ENGINE, GIF) continue to scale up training data for whole-body and bimanual manipulation.
