Executive Summary

  • Whole-body humanoid control and loco-manipulation see several advances, from precise SE(3) end-effector tracking and robust human-data-driven skills to forceful object handling and learned locomotion styles.
  • Navigation and motion planning are strongly represented, with new benchmarks for collaborative air–ground VLN and lifelong small-object search, faster lattice planning and multi-robot braid MPC, and risk-aware dynamic replanning.
  • Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation work spans visuo-tactile grasp stability, contact-factorized imitation, sim-to-real tactile control, and robust timing/retiming layers for action-chunk and VLA policies.
  • Tooling and infrastructure for planning and control improve via geodesic motion-planning libraries, fast learned contact dynamics, belief-space planning with estimator error, and GPU-accelerated parallel simulations for adaptive MPC.
  • Multiple benchmarks and datasets target higher-level embodied intelligence, including RobotWorld and RoboQuest for multimodal agents, OpenViTac for visuo-tactile policies, and new datasets for sign-based navigation and manipulation localization.
  • Institution-matched work from CMU, MIT, UC Berkeley, UC San Diego, and ETH Zurich focuses on deformable-object manipulation, precise humanoid tracking, generalist locomotion/manipulation policies, diffusion-policy multimodality, multi-robot task admission, offline RL, and geometry-aware robotic assembly.

Point It, Strike It: Direction-Conditioned Dynamic Manipulation of Deformable Linear ObjectsCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces DeformX2.0, a massively accelerated Cosserat-rod simulator with aerodynamics, and TRACE, a sampling method for generating reliable rope-strike data. A conditional flow-matching policy plus a residual calibration policy (RECAP) adapts sim parameters and actions to real ropes, achieving high-accuracy, direction-conditioned rope striking across multiple ropes on hardware.Link
Precise SE(3) End-Effector Tracking in Whole-Body Humanoid ControlUC BerkeleyProposes ResGAC, combining geometric admittance control with residual RL for precise end-effector SE(3) tracking on a floating-base humanoid. A ground-attached heading frame reduces pelvis-induced motion in the manipulation reference, yielding markedly better tracking and success in peg-in-hole tasks compared with SONIC on the Unitree G1.Link
Workhorse: Learning Robust Whole-Body Humanoid Loco-Manipulation from Human DataUC BerkeleyLearns a visual planner and a whole-body tracking controller for humanoid loco-manipulation directly from human demonstrations without robot-in-the-loop retargeting. The system executes tasks like box sorting, catching thrown objects, and climbing a suitcase on a Unitree G1, and shows robustness to human disturbances and across different humanoid embodiments.Link
On-Demand Robotic Assembly via Differentiable Geometric Part RepairETH ZurichPresents an end-to-end pipeline that converts language prompts into bespoke wooden assemblies and automatically repairs part geometries for robotic assemblability via differentiable optimization. A graph-attention surrogate enables gradient-based geometric corrections, achieving high screwdriving feasibility and demonstrating dual-UR5e assembly for a variety of AI-generated designs.Link
Making Task Abstractions Executable: Control-Aware Layout Repair for a Fixed ControllerCarnegie Mellon UniversityFormulates control-aware repair of task layouts as affine constraint satisfaction that preserves event structure while modifying only continuous coordinates. A most-violated-row update with a separate quadratic projector yields certified layout repairs enabling fixed controllers to execute entire task sequences successfully in simulation.Link
Immiscible Diffusion Policy: Preserving Multimodal Robot Actions through Label-Free Noise AssignmentUC BerkeleyAnalyzes why diffusion policies often collapse to a single action mode and attributes this to noisy mixing of diffusion paths. Proposes a label-free noise-assignment strategy that preserves distinct noise-to-action routes, substantially improving recovery of minority action modes across simulated and real humanoid manipulation tasks without changing inference-time architecture.Link
Embedded Evaluation of Task Admission Coalescing in Decentralized Multi-Robot SystemsUC San DiegoEmpirically studies batching of task admissions for several decentralized MRTA algorithms on embedded and desktop hardware. Shows that coalescing can greatly reduce computation and even latency on constrained processors, with tradeoffs depending on task arrival rate, allocator design, and platform capabilities.Link
LASER: Latent Space Adjoint Matching for Support-Constrained Entropy-Regularized Offline RLMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyIntroduces LASER, an offline RL algorithm that performs entropy-regularized policy optimization in the latent space of a flow-matched behavior model, avoiding backpropagation through time via latent adjoint matching. On 40 OGBench tasks, it attains state-of-the-art performance with fixed hyperparameters, improving robustness to dataset quality variations.Link
