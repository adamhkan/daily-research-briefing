Executive Summary

  • Strong emphasis on advanced motion planning and navigation, from neural planners (NeurRAFT) to iterative-learning and trusted convex action sets (VIP, PAS).

  • Institution-aligned work from MIT, Stanford, CMU, ETH Zurich, UC Berkeley pushes tactile robustness, world-model-based control, VLA adaptation, and latency-aware RL finetuning.
  • Surgical and dental robotics, underwater perception, and maritime navigation illustrate broader application domains for high-precision, constrained motion planning.

A Durable Vision-Based Tactile Fingertip for Robotic ManipulationMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyIntroduces a vision-based tactile fingertip with a protective TPU film and replaceable sensing cartridge to withstand abrasion and concentrated loading. Accelerated tests show durability over two orders of magnitude higher than GelSight Mini and DIGIT, with gradual degradation and fast cartridge replacement enabling practical deployment in demanding manipulation tasks.Link
TrAct: Bridging Robot Control and Visual Prediction with Visual TracksStanford UniversityProposes TrAct, a decision-making framework where a VLA generates both actions and visual tracks, a track-conditioned world model predicts visual futures, and a vision-language reward model selects the best candidate. Visual tracks serve as an embodiment-agnostic interface between control and prediction, doubling simulated success rates and substantially improving real Franka manipulation over a strong VLA baseline.Link
Hierarchical Skill Retrieval for Data-Efficient Adaptation of Vision-Language-Action ModelsCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces Hierarchical Skill Retrieval (HSR) to adapt VLAs to new manipulation tasks by decomposing tasks into skills and retrieving semantically and behaviorally compatible demonstrations. A two-stage pretrain–finetune pipeline leveraging skill-level retrieval yields sizable gains in LIBERO and real-world tasks, improving data efficiency for long-horizon manipulation.Link
Learning to Act While Waiting: RL Finetuning of Generalist Robot Policies Under Inference LatencyETH Zurich; UC BerkeleyAddresses RL finetuning of large generalist policies under significant inference latency via the ARLI framework, which augments state with committed actions and mid-inference observations. This restores approximate Markov structure for asynchronous control, enabling effective policy improvement in both simulated and real manipulation settings where standard RL fails.Link
Fiber Bragg Grating Whiskers for Bioinspired Hydrodynamic Perception on Underwater RobotsStanford UniversityDevelops FBG-based whisker sensors with seal-inspired geometry that provide monotonic flow response, reduced self-oscillation, and strong angle dependence. Experiments demonstrate vortex-trail detection and enable an underwater robot to choose flow-following actions using whisker signals alone, suggesting compact hydrodynamic sensing for underwater navigation and tracking.Link
Interpreting Control Latents for System Identification via Conditional Flow MatchingUC BerkeleyDecodes latent variables of an adaptive quadrotor controller into distributions over physical models using conditional flow matching, without changing the policy. The resulting model ensembles support online gain tuning and robustness analysis, improving tracking under perturbed dynamics and providing interpretable structure for previously opaque control latents.Link

VIP: Variation-based Iterative-learning Planning for Robotic NavigationBeihang UniversityPresents VIP, a motion planning framework that directly optimizes a continuous command function in an infinite-dimensional space, avoiding large discrete trajectory parameterizations. The method scales as O(n) per iteration and supports both single robots and swarms, achieving efficient, iteratively improving plans in complex environments with limited onboard compute.Link
NeuralParker: A Reinforcement Learning Planner for Irregular Parking EnvironmentsIntroduces NeuralParker, an RL-based hybrid planner that encodes full-environment geometry in a target-relative vertex representation for arbitrary-pose parking. By combining learned arc policies with a terminal ensemble of cubic connections, it outperforms baselines on long-range and factorial parking benchmarks and transfers to a real delivery vehicle at low computational cost.Link
NeurRAFT: Robot Motion Planning via Anchor-Level Flow Matching with Clearance-Aware Preference TuningTexas A&M UniversityNeurRAFT is a generative motion planner that predicts compact anchor waypoints via flow matching, then interpolates smooth trajectories while weighting anchors by task-space impact. A clearance-aware preference-tuning step reshapes the distribution toward safer paths, significantly reducing collisions in cluttered settings and transferring zero-shot to a Franka arm under noisy depth sensing.Link
Safety-aware Model Predictive Path Integral Control with Signal Temporal LogicUniversity of Southern CaliforniaCombines Signal Temporal Logic with Control Barrier Functions inside an MPPI sampling-based controller to enforce complex temporal safety specifications. The resulting safety-aware-stl-mppi framework runs efficiently in parallel, achieving high safety and task performance in challenging Mars rover scenarios and demonstrating feasibility on a quadcopter in Isaac Lab.Link
Trusted Polytopic Action Sets for Fast Planning in Underactuated SystemsExtends polytopic action sets to underactuated, nonlinear systems by building local finite-dimensional action coordinates around nominal trajectories and inflating trusted convex regions in action space. These reusable sets enable rapid linear-program-based planning with nonlinear fidelity, yielding 14–78× speedups and improved terminal accuracy over kinodynamic RRT and NLP baselines.Link
One-Shot Learning from Demonstration of Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation by Identifying Physical InteractionsProposes an LfD framework that explicitly segments and models physical interaction events (contact making/breaking) and uses a hybrid position–force controller driven by demonstration-derived transition conditions. The method executes complex, sequential, contact-rich tasks such as doors, locks, and screwing from a single demonstration, handling geometric variability and online adaptation without prior task knowledge.Link
Gripper-aware Vision Language Action ModelsIntroduces MiGA, a multi-gripper dataset with over 100k demonstrations across five gripper types, capturing embodiment-dependent grasping strategies. The proposed GVLA model uses gripper tokenization and adapter-based policy routing to learn structured, gripper-conditioned behaviors, improving performance, zero-shot generalization, and gripper adaptation over standard VLA baselines.Link
Trajectory-Level Continuous Action Representation for Robotic ManipulationCAT encodes actions as continuous latent trajectories over fixed time intervals with frequency-aware positional encoding, decoupling representation from control rate. This avoids redundancy at high sampling frequencies and fixed temporal grids, yielding higher success across diverse backbones and control frequencies on LIBERO, MimicGen, and long-horizon real-world manipulation tasks.Link
