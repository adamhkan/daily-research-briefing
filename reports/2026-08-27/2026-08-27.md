| A Durable Vision-Based Tactile Fingertip for Robotic Manipulation | Massachusetts Institute of Technology | Introduces a vision-based tactile fingertip with a protective TPU film and replaceable sensing cartridge to withstand abrasion and concentrated loading. | Hierarchical Skill Retrieval for Data-Efficient Adaptation of Vision-Language-Action Models | Carnegie Mellon University | Introduces Hierarchical Skill Retrieval (HSR) to adapt VLAs to new manipulation tasks by decomposing tasks into skills and retrieving semantically and behaviorally compatible demonstrations. | Learning to Act While Waiting: RL Finetuning of Generalist Robot Policies Under Inference Latency | ETH Zurich; UC Berkeley | Addresses RL finetuning of large generalist policies under significant inference latency via the ARLI framework, which augments state with committed actions and mid-inference observations. This restores approximate Markov structure for asynchronous control, enabling effective policy improvement in both simulated and real manipulation settings where standard RL fails. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23831 |
| Fiber Bragg Grating Whiskers for Bioinspired Hydrodynamic Perception on Underwater Robots | Stanford University | Develops FBG-based whisker sensors with seal-inspired geometry that provide monotonic flow response, reduced self-oscillation, and strong angle dependence. Experiments demonstrate vortex-trail detection and enable an underwater robot to choose flow-following actions using whisker signals alone, suggesting compact hydrodynamic sensing for underwater navigation and tracking. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24724 |
| Interpreting Control Latents for System Identification via Conditional Flow Matching | UC Berkeley | Decodes latent variables of an adaptive quadrotor controller into distributions over physical models using conditional flow matching, without changing the policy. The resulting model ensembles support online gain tuning and robustness analysis, improving tracking under perturbed dynamics and providing interpretable structure for previously opaque control latents. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23887 |
