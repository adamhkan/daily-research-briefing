Executive Summary

  • Carnegie Mellon highlights: ModAR advances world-action modeling with multi-modal future prediction, while ManiSkillFormer introduces a neuro-symbolic, demo-free pipeline for compositional manipulation using geometric contracts.
  • NYU proposes an adapter-bank architecture where low-rank perturbations of a shared recurrent core act as compositional motor options, enabling novel movement sequences with strong generalization.
  • Whole-body humanoid control and dexterous manipulation see rapid progress, from learning loco-manipulation policies (Weave, WholeBodyWAM) to geometry-aware collision avoidance (RECAL) and constraint-conditioned dexterous grasp synthesis (ConGraspXL).
  • New motion planning and safety methods span high-dimensional sampling (HAR-RRT hybrids), hybrid-systems HJ reachability with safety guarantees, quadrotor escape-aware control barriers, and learning-based UAV path planning under complex constraints.
  • Navigation and SLAM robustness are pushed by video-based planning for navigation (CueNav), continual traversability prediction, ToF-inertial odometry for nano-UAVs (TIO-Former), and human-inspired semantic place recognition (HuMemSLAM).
  • Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation gain resilience and sensing via residual fault adaptation for in-hand manipulation, proximity-based interaction dynamics (ProxiDex), unified visuo-tactile policies from human video (UniDex-ViTac), and teleoperation with tactile-informed force control (XRoboToolKit-T).

Modality-Autoregressive World-Action ModelsCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces ModAR, a world-action model that autoregressively denoises multiple future modalities (e.g., depth, point tracks, DINO features) before predicting actions, allowing each to condition on previously generated signals. Experiments show that incorporating non-RGB geometric/semantic modalities improves manipulation performance, with ModAR outperforming prior WAMs and approaching Flex-π performance at ~20× lower training FLOPs, including on real bimanual tasks.Link
ManiSkillFormer: Demonstration-Free Compositional Manipulation via Task-Conditioned Geometric ContractsCarnegie Mellon UniversityProposes ManiSkillFormer, a neuro‑symbolic framework where manipulation skills are defined via task-conditioned geometric contracts (keypoints, normals) that mediate between perception and reusable motion templates. LLMs generate contracts and templates, enabling demonstration-free, compositional dual-arm manipulation with strong zero-shot performance across diverse objects, functional tasks, and long-horizon sequences.Link
Learning Options for Compositional Motor Control with Adapter BanksNew York UniversityImplements a motor-control architecture where a shared recurrent core is modulated by a bank of residual adapters, each acting as a low-rank perturbation selected by a discrete latent code. Trained on biomechanical control tasks, the system learns compositional motor primitives that can be sequenced by a high-level policy to generate novel movements, substantially improving generalization over multitask baselines.Link
A Programmable Optics Cloud LaboratoryMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyPresents PICO, a robotic cloud-laboratory architecture for programmable, reconfigurable free-space optics experiments. A domain-specific software layer abstracts physical optics setups, enabling remote interactive use, scripting, autonomous routines, and versioned experiment configurations on a shared robotic platform.Link

Motion planning in high dimensional spaces hybridizing RRT and HAR via position-direction decouplingProposes hybrid sampling-based planners that decouple the choice of extension point from extension direction, unifying RRT and Hit-and-Run (HAR) as special cases and introducing sparse-move strategies where only a subset of robots moves per step. The resulting HARF planner solves cluttered multi-robot motion planning problems with up to 384 DoF, achieving up to 100× speedups over classical RRT in difficult instances.Link
Optimized Wrench Polytope Analysis for Real-Time Stability Control of Legged Robots in Complex Multi-Contact ConfigurationsDevelops an optimized algorithm to compute the full actuatable wrench polytope for arbitrary multi-contact configurations on legged robots at ~49 Hz, enabling inclusion in real-time control loops. The resulting stability controller is validated in simulation and hardware, maintaining balance in challenging environments such as slopes and scaffolding that are difficult for existing controllers.Link
IL-ACT: Imitation Learning with Adaptive Cartesian Tracking Control for a 30-ton ExcavatorIntroduces IL-ACT, combining an imitation-learned joint-rate policy with adaptive Cartesian feedback and a stopping-distance governor for precise motion control of a 30-ton excavator. Simscape evaluations over varied trajectories and conditions show complete task success and reduced tracking error versus teacher and PID baselines, with theoretical bounds on adaptive states and feedback.Link
Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability for Hybrid Systems: Unified Goal-Driven Control with Safety GuaranteesExtends Hamilton-Jacobi reachability to hybrid systems with discrete modes and continuous dynamics, computing value functions that define safe sets under control limits and uncertainty. Builds hybrid least-restrictive safety filters and backward reach-avoid tubes that jointly guarantee safety and goal reachability, validated on simulated and real quadruped experiments.Link
Escape-Aware Control Barrier Functions for Quadrotor Safety under Body-Rate LimitsAnalyzes the gap between state-only control barrier functions and true safety when thrust reorientation is rate-limited on quadrotors, deriving a closed-form characterization of the certification gap. Proposes an escape-aware barrier defined over state and previous input, yielding larger safety margins and zero observed unsafe escapes across hundreds of simulated episodes compared to standard and backup-CBF baselines.Link
WholeBodyWAM: Generalizing Pre-trained World-Action Priors to Humanoid Loco-Manipulation via WBC-Grounded CoordinationExtends world-action models from arm-centric settings to humanoid loco-manipulation by jointly predicting visual dynamics, manipulation actions, and whole-body control intents. By grounding pre-trained priors in heterogeneous whole-body controllers rather than relearning from scratch, WholeBodyWAM attains ~92% simulation success and smoother performance across controllers in real humanoid tasks.Link
Weave: Learning Whole-Body Dexterous Loco-Manipulation from Human-Object InteractionsThe Chinese University of Hong KongPresents Weave, which converts human-object interaction demonstrations into robot-executable trajectories and trains a unified policy controlling 29 body and 12 finger joints for humanoid loco-manipulation. Achieves high success across nine objects, including zero-shot performance on unseen interaction sequences, and releases ~23 hours of physically executed rollouts with contact labels.Link
Collision-Aware Humanoid Whole-Body Control under Imperfect Tracking TargetsIntroduces RECAL, a robot–environment cross-attention layer that augments standard whole-body controllers with point-cloud-based collision awareness, trading off target tracking against geometric safety. Demonstrated in simulation and on a Digit V3 humanoid, RECAL reduces collisions for locomotion and manipulation tasks, including with carried objects, while preserving tracking performance.Link
Fingers as Legs: Learning Self-Supported Locomotion and Manipulation with an Anthropomorphic HandShows that an anthropomorphic robotic hand can serve as a compact mobile manipulator by using its fingers for both locomotion and manipulation. Reinforcement-learned policies enable crawling, steering, fall recovery, and object interaction such as keyboard input and pushing tasks, on a self-contained platform with onboard power and compute.Link
SWIM: Vision-Language-Grounded Soft Whole-Body Interactive ManipulationNanyang Technological UniversityProposes SWIM, a vision-language-action framework that maps RGB observations and language instructions to actuation sequences for a tendon-driven soft continuum robot. A diffusion-based action head and Visual Soft Proprioception jointly encode body geometry, enabling high success in packing, reaching, and grasping tasks in simulation and hardware, outperforming adapted VLA baselines.Link
