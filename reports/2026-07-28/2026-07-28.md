## Executive Summary

- Navigation and planning see advances from a large-scale multicultural social-navigation dataset, robust chance-constrained planning under unknown dynamics, and efficient priors for partially observable path planning.
- Onboard and resource-constrained navigation is pushed forward via fully offline vision-language navigation for outdoor robots and flight-ready LiDAR-inertial odometry for embedded UAVs.
- Manipulation-focused work spans multi-arm policy execution, sample-efficient bimanual coordination in dynamic environments, gradient-free diffusion policy guidance, and modular variable-impedance control.
- World models continue to mature for manipulation and medical robotics, from robot-factored video prediction and visuo-tactile rollouts to action-conditioned ultrasound probe guidance.
- Generalist and learning-centric methods improve orchestration, action tokenization, and progress reward modeling, enhancing the usability of large visuomotor and VLA policies in complex tasks.
- Whole-body and tactile interaction are addressed through humanoid skill learning from synthetic videos and evaluated withdrawal reflexes for skin-covered robot arms.
