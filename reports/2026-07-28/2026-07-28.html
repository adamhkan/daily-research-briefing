Executive Summary

  • Navigation and planning see advances from a large-scale multicultural social-navigation dataset, robust chance-constrained planning under unknown dynamics, and efficient priors for partially observable path planning.
  • Onboard and resource-constrained navigation is pushed forward via fully offline vision-language navigation for outdoor robots and flight-ready LiDAR-inertial odometry for embedded UAVs.
  • Manipulation-focused work spans multi-arm policy execution, sample-efficient bimanual coordination in dynamic environments, gradient-free diffusion policy guidance, and modular variable-impedance control.
  • World models continue to mature for manipulation and medical robotics, from robot-factored video prediction and visuo-tactile rollouts to action-conditioned ultrasound probe guidance.
  • Generalist and learning-centric methods improve orchestration, action tokenization, and progress reward modeling, enhancing the usability of large visuomotor and VLA policies in complex tasks.
  • Whole-body and tactile interaction are addressed through humanoid skill learning from synthetic videos and evaluated withdrawal reflexes for skin-covered robot arms.

Conformal Constraint Tightening for Chance-Constrained Motion Planning with Unknown DynamicsArizona State UniversityThis work introduces a planner-agnostic constraint-tightening method that uses conformal prediction to bound deviations between nominal and true system trajectories under unknown dynamics. By tightening constraints in the nominal planning problem, it provides probabilistic task-completion guarantees on the true system and shows improved success rates over nominal-model planners.Link
Offline Vision-Language Navigation with Geometric Goal Localization for Outdoor EnvironmentsThe paper benchmarks 17 edge-deployable small language models for decomposing navigation instructions and proposes a hybrid semantic–geometric goal localization pipeline combining open-vocabulary detection, segmentation, and LiDAR. Integrated into the Edge-BehAV system, it enables fully onboard, cloud-independent outdoor navigation with order-of-magnitude latency gains and substantially reduced goal localization error.Link
Safe Learning Predictive Control for Ego-World Robotic SystemsUniversity of BolognaSOWL-MPC is a learning-based MPC framework for safe navigation in multi-robot settings where the other robot’s policy is unknown. It uses sparse variational Gaussian processes with online variational conditioning to infer the latent world policy from noisy states and propagates this uncertainty through nonlinear dynamics to enable provably safe receding-horizon control.Link
Learning Spatiotemporal Decision Priors for Efficient Path Planning under Partial ObservabilityImiPath learns reusable spatiotemporal decision priors from demonstration trajectories to guide planners under local sensing. A spatiotemporal-attention policy network produces directional preferences that bias heterogeneous planners, reducing redundant node expansions and improving path efficiency, with validation including experiments on a magnetic microrobot platform.Link
One Hand Watches The Other: Dynamic Multi-Agent Cooperation for Sample-Efficient Bimanual Manipulation in Dynamic EnvironmentsUniversity of FreiburgDynaMAC reframes each arm in a bimanual or dynamic manipulation setting as a dynamic task parameter for the other, enabling multi-stream policies to handle moving reference frames without strict leader–follower roles. On the DynaBench benchmark, it achieves over 35-point performance gains while using 20× fewer samples and generalizes from static demonstrations to dynamic scenarios.Link
Embodying Multi-Hand Manipulation Policies by Searching the Assignment and Null SpacesThis work presents a search-based framework for mapping abstract multi-hand trajectories from learned policies onto physical multi-arm robots. By jointly searching over hand-to-arm assignments and manipulators’ Jacobian null spaces using Conflict-Based Search, it achieves theoretically complete, collision-aware embodiment of coordinated manipulation policies.Link
GRACE: Gradient-Free Robot Action Generation via Combined Diffusion-MPPI Posterior Mean EstimationGRACE couples pretrained diffusion policies with Model Predictive Path Integral control to enforce safety and task constraints using only forward cost evaluations. The method constructs a cost-conditioned posterior at each reverse step and estimates its mean via a single MPPI update, outperforming diffusion and sampling baselines and enabling obstacle-avoiding manipulation without differentiable costs.Link
Robot-Factored World Models via Robot RenderingThe authors propose robot-factored world models that separate action realization and robot geometry from the learned video model by rendering nominal robot trajectories via URDF into RGB/depth. This shared visual interface, including end-effector and scene depth, improves prediction of object responses to robot motion and enables generalization across robot embodiments and retargeting from human hand motion.Link
ViTacWorld: Scaling Visuo-Tactile World Models for Contact-Rich Robot ManipulationViTacWorld is an action-conditioned visuo-tactile world model trained on large-scale real and simulated tactile datasets to predict future visual and tactile feedback. It generates visuo-tactile rollouts for contact-rich tasks, both augmenting data to improve tactile manipulation policies and serving as a simulator to evaluate policies under controlled action sequences.Link
Plug, Play, and Comply: A Modular Framework for Online Variable Impedance with Arbitrarily Oriented Compliance AxesUniversity of Science and Technology of ChinaThis work extends ROS control with a plugin-based, robot-agnostic framework for joint- and Cartesian-space compliant control, including variable impedance and nullspace control interfaces. A reference Cartesian impedance controller supports online rotation of stiffness and damping axes based on task geometry, enabling task-dependent compliance in contact-rich manipulation and portability across arms.Link
