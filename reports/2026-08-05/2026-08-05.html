Executive Summary

  • Strong emphasis on motion planning and control across platforms, from SE(3) trajectory generation and Riemannian informed trees to stochastic safe navigation and multi-agent dynamic games.
  • Multiple works push mobile and aerial navigation, including agile UAV world–action models, triangulation-aware VIO, stochastic temporal roadmaps, and reusable object-navigation memories.
  • Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation advances focus on tactile/VLA integration, cross-embodiment grasp synthesis, human-derived manipulability priors, and contact-aware visuo-tactile fusion.
  • Vision-language-action models continue to expand for mobile and dynamic manipulation, with trajectory-guided policies, panoramic or multi-camera perception, geometry-shaped representations, and analysis of failure modes.
  • Whole-body and humanoid control receives attention via rapid training for motion tracking, single-leg balance benchmarks, teleoperation and imitation systems, and composition of locomotion and manipulation skills.
  • Institution-aligned work includes MPC-based doorway traversal for mobile manipulators (MIT) and rapid online embodiment adaptation for quadrupedal locomotion (UC San Diego).

Motion Planning for Mobile Manipulators Navigating Doorways via Model Predictive ControlMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyIntroduces an MPC-based motion planning framework that treats the robot and door as a coupled dynamical system to autonomously open and traverse push/pull doors. Manipulation feasibility is enforced via penalty terms instead of explicit arm kinematics, yielding dynamically feasible, collision-free trajectories validated in simulation and hardware.Link
Rapid Embodiment Adaptation for Quadrupedal LocomotionUC San DiegoProposes an online embodiment adaptation module that infers hardware changes, such as joint range constraints and trunk-mass variations, from short interaction histories and conditions a generalist locomotion policy on this estimate. In both simulation and real-world tests on a Unitree Go2, the method maintains stable gait under severe degradation where non-adaptive baselines fail.Link

RIT*: Riemannian Informed Trees for Cost-Adaptive Optimal Motion PlanningReplaces Euclidean primitives in batch informed trees with Riemannian counterparts to better handle anisotropic and spatially varying cost metrics in high-dimensional spaces. The framework also learns a collision-adaptive metric online, substantially reducing path cost versus BIT* in 6-DOF and 14-DOF manipulation benchmarks.Link
Learning Smooth SE(3) Trajectories under Left-Invariant Riemannian MetricsUniversity of PennsylvaniaPresents a neural framework for generating higher-order smooth rigid-body trajectories on SE(3) under general left-invariant Riemannian metrics. By learning polynomial body-twist parameters conditioned on the metric, it approximates optimal variational solutions in milliseconds and is demonstrated on motion primitives and quadrotor flight refinement.Link
Complete Motion Planning using Workspace-Fibered Decomposition for nR-Planar ManipulatorIntroduces a workspace-fibered decomposition that plans for redundant planar manipulators via obstacle-constrained reachable workspaces of sub-chains and recursive lifting through orientation fibers. This preserves collision-free connectivity while avoiding explicit full C-space obstacle construction, reducing collision-checking complexity.Link
Learning-Based Motion Planning for Dynamic Environments: From Foundational Algorithms to Emerging ParadigmsChinese Academy of Sciences; Shanghai Jiao Tong University; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences; University of Connecticut; University of Science and Technology of ChinaSurveys motion planning in dynamic environments with an emphasis on how learning augments classical planners. Proposes a taxonomy spanning direct policy learning, learning-augmented planning, hybrids, and training enhancements, and outlines open challenges in safety, sim-to-real transfer, dense crowds, and perception–planning coupling.Link
DreamTrajectory: Trajectory-Guided Action Generation with World Model Alignment for Mobile ManipulationCombines intention-level end-effector trajectory prediction with action-chunk generation to better coordinate base–arm motion in mobile manipulation. A lightweight trajectory world model scores candidate chunks against the intended path, improving success rates substantially in simulation and on real mobile manipulation tasks.Link
Learning Panorama-Aware VLA for Mobile Manipulation with Whole-Body TeleoperationBeihang University; Johns Hopkins UniversityDevelops a VR-based whole-body teleoperation system and PanoVLA, a Mixture-of-Transformers VLA policy that encodes panoramic observations for bimanual mobile manipulation. The panoramic fusion significantly boosts stage and end-to-end task success over local-view baselines across real-world mobile manipulation tasks.Link
TANGO-VIO: Triangulation-Aware Navigation with Guaranteed Feature-Observability for Visual-Inertial OdometryIntroduces a triangulation-aware navigation framework that embeds a feature observability metric into a control barrier function to maintain good parallax for VIO. The method corrects velocity commands to keep the log-determinant information metric above a threshold, improving triangulation conditioning in both simulated and real flights.Link
FlowPilot: Real-Time World-Action Modeling for Agile UAV NavigationShanghai Jiao Tong University; The University of Hong KongPresents a compact world–action model that jointly denoises future depth and trajectories using flow matching, coupling video and action experts. With Bernstein-polynomial action parameterization, the onboard system generates smooth, trackable references in under 18 ms and achieves fast, clutter-robust UAV navigation using only depth.Link
StochSIPP: Safe Interval Path Planning in Stochastic Dynamic EnvironmentsExtends Safe Interval Path Planning to stochastic temporal roadmaps by planning macro-actions over an AND/OR action–observation graph of uncertain edge and vertex statuses. Under mild assumptions, the policy is provably collision-free and minimizes expected arrival time; experiments show improved arrival times and feasibility in gated scenarios.Link
A Forward-Inverse Dynamic Game Framework for Enhanced Multi-Agent Trajectory PlanningZhejiang UniversityFormulates multi-agent trajectory planning as a KL-regularized dynamic game with state-dependent weighting to capture bounded rationality and varying interaction strength. A context-aware inverse game infers agent objectives from demonstrations, leading to effective feedback Nash strategies validated in cooperative navigation and merging experiments.Link
