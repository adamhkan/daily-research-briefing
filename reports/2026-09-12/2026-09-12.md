## Executive Summary

- Dexterous manipulation advances include real-time Jacobian-based in-hand pen writing and new exoskeleton-based data collection for contact-rich hand tasks.
- VLA and world-action modeling papers push long-horizon, memory- and safety-aware manipulation control, with faster, safer, and more steerable action heads.
- New planning and kinematics methods address constrained manipulators and closed-chain mechanisms, improving trajectory optimization with general-purpose IK.
- Navigation and locomotion work spans swarm platforms, valuation-aware hazardous exploration, robust humanoid and quadruped control, and low-cost or degraded-vision depth/SLAM.
- Perception for autonomy is strengthened by relocalization-informed depth from 3DGS, radar- and event-based sensing, and monocular Visual-SLAM tailored for agriculture.
- Framework papers propose unified runtimes and interfaces for quadruped inspection and end-user sketch programming of swarms, emphasizing deployability and usability.

| Rapid Learning of Dexterous In-Hand Pen Writing through Real-Time Jacobian Estimation | ETH Zurich | Introduces an embodied controller that estimates the task Jacobian of the hand–object system online, enabling an anthropomorphic hand to perform in-hand pen writing after ~18 s of initialization, without analytic models, simulation, or demonstrations.
