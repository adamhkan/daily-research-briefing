Executive Summary

  • Dexterous manipulation advances include real-time Jacobian-based in-hand pen writing and new exoskeleton-based data collection for contact-rich hand tasks.
  • VLA and world-action modeling papers push long-horizon, memory- and safety-aware manipulation control, with faster, safer, and more steerable action heads.
  • New planning and kinematics methods address constrained manipulators and closed-chain mechanisms, improving trajectory optimization with general-purpose IK.

  • Framework papers propose unified runtimes and interfaces for quadruped inspection and end-user sketch programming of swarms, emphasizing deployability and usability.

Rapid Learning of Dexterous In-Hand Pen Writing through Real-Time Jacobian EstimationETH ZurichIntroduces an embodied controller that estimates the task Jacobian of the hand–object system online, enabling an anthropomorphic hand to perform in-hand pen writing after ~18 s of initialization, without analytic models, simulation, or demonstrations. The same formulation transfers across three hand embodiments and achieves sub-millimeter accuracy on 3D pen trajectories, offering a lightweight alternative to RL/IL for dexterous in-hand manipulation.Link
RIDE: Relocalization-Informed Depth Estimation with 3D Gaussian SplattingCarnegie Mellon UniversityProposes RIDE, which fuses sparse metric depths from render–match–PnP relocalization against a metrically scaled 3DGS map with a video-depth prior to produce dense, temporally consistent metric depth from RGB. Global/local correction and temporal memory handle intermittent observations, improving depth accuracy and consistency for robot perception without task-specific fine-tuning.Link

SEED-UMI: Sharing the Exoskeleton between human and robot for onE-to-one Dexterous demonstrationPeking UniversityPresents SEED-UMI, where a single exoskeleton is physically shared by human and robot so joint encoders and wrist-mounted cameras provide a common embodiment-centric observation space. This paired supervision enables policies to learn directly from raw exoskeleton images for contact-rich dexterous tasks, tripling data-collection efficiency over teleoperation and achieving 70% average success.Link
Morphology-Aware Human Motion Retargeting for Wheeled-Humanoid Loco-ManipulationHarbin Institute of TechnologyBuilds a pipeline to retarget SMPLX human motion onto a wheeled humanoid that lacks legs, redistributing lower-body motion into planar base and torso while preserving manipulation-relevant arm kinematics. A reference-twist planner and learned BaseDecode policy in simulation yield executable loco-manipulation behaviors for the Galaxea R1 Pro under realistic actuation limits.Link
Contact-Aware Incremental Model Predictive Control for an Underactuated Aerial ManipulatorCombines NMPC for precise end-effector pose and normal-force tracking with whole-body incremental nonlinear dynamic inversion on a quadrotor with a simple 1-DoF arm. Experiments on aerial writing across surfaces, forces, friction, and wind show robust 5-DoF pose and force control without fully actuated platforms or dedicated force sensing.Link
Memory as Plans: World-Action Modeling with Memory-Grounded PlanningHarbin Institute of TechnologyMaP-WAM reframes memory for non-Markovian manipulation as planning-time episodic context: past segments are stored as multimodal records, converted into compact language-plus-visual plans that condition a fixed-context executor. A World-Action-Progress model predicts action chunks and execution progress, achieving strong RMBench and real-robot performance with near-constant inference latency over long histories.Link
2AM: Grounding Agent-Side Memory as Guidance for Steerable Action Models in Long-Horizon ManipulationKU LeuvenSeparates memory and control by making a multimodal Agent the sole memory holder and a stateless RGB-only Action Model the executor for manipulation. The Agent emits language and 2D spatial hints that steer the VLA, and training with noisy, dropped hints teaches robustness; on LIBERO-Mem, 2AM vastly outperforms prior long-horizon baselines without extra geometry or planners.Link
Beyond Noise Steering: Dual-Latent Space Reinforcement Learning for Generative Robot PolicyIntroduces DLSRL, which controls generative action policies via two latents: one for initial noise and one injected into intermediate token representations through adapters. This dual modulation lets RL adjust action features without modifying the frozen generator, improving adaptation speed and performance on diverse manipulation tasks across generative architectures.Link
LTLDiff: Finite Linear Temporal Logic-Guided Data Generation and Diffusion Policies for Multi-agent Robotic ManipulationUniversity of TorontoLTLDiff learns LTLf task specifications from natural language and embeds them via abstract syntax trees to condition both demonstration generation and diffusion-based multi-agent policies. The logic-conditioned diffusion encourages temporally ordered, coordinated behaviors, yielding higher success rates on multi-arm manipulation tasks requiring precise sequencing and synchronization.Link
ObstaDiff: Generalizable Diffusion Policy Learning via Obstacle-aware RepresentationsUniversity of California, Los AngelesProposes ObstaDiff, which decomposes diffusion policy learning into an obstacle-aware encoder that separates target, obstacles, and background, and a downstream alignment policy that aims for target-relative bottleneck poses. In real greenhouse picking tests, it significantly improves task success and reduces collisions versus standard imitation-learning baselines in clutter.Link
IMLE-VLA: Fast Single-Step Action Generation for Vision-Language-Action PoliciesSimon Fraser University; University of PennsylvaniaReplaces iterative diffusion/flow action heads in VLAs with a single-step conditional IMLE generator that maintains multimodal action coverage. Applied to π₀.₅, IMLE-VLA boosts control frequency and smoothness, achieves state-of-the-art success on LIBERO, and improves real-robot performance and robustness without sacrificing generalization.Link
Planning along Differentiable Charts of Constraint Manifolds with General-Purpose IK SolversShows how to obtain gradients of analytic IK parameterizations from standard forward-kinematics Jacobians via the inverse function theorem, making meta-solver IK (e.g., IKFast) usable in gradient-based trajectory optimization under constraints. A least-squares domain extension and smooth reachability constraint maintain useful gradients outside reach, validated in numerical and hardware box-manipulation tasks.Link
