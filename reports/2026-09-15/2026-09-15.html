Executive Summary

  • Foundation-model work focuses on aligning task and motion planning data with pretrained VLA distributions and on breaking vision–action shortcuts, directly targeting generalizable manipulation and mobile-manipulation policies.
  • Multiple papers advance diffusion and world-model-based policies (for rods, deformable linear objects, humanoid traversal, and DIA for fine-tuning), emphasizing long-horizon credit assignment and material-aware control.
  • Navigation and motion planning see new methods for SE(3) trajectory optimization, context-aware MPC for racing, coverage planning, and battery-aware multirotor planning, along with practical tuning studies for ROS2 costmaps and LiDAR-inertial odometry.
  • Safety, robustness, and SLAM backend reliability are addressed via neural control barrier functions, multi-session LiDAR SLAM, and distributed stochastic optimal control for swarms under uncertainty.
  • Manipulation-focused contributions span dexterous in-hand and bimanual control, deformable and cloth manipulation, underwater and coarse grippers, and MagLev-based grasping, supported by large synthetic datasets and geometric priors.
  • Institution-matched work highlights MIT’s communication-aware multi-robot exploration, Stanford’s distribution-aligned TAMP for data generation, UCSD’s soft “pneumatic neuron” controllers, and NYU’s globally consistent multi-session LiDAR SLAM backend.

Communication-Constrained Multi-Robot Exploration With Adaptive Communication WindowsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyMACE formulates when and where to rendezvous for communication as a vehicle orienteering problem, trading off exploration gain versus travel to communication points. By actively scheduling and valuing communication windows, it reduces total multi-robot exploration time by up to 23% compared to opportunistic or fixed-rendezvous strategies in simulation.Link
DATAFARM: Distribution-Aligned Task and Motion Planning for Fine-Tuning Vision-Language-Action ModelsStanford UniversityDATAFARM modifies TAMP-generated demonstrations so that robot joint configurations, motion style, and timing better match the data distribution used to pretrain VLA models. This alignment lets TAMP-produced trajectories substantially improve fine-tuning performance, approaching human teleoperation success while retaining strong performance on out-of-distribution deformable tasks.Link
Pneumatic neurons for soft robots enable inflate-and-fire networks for rhythmic motionUC San DiegoThe work introduces “Pneu-rons,” soft modules that integrate actuation, logic, and energy conversion, implementing an inflate-and-fire analogue of leaky integrate-and-fire neurons. When arranged in excitatory–inhibitory networks, these elements generate self-sustained, material-level oscillations that can drive adaptive, electronics-free rhythmic locomotion in soft robots.Link
Chain-SLAM: Globally Consistent Backend for Multi-Session LiDAR SLAM via Chained Loop ClosureNew York UniversityChain-SLAM proposes a chained loop-closure scheme that propagates constraints across inter-session keyframes through an adjacency graph, enabling consistent alignment of large multi-session LiDAR maps. Initialized via GNSS-based place recognition and optimized in a unified factor graph, it achieves improved trajectory accuracy and robust cross-session integration without heavy parameter tuning.Link

MoPA: Coordinated Mobile Manipulation via Subsystem-Specific Perception AlignmentMoPA separates perception for base and arm into dual masked visual streams, then couples them in a Mixture-of-Transformers decoder to generate coordinated base and arm action chunks. On ManiSkill-HAB and four real-world mobile manipulation tasks, it achieves state-of-the-art success, showing that subsystem-specific perception alignment improves long-horizon whole-body behavior.Link
STAR: Sparse Tactile Representation Learning in Vision-Tactile-Language-Action Models for Dexterous ManipulationSTAR introduces a 200-hour bimanual dexterous dataset with synchronized vision, tactile, and language, and a VTLA training recipe that sparsifies and predicts tactile tokens. Joint visual–tactile pretraining and sparse future tactile prediction yield a VLA policy achieving 61% average success across four real-robot dexterous tasks after modest post-training.Link
ArtManip: Category-Level Articulated In-Hand ManipulationZhejiang UniversityArtManip tackles in-hand manipulation of articulated objects at the category level via procedurally generated articulated models and functional grasps plus a two-stage RL policy. With articulation physics randomization, curriculum design, and latent distillation, the policy generalizes across unseen instances and grasps in simulation and transfers zero-shot to 12 real objects.Link
Global Path Planner with Multi-Model SwitchingThe planner combines a traversability graph, a heading-aware A* for feasible global paths, and a pure-pursuit controller that switches between kinematic models based on terrain and robot state. Validated on a quadruped and a quadrotor in simulation, the multi-model approach improves path efficiency, robustness, and energy use across diverse terrains.Link
Trajectory Bundle Method in SE(3) for Black-Box Fixed-Wing Aircraft Trajectory OptimizationBrigham Young UniversityThis work extends the Trajectory Bundle Method to SE(3), constructing bundles in the Lie algebra and propagating them through black-box rigid-body dynamics via exp/log maps. It provides error bounds on SE(3) interpolation and demonstrates derivative-free optimization of an acrobatic, collision-free fixed-wing maneuver through a rotated aperture with unknown dynamics.Link
Driving Context-guided Model Predictive Planning and Control for Autonomous Car Racing at the Limit and BeyondThe authors integrate a Model Predictive Planner and Controller under a Cost Blending state machine that switches context weights for overtaking, nominal driving, and countersteering. On a fully autonomous Superformula racecar, the system achieves lap times within 2% of a top human and robustly handles high-oversteer recovery and aggressive overtakes.Link
A Hierarchical Coverage Path Planning Algorithm for Unknown EnvironmentsUniversity of Science and Technology of ChinaThe proposed online coverage planner incrementally decomposes an unknown environment into subareas organized in a decomposition tree, maintaining an adaptive global coverage tour. A local planner covers each subarea while the tree prioritization balances exploration state and distance, yielding shorter paths and lower overlap than three baselines in simulation.Link
AnchorVLN: Geometry-Anchored Vision-Language Grounding Reasoning for Open-Vocabulary NavigationAnchorVLN enforces a separation where a VLM proposes semantic cues and geometric tools handle metric reasoning, exposed via an MCP server without metric-bearing APIs. On the CMU VLN Challenge 2026 tasks, geometric anchoring significantly improves instruction following and object-referencing accuracy over direct coordinate estimation while keeping the autonomy stack unchanged.Link
Battery-Aware Predictive Trajectory Planning and Control for Multirotors Under Disturbances.B is with department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at UniversityThe framework evaluates candidate multirotor trajectories using closed-loop simulations that include vehicle dynamics, motor models, and a reduced-order battery model, under spatially localized disturbances. By incorporating predicted energy use and battery-dependent actuator limits into planning, it reduces electrical energy consumption and tracking error relative to disturbance-aware baselines, especially at low state-of-charge.Link

They identify configuration regimes that are energy-efficient yet performant, underscoring that environment-specific costmap tuning materially impacts operational endurance of mobile robots.Link
