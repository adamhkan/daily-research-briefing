Executive Summary

  • Whole-body and task-aware control is advancing across domains, from humanoid object lifting to extreme off-road vehicle jumps and redundant cable-driven systems.
  • Safe and robust motion generation is being tackled with homotopy-aware corridor planning, decentralized multi-robot safety, and barrier-enhanced generative action models.
  • Manipulation research emphasizes semantic and physical consistency, including transparent labware grasping, affordance-guided grasp selection, and robustness to visual shifts.
  • New planning formulations improve navigation and viewpoint selection in challenging environments, including underwater multimodal dynamics, plant-leaf NBV planning, and vision-language navigation with agentic memory.
  • Multi-robot and VLM-based frameworks are enabling heterogeneous teams to execute language-specified tasks in unknown environments without centralized control.
