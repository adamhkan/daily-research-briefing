Executive Summary

  • Humanoid and legged loco-manipulation see strong progress, from keyframe-bridged VLM planning and RL whole-body control to adaptive MPC and large-scale traversal learning.
  • Motion planning advances span multi-robot TAMP optimality, cascade-aware collision risk, kinodynamic planning benchmarks, and LLM-generated MIP formulations for multi-modal planning.
  • Force-, contact-, and tactile-aware methods are increasingly central for dexterous and contact-rich manipulation, with new VLAs, correction policies, tactile priors, and grasp representations.
  • Navigation and trajectory planning in dynamic, cluttered, or adverse environments leverage richer risk models, social interaction reasoning, SLAM/mapping innovations, and UAV/quadrotor-specific planners.
  • Vision-language(-action) models permeate planning and control, guiding keyframes, route planning, world models, and adaptation policies while exposing new challenges in tokenization and real-time execution.
  • Institution-linked work from CMU, MIT, ETH Zurich, and NYU emphasizes active perception/mobile manipulation, language-grounded route planning, open soft-robot platforms, and reproducible manipulation kits.

KINO: A Keyframe Interface for VLM Planning and Whole-Body Control in Humanoid Loco-ManipulationETH ZurichIntroduces a hierarchical humanoid loco-manipulation system where a VLM selects task-relevant whole-body keyframes, which are then retargeted and tracked by a keyframe-conditioned RL controller. A saliency-based keyframe sampling strategy enables large performance gains, achieving up to 92% success on diverse one- and two-handed tasks with a Unitree G1.Link
ActiveScale: Scaling Active Perception for Robots across Model, Data, and HardwareCarnegie Mellon UniversityProposes an active perception framework for VLAs that augments policies with camera-pose-aware tokens, temporal video inputs, and a mid-training recipe using 1000 hours of egocentric/robotic data. A new mobile-manipulation platform (AMP) supports single-operator teleoperation for large-scale data collection, improving success on viewpoint-dependent manipulation tasks.Link
Map2Route: Benchmarking Compositional Language-Grounded Route Planning over Semantic MapsCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces Map2Route, a benchmark of 1,000 episodes testing compositional route planning over semantic maps with rich relational, preference, and constraint language. The accompanying Grounding2Route method converts language into executable code with verification-guided repair, outperforming adapted baselines but still lagging human performance.Link
SPROUT: The Open-Source Soft Growing Robot for Search and RescueMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyReleases a fully open soft growing robot system (SPROUT) designed with USAR practitioners for operation in dusty, wet, and confined collapsed-structure environments. Modular hardware and ROS 2 software support varied payloads and control strategies, with field demonstrations of inspection and traversal in first-responder training sites.Link
WetRobo: A Reproducible Robot Kit for Coding Agents in Biological LaboratoriesNew York UniversityPresents WetRobo, a standardized arm-and-labware kit plus code and demonstrations that allow coding agents to synthesize task programs from natural language without local teleoperation data. In two different labs, a Codex-based agent successfully executes common lab manipulation tasks and transfers better than a lab-specific VLA policy.Link
Not All Layers Need Tuning: Diagnosing and Directing Adaptation in Vision-Language-Action ModelsCarnegie Mellon UniversityAnalyzes where VLAs actually need adaptation under different deployment shifts by measuring per-region parameter displacement and proposing a diagnostic that predicts region importance from a handful of unlabeled target observations. A rank-based LoRA allocator then focuses capacity on key regions, matching or beating uniform adapters and, in real-robot tests, rivaling full fine-tuning with <0.1% of parameters.Link

Learning Holistic Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation with a Bipedal Mobile ManipulatorUniversity of Illinois Urbana-ChampaignTrains a unified RL-based whole-body controller that maps 6-DoF end-effector targets directly to coordinated arm and leg actions for a bipedal base, handling reaching, posture adaptation, and stepping without explicit gait commands. A reward-gating scheme and temporal context estimator yield a single policy usable with VR teleoperation, diffusion policies, or scripted goals on hardware.Link
Adaptive-MHE : A Sampling-Based Adaptive MPC for Legged Loco-Manipulation via Moving Horizon EstimationUniversity of CalgaryCombines sampling-based moving-horizon estimation with MPC to identify object and terrain parameters such as mass and friction online for legged loco-manipulation. The framework adapts control to changing conditions without differentiable dynamics, improving robustness over non-adaptive baselines and approaching performance with ground-truth parameters.Link
Learning Multi-Humanoid Pickup and Transport via Decentralized Object-Centric ControlDynamics Robotics and AI Lab (DRAIL), Oregon State University, Corvallis, OR, USA.Develops decentralized object-centric policies where each humanoid controls a local attachment region on a shared object, enabling gripperless bimanual pickup, cooperative transport, and handover. Policies trained on single-robot pickup generalize surprisingly well to multi-robot scenarios, with additional gains from explicit multi-humanoid training and successful sim-to-real demonstrations.Link
PASSAGE: Scaling Scene-Aligned Motion Learning for Perceptive Humanoid Traversal in Cluttered EnvironmentsUses 100 hours of VR-captured, scene-aligned human motion to train a conditional flow-matching planner and a whole-body tracker for humanoid traversal through clutter. The system runs fully onboard with LiDAR mapping and achieves over 70% contact-free success on unseen layouts, composing behaviors like stepping over, ducking, and squeezing past obstacles without skill annotations.Link
ElastiQP: An Always-Feasible QP Solver for Constrained Robot ControlIntroduces a dual active-set QP solver that analytically folds L1-penalized slack variables into inequality constraints, guaranteeing a usable solution even when the nominal QP is infeasible. On robot control benchmarks, it matches or exceeds state-of-the-art solvers on feasible problems and delivers valid, minimally violating controls up to 40× faster on infeasible instances, with open-source C++/Python/JAX implementations.Link
Asymptotically Optimal Multi-Robot Task and Motion PlanningNanyang Technological UniversityEstablishes sufficient conditions for asymptotic optimality in multi-robot TAMP where task transitions constrain different robot subsets and thus different subspaces. The proposed planner builds evolving single-robot roadmaps and performs implicit tensor-product search with conditional transition sampling and lazy collision checking, yielding asymptotically optimal multi-robot manipulation plans without constructing the full composite roadmap.Link
CaSCo: Cascade-Aware Soft-Collision Motion PlanningFormulates motion planning that minimizes semantic risk over both direct robot-object and induced object-object collisions using VLM-assigned object risks and physics simulation. Roadmap states are augmented with object arrangements and incurred-risk sets, and an optimal graph search with a cascade-relaxed heuristic finds paths that reason about collision cascades in cluttered manipulation scenes.Link
DynoFluxBench: Benchmarking Kinodynamic Space-Time Planners in Dynamic EnvironmentsIntroduces DynoFluxBench, a benchmark for kinodynamic planning in known dynamic environments with unbounded arrival time, and three new planners that fuse kinodynamic and space-time search. The methods are analyzed for probabilistic completeness and empirically compared, with ST-Db-RRT achieving up to 32× faster first-solution times while alternatives remain robust when trajectory optimization is fragile.Link
M$^3$P-R1: Reinforcement Learning for Large Language Model Guided Multi-Modal Motion Planning via MIP Code GenerationDuke University; Purdue UniversityUses RL fine-tuning of LLMs to synthesize executable MIP code for multi-modal motion planning problems that mix continuous dynamics and discrete mode switches. Rather than outputting trajectories directly, the model generates solver-ready Python programs that define variables, constraints, and objectives, learning effective discretizations and coupling constraints from solver feedback.Link
InterMASH: A Unified Geometric Representation for Grasp SynthesisUniversity of Science and Technology of ChinaProposes InterMASH, a cross-embodiment grasp representation based on sphere-anchored tokens whose spherical-harmonic features jointly encode local hand and object geometry plus contact. A conditional diffusion transformer operates on these tokens to generate consistent hand shapes and contacts, achieving competitive physical feasibility on ShadowHand benchmarks and benefiting from joint human–robot training.Link
