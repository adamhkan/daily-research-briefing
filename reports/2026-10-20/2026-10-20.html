Executive Summary

  • New proprioception- and touch-centric methods substantially improve contact-rich and blind manipulation, from peg-in-hole assembly to dexterous Baoding ball rotation.
  • Foundation-model-driven perception and spatial memory are being tightly integrated with mobile manipulators and VLN agents for more robust navigation and rearrangement under occlusion and body constraints.
  • Multiple works advance world and dynamics modeling for planning, including object-centric rigid 3D world models, neural time fields, and structured off-road dynamics for aggressive navigation.
  • Generalist VLA/WAM ecosystems are rapidly maturing, with new frameworks for multi-arm coordination, long-horizon language-guided manipulation, event-triggered memory, and efficient or transferable action experts.
  • RL and control advances target safer, more sample-efficient planning and control, from sparsemax STL robustness and metareasoning about compute budgets to whole-body loco-manipulation and adaptive vehicle control.
  • Mapping, localization, and SLAM see notable progress, including microscopic-precision localization, sonar-based full 6-DoF SLAM, and dynamic-scene SLAM leveraging motion-derived uncertainty.

Blind Precise Assembly: Proprioceptive Sim-to-Real Peg-in-Hole for Diverse PartsMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyDemonstrates blind, contact-rich assembly policies that rely solely on joint proprioception, without vision or tactile/force sensing. RL-trained in simulation over many randomized assemblies, the policies transfer zero-shot to real robots, achieving 73% success on diverse peg insertions and generalizing better to unseen parts than matched visual policies.Link
Foundation-Model-Driven 3D Scene Graphs for Mobile ManipulationMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyIntegrates a foundation-model-driven 3D scene-graph system (FOUND-IT) into a mobile manipulator to serve as spatial memory for navigation and manipulation. On-robot experiments show support for change detection, multi-session mapping, path determination, and direct grasp execution for scene rearrangement, along with a detailed analysis of monocular 3D scene-graph limitations.Link
Ego2Contact: Predicting Touch from Egocentric Human Video for Contact-Rich Robot ManipulationCarnegie Mellon UniversityLearns a visual–tactile prior from human egocentric video and transfers it to robot hands to predict dense bimanual contact fields from RGB alone. The predictor, aligned via anatomical region correspondences, outperforms prior tactile predictors and enables policies that recover part of the performance of true tactile sensing in both simulated RL and real-robot post-training.Link
Structured Perception Representations for Off-road Dynamics LearningCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces SpatialBEV, which conditions off-road vehicle dynamics on local bird's-eye-view perception features queried around predicted states. Across seasonal datasets and high-speed hardware trials, it cuts prediction error by 17.1% and maximum cross-track error by 25.3% compared to physics-based and learned baselines, improving aggressive off-road navigation.Link
What Makes a Good Symbol for Hybrid Discrete-Continuous Planning?Carnegie Mellon UniversityDefines "solver-sufficient" symbolic abstractions that expose the discrete commitments needed for efficient hybrid planning and proposes a counterexample-guided symbol-invention algorithm. Extends the concept to neural energy-based objectives, showing learned symbols can cut solving cost while preserving solution quality and improving interpretability in simulated and real-robot tasks.Link
FCTS: Failure-Guided Curriculum Tree Search Over Generated Terrain for Legged ParkourCarnegie Mellon UniversityPresents a failure-conditioned terrain curriculum that synthesizes new training terrains directly from localized traversal failures for quadruped parkour. Using MCTS to explore curriculum trees under a fixed budget, it boosts success from 28.4% to 85.8% on medium and 14.9% to 44.7% on hard construction-site courses, with demonstrated real-world transfer.Link
Embodiment-Classifier Guidance: Transferring Frozen VLAs Across Robot Dynamics without DemonstrationsUC BerkeleyAdapts a frozen flow-matching VLA ($\pi_0$) to new robot embodiments using a lightweight embodiment classifier trained on a few calibration tasks, without new demonstrations or policy updates. Classifier gradients guide each denoising step toward target-body-successful actions, yielding a 31% relative reduction in failures across 12 LIBERO suite–controller pairs and suggesting a promising sim-to-real transfer mechanism.Link
EmbodiedMidtrain: Bridging the Gap between Vision-Language Models and Vision-Language-Action Models via Mid-trainingCarnegie Mellon UniversityQuantifies the distribution gap between generic VLM data and VLA training data, then introduces a mid-training data engine to select VLA-aligned VLM samples and mid-train the backbone before action fine-tuning. Across multiple VLMs and VLA heads, this mid-training consistently improves manipulation and navigation performance, including on tasks not used to select the data.Link
