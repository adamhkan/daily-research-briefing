## Executive Summary

- New proprioception- and touch-centric methods substantially improve contact-rich and blind manipulation, from peg-in-hole assembly to dexterous Baoding ball rotation.
- Foundation-model-driven perception and spatial memory are being tightly integrated with mobile manipulators and VLN agents for more robust navigation and rearrangement under occlusion and body constraints.
- Multiple works advance world and dynamics modeling for planning, including object-centric rigid 3D world models, neural time fields, and structured off-road dynamics for aggressive navigation.
- Generalist VLA/WAM ecosystems are rapidly maturing, with new frameworks for multi-arm coordination, long-horizon language-guided manipulation, event-triggered memory, and efficient or transferable action experts.
- RL and control advances target safer, more sample-efficient planning and control, from sparsemax STL robustness and metareasoning about compute budgets to whole-body loco-manipulation and adaptive vehicle control.
- Mapping, localization, and SLAM see notable progress, including microscopic-precision localization, sonar-based full 6-DoF SLAM, and dynamic-scene SLAM leveraging motion-derived uncertainty.
