## Executive Summary

- Humanoid whole-body loco-manipulation and interaction continues to accelerate, with multiple systems (OCLO, I-BFM, InterMimicGen, CoDance, rickshaw pulling) demonstrating robust, compliant behaviors on Unitree G1 in contact-rich tasks without requiring large curated motion datasets.
- Dexterous and bimanual manipulation is a major focus, with new cross-embodiment primitives, high-fidelity human-to-robot retargeting, tactile-guided correspondence, and interaction-pattern tokenization substantially boosting success rates and embodiment transfer.
- Navigation and motion planning work spans safe nonlinear MPC/MPPI hybrids, diffusion and flow-based trajectory optimization, risk-aware agile flight, multi-robot coordination, and certified car-like planning, often blending sampling, optimization, and learned components.
- Vision-language(-action) and world-action models are rapidly maturing into unified planning and control backbones, with contributions on continuous spline actions, hierarchical and memory-augmented world models, efficient real-time WAMs, and multiple execution-time adaptation, safety, and verification schemes.
- Mobile manipulation and human–robot co-transport receive dedicated attention through new benchmarks (RMMBench), cloth-aware whole-body planning, human-UGV cooperative routing, and mobile dual-arm and quadruped payload handling frameworks.
- Sim-to-real and data-efficiency themes recur across papers via disturbance observers, active curation, recursive self-improvement, test-time adaptation (TTT, ZO), real-to-sim co-training pipelines, and rich new datasets and sensing setups for navigation and manipulation.
