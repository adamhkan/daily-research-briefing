Executive Summary

  • Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation sees multiple advances, from modular three-point grasping and tactile-informed policies to tight insertion (ContactDP) and precision assembly with reconfigurable hands.
  • Robot foundation models evolve toward 3D awareness, visuo-tactile world-action modeling, and explicit force/compliance outputs, improving robustness under occlusion and contact (Bridge3D, DexTacWAM, CompVLA, Opt2VLA).
  • A rich cluster of work targets long-horizon, failure-robust manipulation via world models, corrective execution, task-state grounding, and active perception (Imagine-RL, CARE, TaskAnchor, ATAP, SCULPT-VLA, AR-WAM).
  • Navigation and motion planning span SE(3) neural potential fields, grid-native RRT* acceleration, safety with barrier functions and feasibility fields, social and semantic navigation, and LiDAR/UAV exploration.
  • Whole-body and locomotion control advances include constraint-aware humanoid smoothness (DeCap), unified point-level fusion for rough terrain (UniPoint), predictive diffusion control for humanoids (PredActor), and transferable whole-body skills (STRIDER, WB-UMI).
  • Benchmarks and datasets (H2RBench, LIBERO-VPro, ActiveArena, PackLab, ARCGym, HEARTH) push evaluation of robustness, human-to-robot transfer, active perception, navigation in deformable or cluttered environments, and temperature-/tactile-aware manipulation.

Steerable and Reactive Grasping Through Modular Design with a Three-Point InterfaceMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyProposes a modular dexterous grasping pipeline that separates global geometric grasp selection from local contact control using a compact three-point interface. A model-based controller guides the hand toward sampled contact triples, while an object-agnostic RL policy refines and stabilizes grasps using only proprioception, enabling shared policies across objects and grasp types in both simulation and hardware.Link
ARSTAG: An Agentic Real2Sim2Real System for Task-Specific Robot Data GenerationCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces ARSTAG, an agentic Real2Sim2Real pipeline that turns a single RGB image plus language instruction into large task-specific simulation datasets for manipulation. Hierarchical language agents construct scenes, generate demonstrations, and randomize tasks, enabling sim-to-real transfer of visuomotor policies with up to 74.6% average real-world success on dual-arm tasks.Link
Grounded Action Model: 3D Grounding as a Foundation for RoboticsUniversity of WashingtonPresents Grounded Action Models that explicitly 3D-ground target objects via language, point, or box prompts into an object-centric representation fused with robot state in a multi-stream transformer. The model improves robustness to target relocation and visual perturbations, achieving state-of-the-art performance on LIBERO-PRO and strong robustness on real bimanual and Franka platforms.Link
ContactDP: Contact-Guided Diffusion Policy for Tight Insertion TasksStanford UniversityDevelops ContactDP, a diffusion-policy framework that fuses wrist RGB, fingertip tactile, and force-torque sensing for tight connector insertion. Coupled with a hybrid position-force controller, it infers contact states and generates compliant corrective motions, significantly outperforming vision-only diffusion baselines on industrial-grade insertion tasks.Link
RopeFormer: Cross-Trial Adaptation from Interaction History for Dynamic Rope ManipulationUC BerkeleyIntroduces RopeFormer, a history-conditioned control framework that leverages prior interaction trajectories as context to adapt to unknown rope dynamics without online parameter estimation. In both simulation and real Unitree H1-2 experiments, retaining interaction history substantially improves dynamic rope tasks such as swinging, twirling, and whipping.Link
A Reconfigurable Dual-Opposition Architecture for Single-Hand Assembly and ManipulationUC BerkeleyProposes a four-finger hand with two virtual-finger oppositions and a reconfigurable palm to enable simultaneous grasping of two parts and coordinated in-hand assembly. Kinematic modeling and experiments show high workspace coverage, robust object rotation under disturbances, and improved in-hand assembly vs. a LEAP Hand, both in simulation and hardware.Link
H2RBench: A Real-to-Sim Benchmark for Evaluating Human-to-Robot TransferCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces H2RBench, a Real2Sim benchmark with standardized protocols to compare human-to-robot transfer methods across four diverse manipulation tasks. It shows substantial differences in how methods leverage extra human data and finds strong correlation between simulation and real-robot rankings, enabling scalable pre-deployment evaluation.Link
MimicAgent: Quadruped Skills via Prompt-to-Trajectory GenerationCarnegie Mellon UniversityMimicAgent uses LLM-powered coding agents to synthesize reference motion trajectories for quadrupeds directly from skill prompts, bypassing manual reward shaping. These references supervise example-guided RL, yielding diverse dynamic skills in simulation and real hardware, with 87% of prompts producing semantically aligned motions.Link
Anticipatory Robot Goalkeeping via Monotone Optimal StoppingCarnegie Mellon UniversityFormulates when to launch an interception in robotic goalkeeping as a finite-horizon optimal stopping problem conditioned on a learned save policy. The proposed monotone optimal stopping method learns an advantage-based act-vs-wait rule that respects physical urgency, improving save rate and direction-reversal saves in simulation and on real quadruped hardware.Link
Elevator-VIGS: Separating Elevator Motion from Robot Motion in Visual-Inertial Gaussian Splatting SLAMETH ZurichElevator-VIGS augments 3D Gaussian Splatting SLAM with an explicit elevator-motion state to reconcile conflicting camera and IMU cues during elevator rides. Using VLM-based ride detection and transport-state estimation within bundle adjustment, it maintains accurate tracking and mapping through vertical transport while preserving state-of-the-art performance on standard benchmarks.Link
PileBelief: Persistent Physical State for Interaction-Driven World ModelingMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyProposes PileBelief, a world model for excavation that maintains a persistent belief over hidden terrain support and material state beyond local observations. By combining priors with deformation and response memories, it improves multi-step prediction and action selection for scooping tasks on both MPM simulations and real excavation data.Link

SE(3) Neural Potential Fields for 6-DoF Trajectory Planning Directly from Images Without Explicit 3D ReconstructionIntroduces an SE(3) neural potential field trained to approximate a true navigation function—the geodesic distance to a grasp pose—directly from posed RGB images. On tabletop UR10 experiments, the learned field yields collision-free, high-clearance 6-DoF trajectories that converge from all tested starts while planning orders of magnitude faster than RRT* over reconstructed geometry.Link
ScaleMPA: Rethinking Scalable RRT* Acceleration With a Grid-Native RepresentationPresents ScaleMPA, a dedicated motion-planning accelerator that reformulates RRT* around a grid-native representation with direct cell access instead of tree-centric structures. Implemented in 28 nm CMOS, it uses multi-resolution grids and hash-based memory to achieve millisecond-level planning and 4.7–44.4× speedups over prior hardware accelerators in high-dimensional spaces.Link
Feasibility Distance Fields for Heterogeneous Constraints in Robot Configuration SpaceDefines feasibility distance fields as distances in joint space to the union of infeasible configurations, giving a unified, Lipschitz-continuous margin for heterogeneous constraints (collisions, torque, dexterity, manipulability). Neural approximations of these fields support composable safety margins and gradient-based motion planning, with strong empirical accuracy on UR5e and dual-arm setups.Link
Fast and Robust Temporal Logic Planning via ADMM-based Trajectory OptimizationTechnical University of MunichDevelops a nonconvex ADMM-based trajectory optimizer for continuous-time motion planning under temporal-logic constraints, representing safety and task specs as unions of convex sets encoded in a feasibility graph. Euclidean projections reduce to shortest- and widest-path problems, yielding smooth, collision-free trajectories with a 4.7× average speedup over state-of-the-art TL planners.Link
Rapidly-Iterating Grid-Based Near-Optimal Kinodynamic Motion PlanningProposes RDG, a kinodynamic planner that uses a state-space grid to achieve constant-time node selection, propagation, and graph revision, while being probabilistically complete and asymptotically near-optimal. Across environments up to 10 DoF, RDG outperforms SST and DIRT in solution quality and maintains 100% success where competitors fail.Link
A Topological Representation with Object-Path Graphs for Open-Vocabulary Instance NavigationHong Kong University of Science and TechnologyIntroduces object–path graphs that couple open-vocabulary semantic reasoning with topological navigation, enabling agents to navigate via graph-based localization and semantic visual servoing instead of dense metric maps. Experiments on HM3D, Replica, and real robots show competitive grounding and effective navigation using this lightweight representation.Link
Learning to Drive on Mars: Visual Multimodal Traversability Estimation for Off-World NavigationCalifornia Institute of Technology; University of California, Los Angeles; University of Southern CaliforniaReleases a large Mars 2020 rover dataset (500 sols, 45 km) and proposes an uncertainty-aware multimodal traversability estimator using stereo, IMU, rocker-bogie angles, tilt, and slip. The model outperforms baselines in AUROC and F1 and can be integrated into planners like ENav, improving off-world navigation on challenging Martian terrain.Link
Performance-Preserving Online Adaptation in Social Navigation via Diffusion SteeringApplies diffusion steering RL to adapt social navigation policies online by training only a noise policy while keeping the base diffusion policy fixed, preserving original performance. The method efficiently adapts to new social conventions in simulation and hardware-in-the-loop tests while maintaining collision avoidance and goal-reaching capabilities.Link
UniPoint: Unified Point-Level Sensor Fusion for Humanoid Locomotion Across Challenging TerrainsZhejiang UniversityProposes UniPoint, which early-fuses 360° LiDAR and multiple depth cameras into a unified point token set processed with linear and cross-attention for humanoid locomotion. Trained once with terrain-aware rewards and degradation randomization, a single policy enables a DR02 humanoid to traverse gaps, platforms, thin barriers, and sparse footholds, running onboard in real time.Link
WOLF: World Model Guided LiDAR Exploration with Predictive FrontiersUniversity of Science and Technology of ChinaIntroduces WOLF, which learns a recurrent world model over LiDAR observations to predict future occupancy and visibility, generating predictive frontiers that complement measured frontiers for exploration. Simulation and real UAV experiments show reduced exploration time and higher coverage, especially in occluded environments like tunnels.Link
