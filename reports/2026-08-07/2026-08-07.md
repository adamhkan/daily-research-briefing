## Executive Summary

- Safety-aware navigation and motion planning see advances from certified FoV-constrained exploration (SCOPE) to GPU-accelerated joint-space planning (GASP) and ultra-large-scale multi-agent pathfinding (PRIMAL3).
- Multiple works push robustness and efficiency in embodied navigation and control, including spiking neuromorphic policies (SpikingNav), vision-based UUV operations, and interpretable UAV guidance.
- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) systems for manipulation mature with instruction-aware spatial alignment (Mind-VLA), memory-augmented architectures (BridgeVLA++), and test-time correction of frozen policies (RTCF).
- Dexterous and household manipulation benefit from curated egocentric human data (SiMDex) and modular foundation-model-based perception pipelines for kitchen robotics.
- Long-horizon task execution and planning under uncertainty are addressed via neuro-symbolic and language memory systems (Mimir, explicit language memory), temporal-logic-constrained RL and planning, and rulebook-based approximate multi-objective search.
- New methods for pose estimation and action-conditioned world modeling improve robustness to calibration errors and statistical bias, enabling more reliable state estimation and control for robotic systems.
