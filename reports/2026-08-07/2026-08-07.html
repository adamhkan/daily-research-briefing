Executive Summary

  • Safety-aware navigation and motion planning see advances from certified FoV-constrained exploration (SCOPE) to GPU-accelerated joint-space planning (GASP) and ultra-large-scale multi-agent pathfinding (PRIMAL3).
  • Multiple works push robustness and efficiency in embodied navigation and control, including spiking neuromorphic policies (SpikingNav), vision-based UUV operations, and interpretable UAV guidance.
  • Vision-Language-Action (VLA) systems for manipulation mature with instruction-aware spatial alignment (Mind-VLA), memory-augmented architectures (BridgeVLA++), and test-time correction of frozen policies (RTCF).
  • Dexterous and household manipulation benefit from curated egocentric human data (SiMDex) and modular foundation-model-based perception pipelines for kitchen robotics.
  • Long-horizon task execution and planning under uncertainty are addressed via neuro-symbolic and language memory systems (Mimir, explicit language memory), temporal-logic-constrained RL and planning, and rulebook-based approximate multi-objective search.
  • New methods for pose estimation and action-conditioned world modeling improve robustness to calibration errors and statistical bias, enabling more reliable state estimation and control for robotic systems.

SCOPE: Field-of-View-Aware Path Planning in Unknown 3D Environments via Safety-Volume CertificationCarnegie Mellon UniversitySCOPE introduces a planning framework that certifies a robot’s inflated safety volume using a graph of FoV-verified states, decoupling optimistic goal-directed guidance from certified execution. It converts uncertified path points into explicit observation obligations resolved via targeted viewpoint search and observation-aware trajectory optimization, achieving conditional completeness and near-zero entry into uncertified space in unknown 3D environments.Link
PRIMAL3: Pathfinding via Reinforcement and Imitation Multi-Agent Learning - Leveraging LaCAM3Stanford UniversityPRIMAL3 is a learning-based multi-agent pathfinding framework that combines RL, topology-aware communication graphs, LaCAM3-guided interventions during training, and PIBT-based action refinement. It targets bottlenecks and conflict zones, scaling to up to 100,000 agents while outperforming SOTA learned MAPF baselines and demonstrating feasibility on real robot fleets.Link

GASP: GPU-Accelerated Safe Planner for Real-Time Collision-Aware Motion Generation with Latent Trajectory SamplingGASP is a GPU-accelerated joint-space motion generator that parameterizes trajectories as clamped B-splines, using a CNN and conditional VAE to sample and evaluate many candidate motions in parallel for collision-aware planning. It achieves near-millisecond inference with analytical-level feasibility and is validated as both an online planner and a reset generator in robotic table tennis, cutting collisions and computation time versus optimization-based baselines.Link
SpikingNav: Robust Embodied Navigation with Spiking Neural PoliciesChinese Academy of Sciences; Tsinghua University; University of Chinese Academy of SciencesSpikingNav proposes a spiking sensing encoder and recurrent spiking policy network for embodied navigation, exploiting event-driven computation and membrane dynamics. On PointNav/ObjectNav with visual corruptions, it matches ANN performance in clean settings while improving robustness and efficiency, and is demonstrated on a neuromorphic chip for practical deployment.Link
Deliberate Before You Fly: Vision-Guided Spatial Deliberation for UAV See-and-Reach NavigationDBFly introduces a vision-language waypoint prediction framework for UAV see-and-reach tasks that explicitly reasons about spatial maneuvers before generating waypoints. It uses a maneuver decision chain, an implicit flight corridor, and a convergence-aware stopping strategy to align semantic intent with control, boosting success rates by over 25 percentage points on challenging benchmarks.Link
A Vision-based Control Framework for Real-time Autonomous UUV OperationsNorwegian University of Science and TechnologyThis work presents an integrated vision-based pipeline for unmanned underwater vehicles that fuses localization, mapping, and navigation in real time. The system supports both net-relative and global localization with continuous 3D mapping, and is validated in synthetic datasets and real UUV net-navigation experiments in visually challenging, dynamic underwater environments.Link
Interpretable Fuzzy Inference for UAV Target Tracking Using Bounding-Box GeometryThe paper proposes a fuzzy-inference-based controller to generate continuous yaw commands for UAVs using only low-dimensional features from YOLO bounding boxes. With compact Mamdani and Takagi–Sugeno models, it achieves sub-degree yaw errors and high directional consistency, offering a transparent, data-efficient alternative to deep models for real-time vision-based target following.Link
BridgeVLA++: A Data-Efficient, Generalizable, and Memory-Augmented Vision-Language-Action Framework for 3D ManipulationChinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of SciencesBridgeVLA++ augments a VLM-aligned 3D VLA pipeline with unified spatio-temporal memory that tracks persistent spatial context and interaction history. It maintains data efficiency and strong generalization while achieving SOTA on memory-dependent manipulation benchmarks, extends to bimanual settings, and is validated on additional real robot platforms.Link
Mind-VLA: Instruction-Aware Spatial Representation Alignment for Vision-Language-Action ModelsMind-VLA focuses representation alignment on the specific target object referenced by language, using tri-view target projections and aligned VAE/VGGT features. By making VLA embeddings instruction-aware, it greatly improves performance on fine-grained and occluded manipulation tasks in simulation and boosts real-robot success under target occlusion.Link
SiMDex: Mining Similar Egocentric Videos for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous ManipulationSiMDex casts selection of human egocentric videos for dexterous manipulation as a recommendation problem, using a recall–ranking–re-ranking pipeline to mine task-relevant samples. From a pool of ~32M clips, it selects <5% most relevant data yet raises VLA success on dexterous tasks from 47.7% to 61.1%, showing focused human data curation outperforms random mixing.Link
Kitchen Robotic Manipulation utilizing Foundation Models. Jeon is with the School; University of Illinois Urbana-ChampaignThe authors design a modular perception stack for kitchen manipulation that combines open-vocabulary detection, multi-view segmentation, 3D reconstruction, and feature fusion for 6D pose estimation and grasp planning. Evaluated on a cluttered kitchen dataset, the best combination of foundation models achieves high pose accuracy and transfers to real robots for tasks like dish loading without retraining.Link
Toward Integrating Adaptive Experience Replay and Online Uncertainty Estimation in Safe Actor-Critic Optimal ControlThis work couples uncertainty estimation, control barrier functions, and prioritized replay in a unified safe actor-critic framework for navigation with corrupted obstacle measurements. On a 2D robot task under extreme perception noise, the integrated design eliminates contacts while maintaining goal reachability, with theoretical bounds on replay exposure and robust barrier conditions.Link
