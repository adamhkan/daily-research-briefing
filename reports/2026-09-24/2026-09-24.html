Executive Summary

  • CMU introduces JAMB, a joint action–motion diffusion policy that substantially boosts success and robustness in bimanual manipulation.
  • New motion-planning and control methods advance constrained planning, dynamic deformable-object manipulation, and humanoid whole-body loco-manipulation.
  • Multiple works push dexterous and forceful manipulation forward, from all-linear Cartesian fingers and visually grounded fingertip forces to tool-use via hand–object mechanisms.
  • Navigation and SLAM see progress in prediction-aware legged obstacle avoidance, monocular and drone navigation, semantic orchard mapping, and radar–lidar SE(3) localization.
  • Vision-language(-action) systems continue to mature, with geometry-aware and safety-wrapped VLA manipulation, sparse semantic navigation, and unified embodied models.
  • UC San Diego, UC Berkeley, and Stanford contribute institution-aligned work spanning continuum catheter localization, free-flyer space manipulation, driving-safety benchmarking, and humanoid-RL education.

JAMB: Joint Action-Motion Diffusion for Bimanual ManipulationCarnegie Mellon UniversityJAMB jointly denoises bimanual actions and future 3D point tracks in a shared transformer, explicitly coupling control and predicted scene geometry. On 16 simulated tasks and several real-robot evaluations, it strongly outperforms action-only and auxiliary-geometry baselines, with better generalization in cluttered and out-of-distribution scenes.Link
Shaft-Configuration-Adaptive Catheter Tip Position Estimation via Motor-History Conditioned Residual LearningUC San DiegoThis work augments a calibrated geometric catheter model with a motor-history-conditioned GRU residual corrector to estimate 3D tip pose without explicit shaft sensing. Across multiple catheters and bent configurations, it cuts tip-position error by ~59% versus a constant-curvature baseline, improving navigation accuracy for tendon-driven continuum instruments.Link
A Deployable Four-Finger Payload for Teleoperated Free-Flying Manipulation with AstrobeeUC BerkeleyThe authors design a 17-DoF, four-finger payload on a linear rail for NASA’s Astrobee, controlled via VR teleoperation to map human hand motions to bimanual free-flyer manipulation. Zero-gravity MuJoCo simulations on cargo transport tasks show feasible performance and quantify success, contact maintenance, and task times.Link
NavSafe-$\infty$: Benchmarking Closed-Loop Driving Safety in Photorealistic EnvironmentsUC San DiegoNavSafe-$\infty$ is a photorealistic closed-loop benchmark of 280 driving scenarios with safety-centric taxonomies for crashes, violations, and incidents. Evaluating 20 end-to-end policies reveals weak correlation between open-loop metrics and closed-loop safety, and shows how common training remedies can trade safety margin for progress.Link
Teaching Reinforcement Learning and Humanoid Robotics to High-School Students: An Expert-Validated Curriculum Design on a Low-Cost Open PlatformStanford UniversityThis paper proposes a research-inspired curriculum where high-school students assemble an open-source humanoid, train a walking policy in simulation, and deploy it on hardware. Expert review surfaces key design tensions—authenticity vs. cognitive load, integration vs. visible progress, and teamwork vs. accountability—informing a reusable framework for precollege robotics courses.Link

Induced Riemannian Metrics for Motion Planning with ConstraintsUniversity of TorontoThe authors formalize motion planning on constraint submanifolds using induced Riemannian metrics, ensuring that implicit and explicit task-space representations share the same geometry. They extend both sampling-based planning and trajectory optimization under general metrics, and demonstrate benefits on constrained bimanual manipulation with dual Franka arms.Link
PLAT: Sparse Timed Keyframe Motion Tracking for Humanoid Control via Privileged Latent Transition LearningPLAT learns humanoid controllers that track sparse future keyframes with timing rather than dense motion, using privileged dense motion during training to shape a latent transition model. A latent residual RL stage refines transitions instead of raw actions, yielding stable, accurate whole-body motions in simulation and on a Unitree G1.Link
HOTICE: Whole-Body Humanoid Object Transportation in Cluttered EnvironmentsHOTICE combines humanoid–object decoupled potential fields with a dual-agent RL architecture that separates upper- and lower-body control while sharing state and rewards. Trained via specialist-to-generalist distillation, the policy enables a Unitree G1 to transport varied objects through clutter with coordinated whole-body collision avoidance.Link
Brace Yourself: Task-Conditioned Environmental Bracing for Forceful Humanoid ManipulationQueensland University of TechnologyThis work introduces Supporting Hand Strategy (SHS), which learns to use one arm to brace against the environment while the other performs forceful tasks. On a Unitree G1, SHS boosts sustainable interaction forces from 13.5 N to about 60 N and improves force tracking, extending humanoid manipulation capability without online trajectory optimization.Link
VisForce: Visual Grounding of Current and Desired Forces for Goal-Conditioned Dexterous ManipulationVisForce encodes current and desired fingertip forces as rendered visual cues over wrist and goal images, then uses cross-attention to produce force-aware actions. On a UR10 with a dexterous hand, it achieves consistent force-conditioned grasps and 40–70% success on multi-stage force-sensitive tasks such as slip-modulated peg-in-hole.Link
The Cartesian Hand: In-Hand Manipulation with All-Linear FingersThe Cartesian Hand is a 7-DoF end-effector with two independent parallel grippers and four translating fingertips that generate relative object motion using only linear DOFs. Its configuration-independent fingertip kinematics enable complex in-hand tasks—threaded closures, pipetting, triggers, screwdriving—on 35 objects, including bimanual use on a humanoid.Link
Relative Contact Velocity-Controlled Hand-Object Mechanism for Dexterous Tool ManipulationThis paper models a hand and tool as a unified hand–object mechanism controlled via Cartesian relative contact velocities and forces between generalized contact frames. A lightweight least-squares motion planner and complementary filter enable five different multi-finger hands to execute full tool-use sequences—grasp, reorient, and wield—from the same simple references.Link
CableVLA: Simulation-Privileged Global-Local Representation Learning for Cable RoutingCableVLA couples a topology-aware visual head (TopoHead) with a tactile encoder (TacSense) to learn global cable configuration and local contact dynamics from simulation-privileged signals. A contact gate injects force-tactile residuals into a frozen policy, raising simulated cable-routing success from ~63% to ~85% and improving slip detection versus CNN-LSTM baselines.Link
Manipulation of Deformable Linear Objects Using Model Predictive Path Integral Control with Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory LearningThe authors learn DLO dynamics with a biLSTM trained on MuJoCo data and embed it in a Model Predictive Path Integral controller for closed-loop cable-like object manipulation. Simulations and experiments show accurate trajectory tracking and efficient control despite the object’s high-dimensional, nonlinear behavior.Link
Manipulation with Stability Guarantees: Linear Deformable Objects with Non-negligible Physical Response Grasped at Multiple LocationThis work develops a fully dynamic model of linear deformable objects with multiple actuation points and recasts manipulation as shape regulation via forces and torques in SE(3). Nonlinear feedback laws provide explicit steady-state convergence conditions, and simulations plus real-time experiments with online shape estimation demonstrate stable manipulation beyond quasi-static regimes.Link
