Executive Summary

  • Humanoid and whole-body loco-manipulation advances ($\omega$-0) show end-to-end world-action modeling for smooth manipulate-while-moving behavior in homes.
  • Navigation and motion planning see progress from fast convex corridor generation on point clouds (PathCover) and underwater navigation under partial observability.
  • Autonomous driving/world-action planners (Adaptive-WAM, Robust-WAM) leverage video diffusion and semantic foresight to cut latency and improve robustness to visual shifts.
  • Multi-agent and multi-robot coordination methods (SJRL for LMAPF-R2, CoMuDi for disassembly) address realistic dynamics, rotations, and makespan optimization.
  • Manipulation research continues to scale generalist VLA/VLWA policies and control fidelity, with advances in dynamics priors, impedance control, affordances, and viewpoint/wrist modeling.
  • Scene-level reasoning for mobile robots (Prior-SG, ETH Zurich) uses LLM-derived priors to segment functional regions in open, arbitrarily-structured environments.

Prior-SG: Task and Prior Driven Region Segmentation for Scene Graphs in Arbitrarily-Structured EnvironmentsETH ZurichIntroduces Prior-SG, which fuses online RGB-D mapping with LLM-synthesized topological priors to build hierarchical 3D scene graphs for mobile platforms. A MAP-based Markov Random Field inference integrates visual, geometric, and object cues with task-specific prior graphs, enabling robust semantic region segmentation even in open-plan environments and allowing the robot to restructure space based on high-level tasks.Link

$\omega$-0: A Latent Predictive World Action Model for Concurrent Humanoid Loco-ManipulationNanyang Technological University; National Taiwan University$\omega$-0 is a latent predictive world-action model that generates whole-body actions for humanoids performing concurrent loco-manipulation from language, vision, and proprioception. It learns compact future observation embeddings coupled with diffusion-based whole-body action generation and is trained on the 40+ hour $\omega$-HOME dataset, enabling smooth manipulate-while-walking behaviors across 11 real household tasks.Link
Adaptive-WAM: Quality-Guided Early-Exit Planning from Intermediate Video-Diffusion FeaturesAdaptive-WAM studies how much of a video diffusion backbone is needed for reliable driving decisions and exploits insensitivity to noise levels to decode trajectories from intermediate layers. By attaching multi-exit trajectory heads and a lightweight quality scorer, it adaptively truncates computation, eliminating full video generation while achieving state-of-the-art navigation performance on NAVSIM and transferring to nuScenes with low error and latency.Link
PathCover: A Fast Convex Decomposition along a Path via Randomized Iterative Space Partitioning (RISP) on Point Clouds. Poulakakis is with Robotics Institute; University of DelawarePathCover introduces RISP, a randomized algorithm that constructs overlapping convex polytopes directly from raw point clouds in expected linear time, forming safe corridors along a reference path. These corridors constrain downstream MPC and trajectory optimization, providing an order-of-magnitude speedup over existing corridor generators and enabling real-time navigation on quadrotor and quadruped platforms with live LiDAR.Link
Unified Planning-Learning Framework for Robust UUV Navigation Under Partial ObservabilityNorwegian University of Science and TechnologyProposes an observation-only pipeline for UUV navigation that combines persistent occupancy mapping, a clearance-aware global planner, and a risk-aware RL local controller. A learned latent state captures environment structure and dynamics, while behavior-tree distillation with uncertainty-aware weighting improves safety and robustness in dynamic, partially observable underwater environments.Link
ErgoSurf: Ergodic Control for the Coverage of Unknown SurfacesTechnical University of MunichErgoSurf presents an online ergodic control framework that simultaneously reconstructs unknown surface geometry and plans contact-rich coverage trajectories. A GP implicit surface model is updated from tactile contacts, while locally sampled point clouds and heat-diffusion-based potentials guide ergodic exploration, yielding smooth trajectories and accurate surface reconstruction in simulation and hardware.Link
Topometric Autonomous Vehicle Localization by Combining Visual Embeddings and Feed-Forward 3D ModelsThis work fuses Visual Place Recognition embeddings with feed-forward 3D geometry models in a topometric framework for robust vehicle localization. An offline mapping stage models pose-appearance interactions, and an online particle filter couples odometry, place beliefs, and FF3D pose estimates, significantly improving appearance-based localization performance and handling perceptual aliasing.Link
Search-Aided Joint Agent-Environment Reinforcement Learning for Robust Lifelong Multi-Agent Path Finding with RotationsAddresses a realistic lifelong MAPF setting with in-place rotation and strict safety constraints (LMAPF-R2) inspired by warehouse robots. SJRL augments policies with Causal PIBT single-step search and jointly learns agent and environment policies, where the environment shapes edge costs for global guidance, yielding large performance gains and validated in mixed-reality experiments with hundreds of agents.Link
Coordinated Multi-Robot Disassembly for Makespan Optimization of Large-Scale AssembliesCoMuDi plans coordinated pick, place, and exit motions for teams of robots disassembling large assemblies under precedence constraints. By propagating temporal constraints and using space-time RRT* to minimize individual arrival times, it reduces idle time and overall makespan, scaling to scenarios with up to 49 parts and 9 robots.Link
Robust-WAM: Bridging Generative Pretraining and Semantic Foresight in World-Action ModelsRobust-WAM augments VAE-latent video-based world-action models with a semantic foresight alignment objective that injects appearance-invariant future scene semantics into the action stream. Learnable query tokens aligned to temporal positions preserve benefits of large-scale generative pretraining while improving robustness to illumination and appearance shifts in embodied control tasks.Link
VIDP: Variable Impedance Diffusion Policy for Compliant Robot Manipulation from Diverse DemonstrationsVIDP learns variable impedance manipulation policies from kinematic demonstrations without force sensing by modeling task-parameterized trajectory distributions via a directionality-aware mixture model. These distributions are mapped to stiffness profiles, enabling joint prediction of poses and compliance, and experiments show better success, lower interaction forces, and reduced tracking error versus fixed-impedance baselines.Link
