## Executive Summary

- Humanoid and whole-body loco-manipulation advances ($\omega$-0) show end-to-end world-action modeling for smooth manipulate-while-moving behavior in homes.
- Navigation and motion planning see progress from fast convex corridor generation on point clouds (PathCover) and underwater navigation under partial observability.
- Autonomous driving/world-action planners (Adaptive-WAM, Robust-WAM) leverage video diffusion and semantic foresight to cut latency and improve robustness to visual shifts.
- Multi-agent and multi-robot coordination methods (SJRL for LMAPF-R2, CoMuDi for disassembly) address realistic dynamics, rotations, and makespan optimization.
- Manipulation research continues to scale generalist VLA/VLWA policies and control fidelity, with advances in dynamics priors, impedance control, affordances, and viewpoint/wrist modeling.
- Scene-level reasoning for mobile robots (Prior-SG, ETH Zurich) uses LLM-derived priors to segment functional regions in open, arbitrarily-structured environments.

| PathCover: A Fast Convex Decomposition along a Path via Randomized Iterative Space Partitioning (RISP) on Point Clouds | .
