## Executive Summary

- Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation sees strong advances in simulation pre-training, teleoperation, force/tactile-aware retargeting, and arm-aware grasp generation, reducing data needs and improving real-world transfer.
- Vision-language-action (VLA) models continue to mature for long-horizon manipulation and whole-body control via better memory, phase-aware and stop-aware interfaces, tactile residual modulation, efficient fine-tuning, and device/edge execution.
- New planning and control frameworks tackle complex motion: mixed discrete-continuous contact planning, hybrid contact-motion planning for deformable and soft robots, and body-aware risk planning for legged platforms and UAVs.
- Spatial and temporal memory for long-horizon tasks is being rethought with task-driven 3D Gaussian maps, compressed visual histories, and structured reward/credit assignment for robust long-horizon manipulation learning.
- Navigation and SLAM robustness improve through motion-blur-aware and Gaussian-splatting SLAM, LiDAR degeneracy analysis, dead-end prediction, curriculum learning for locomotion, and safe region construction for constrained environments.
- Foundation datasets and tools—from high-fidelity human motion and tactile-intelligence reviews to safety scenario characterization—aim to underpin scalable humanoid, locomotion, and manipulation policy learning.
