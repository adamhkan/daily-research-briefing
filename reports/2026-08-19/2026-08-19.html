Executive Summary

  • Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation sees strong advances in simulation pre-training, teleoperation, force/tactile-aware retargeting, and arm-aware grasp generation, reducing data needs and improving real-world transfer.
  • Vision-language-action (VLA) models continue to mature for long-horizon manipulation and whole-body control via better memory, phase-aware and stop-aware interfaces, tactile residual modulation, efficient fine-tuning, and device/edge execution.
  • New planning and control frameworks tackle complex motion: mixed discrete-continuous contact planning, hybrid contact-motion planning for deformable and soft robots, and body-aware risk planning for legged platforms and UAVs.
  • Spatial and temporal memory for long-horizon tasks is being rethought with task-driven 3D Gaussian maps, compressed visual histories, and structured reward/credit assignment for robust long-horizon manipulation learning.
  • Navigation and SLAM robustness improve through motion-blur-aware and Gaussian-splatting SLAM, LiDAR degeneracy analysis, dead-end prediction, curriculum learning for locomotion, and safe region construction for constrained environments.
  • Foundation datasets and tools—from high-fidelity human motion and tactile-intelligence reviews to safety scenario characterization—aim to underpin scalable humanoid, locomotion, and manipulation policy learning.

Pre-training Visual Dexterity in SimulationMassachusetts Institute of Technology; Stanford UniversityIntroduces Simulation Pre-training for Dexterity (SPD), where humans teleoperate a virtual dexterous hand in VR to collect 75 hours of multi-task data for a causal transformer. When fine-tuned on just 1–2 hours of real bimanual dexterous demonstrations, the pre-trained policies outperform behavior cloning from scratch, validating sim-only pre-training as a strong data source for real-world dexterous manipulation.Link
Revisiting Open-Loop Execution in Robotics: Toward Reactive, Higher-Performing PoliciesMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyAnalyzes why action chunking (open-loop execution of action sequences) helps imitation policies and shows its key benefit is compensating for non-Markovian expert demonstrations rather than merely reducing compounding errors. With sufficiently long observation context, fully reactive closed-loop policies outperform long open-loop execution, motivating design of long-context, highly reactive manipulation policies.Link
ScenarioCharacterization: A Modular Toolkit for Characterizing Safety across Trajectory DatasetsCarnegie Mellon UniversityPresents an open-source, configuration-driven toolkit that standardizes how driving trajectory datasets are converted into a common scenario representation and then characterized for features, behaviors, and criticality. The modular design allows rapid analysis and visualization across datasets like Waymo, Argoverse2, and nuPlan, supporting safety and scenario-based evaluation workflows.Link
MistyPilot: Enabling Social-Robot Control through Multi-Agent LLM Skill OrchestrationNew York UniversityImplements a multi-agent LLM control stack for the Misty social robot, splitting responsibilities between agents for physical interaction (sensor-triggered skills) and social dialogue/state management. The system interprets natural-language instructions, binds sensors to skills, and manages task state, achieving robust routing and low-variance behavior while users report good usability.Link

ViHaTeleop: A Low-Cost, Lightweight Visual-Haptic Teleoperation System for Dexterous Manipulation LearningTohoku UniversityIntroduces a $550, 0.7 kg visual-haptic teleoperation interface combining SLAM-based wrist tracking, camera-based hand tracking, and vibrotactile feedback for each finger. User studies across six contact-critical tasks show haptic feedback consistently increases success rates and perceived contact clarity, and demonstrations collected with the system improve downstream visual-tactile manipulation policies.Link
ReForce: Learning Force-aware Retargeting for Dexterous ManipulationPresents ReForce, which augments standard kinematic hand retargeting with a learned force residual to better match human-intended contact forces on robot hands. Using a general force-tracking model trained in simulation, it supports both online teleoperation and offline demonstration translation, improving force tracking and multi-finger contact engagement on demanding tasks like deformable cup grasping.Link
Pre-training Visual Dexterity in SimulationMassachusetts Institute of Technology; Stanford UniversitySPD collects 75 hours of VR teleoperated dexterous-hand data entirely in simulation to pre-train a causal transformer on multi-task manipulation. Fine-tuning the pre-trained model on 1–2 hours of real bimanual-hand demonstrations yields clear gains over training from scratch, showing sim-only teleoperation is a practical pre-training source for real dexterous manipulation.Link
Arm-Aware Guided Dexterous Grasp Generation with Arm-Agnostic Grasp ModelsProposes a framework that turns an arm-agnostic diffusion grasp generator into an arm-aware system at inference time via joint optimization over hand pose and arm configuration. Closed-form gradients for arm- and environment-related constraints guide diffusion sampling toward feasible grasps, drastically increasing feasible grasp rates in constrained scenes without retraining for each arm.Link
FlexWorm: Primitive-augmented Hybrid Contact-motion Planning for Suction-based Multi-segment Deformable RobotsPeking University; School of Advanced Manufacturing and RoboticsIntroduces a hybrid planner for multi-segment suction-based soft robots navigating complex 3D surfaces, explicitly handling adhesion switching and body deformation. A block-wise IK hybrid search explores feasible adhesion transitions, while a learned primitive-retrieval layer accelerates local proposals; the method outperforms baselines in simulation and transfers to real soft robots under uncertainty.Link
Accelerating Mixed Discrete-Continuous Motion Planning via Neural Graphs of Convex SetsNortheastern UniversitySpeeds up Graphs of Convex Sets motion planning by replacing expensive convex relaxations with a graph attention network that predicts promising paths and a small ranking network that orders them. Across quadrotor, 7-DoF arm, and contact-rich pushing tasks, the approach preserves 100% success while achieving up to two orders of magnitude runtime reduction with modest cost suboptimality.Link
FlexWorm: Primitive-augmented Hybrid Contact-motion Planning for Suction-based Multi-segment Deformable RobotsPeking University; School of Advanced Manufacturing and RoboticsDevelops a planning framework for deformable multi-segment soft robots with suction pads, combining best-first search over adhesion patterns with continuous IK under collision and quasi-static constraints. A learned primitive library supplies reusable local motion segments to accelerate search, improving planning success and time and demonstrating feasibility on a pneumatic soft robot platform.Link
Robot-Body-Aware Traversal Risk Graph Planning for Wheeled-Legged Robots in Complex TerrainIntelligent Perception and Human-Machine Collaboration - ShanghaiTech UniversityExtends Traversal Risk Graph (TRG) navigation by explicitly reasoning over an oriented body footprint and yaw sweeps along graph edges for wheeled-legged robots. The resulting RB-TRG planner reduces support-loss and body-interference metrics and raises simulated success rates from 51.5% to 68.5%, with real-world Go2-W trials demonstrating improved safe navigation on rough terrain.Link

The policy, trained on over 40,000 hours of multimodal real-world data, can allocate extra test-time compute to search over subtask proposals, yielding higher subtask prediction accuracy and better closed-loop long-horizon performance.Link
