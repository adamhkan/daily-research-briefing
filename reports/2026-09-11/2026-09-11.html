Executive Summary

  • Dexterous and deformable-object manipulation see major advances, from grounding generated video plans in simulation to real-robot benchmarks and full-shape estimation under partial observability.
  • New frameworks for VLM/VLA-driven manipulation and generalist policies (GTA-2, DUET-DINO, FreqFM, TFGCA, HaWMPO, RoboDrop, JEPA Policy) significantly boost zero-shot skill synthesis, long-horizon planning, and data efficiency.
  • Motion planning and navigation progress spans AGV traffic management and routing, continuum-robot diffusion planners, symmetry-aware learned planners, and high-rate MPC for tiny drones.
  • Whole-body control and locomotion are pushed to more challenging regimes, including humanoid brachiation, granular terrain walking, perceptive visual loco-manipulation, and legged curricula via actuator dynamics.
  • Safety, robustness, and partial observability are addressed via belief-state planning for LLM agents, STL-based corrective control, risk-sensitive driving, odometry drift correction, and adaptive shared control.
  • Benchmarks and systems work (FolDeX, RealSimLoop, InstantMimic, Bayesian optimization survey) emphasize standardized evaluation, real-to-sim adaptation, and massively accelerated policy training.

A multi-object, multi-trajectory HOI tracker trained on over 1,500 grounded videos substantially outperforms baselines and transfers to real robots for diverse grasps and non-prehensile manipulation.Link
GTA-2: A Multi-VLM Framework for Synthesizing Robot Manipulation Skills via Grounded Task AxesCarnegie Mellon UniversityPresents GTA-2, a modular multi-VLM system that composes object-centric task axes into executable, task-specific manipulation skills without task-specific demos or policy training. Four specialized VLM agents decompose tasks, assemble axis-based skills, assign controller parameters, and ground visual features, achieving strong zero-shot and refined performance on 14 real-robot tasks.Link

Assembling Two Parts in One HandHong Kong University of Science and Technology; The University of Hong Kong; University of Hong Kong ffiliation:Formulates in-hand assembly of two rigid objects with a single dexterous hand as an RL problem driven by a goal relative pose between parts. Auxiliary rewards, human-inspired pose regularization, and robust sensing enable a single recipe to solve multiple assembly tasks in simulation with zero-shot transfer to hardware using only one camera.Link

Leveraging complementary views and DINOv3 features, it improves zero-shot planning on diverse manipulation tasks and is robust to visual distribution shifts.Link
