Executive Summary

  • Navigation and motion planning see advances from human-aware crowd navigation, 3DGS-based benchmark generation, and foundational theory relaxing δ-similarity assumptions.
  • Multiple works target robust perception and mapping for navigation, including underwater BEV occupancy, multi-sensor odometry in degenerate environments, surround-depth for driving, and outdoor 3D scene graphs.
  • Autonomous driving research emphasizes cooperative and connected behaviors via LLM-based decision negotiation, edge-assisted fusion and trajectory prediction, and diffusion-based trajectory planning and scenario generation.
  • Dextrous and contact-rich manipulation progresses through tactile-informed world-action models, analysis of hand morphology as an inductive bias, and intent-aware bimanual handover using temporal tactile encoding.
  • VLA and world-action models remain central in manipulation, with new benchmarks, robustness and failure-recovery evaluations, and techniques for efficient online RL, latent reasoning, and fine-grained failure detection.
  • Institution-highlighted work from MIT applies differential game theory to drone-swarm defense, demonstrating statistically significant gains in interception success over unilateral optimization tactics.

Game-Theoretic Drone Swarm Defense: A Case Study in Applied Differential Game TheoryMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyThis report applies differential game theory to assign defenders and guide midcourse maneuvers for drone swarms protecting high-value assets. Compared to unilateral optimization baselines, Nash-equilibrium-based tactics significantly increase full-interception probability, especially against evasive intruders, with Bayesian analysis indicating a 99.9% posterior probability of superior defense performance.Link

Human-Human & Human-Robot Interaction Transformer (H2INT) for Robot Navigation in Dense and Uncertain CrowdsH2INT is a reinforcement learning framework for robot navigation in dense crowds that explicitly models heterogeneous human-human and human-robot interactions via a gated two-stage Transformer and a recurrent policy. By inferring pedestrian responsiveness from relative geometry and using a curriculum that reduces responsiveness, it improves simulated safety and robustness and transfers to real-robot deployment without retraining.Link
NavArena: Automated Construction of Goal-Oriented Navigation Benchmarks from 3D Gaussian Splatting ReconstructionsNavArena converts fixed 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstructions into large-scale goal-oriented visual navigation benchmarks by combining egocentric RGB-D rendering, occupancy costmaps, and semantic goal extraction from open-vocabulary masks. Across over 2,000 scenes and 22.2M expert trajectories, it enables unified, closed-loop evaluation protocols for spatial and semantic navigation policies.Link
Achieving Asymptotic Near-Optimality Without $\delta$-SimilarityThis work revisits theoretical guarantees for sampling-based motion planners that rely on δ-similar trajectories to prove asymptotic near-optimality. It identifies a "crowding out" failure mode where low-cost local paths prevent δ-similar segments from entering the tree, and shows that near-optimality can still be ensured by explicitly accounting for this phenomenon without requiring δ-similar solutions.Link
AquaBEV: Monocular Underwater BEV Occupancy with 3D Sonar SupervisionUniversity of South FloridaAquaBEV predicts local bird’s-eye-view occupancy for underwater robots from a single RGB image, using paired 3D imaging sonar as geometric supervision during training. A polar, calibration-free representation with causal range-wise decoding improves Visible IoU and Observed IoU by roughly 4% over adapted terrestrial occupancy baselines on a controlled underwater benchmark.Link
FIRE-LIVWO: Robust LiDAR-Inertial-Visual-Wheel Odometry via Failure-Immune mmWave Radar Enhancement. Zhou and E. Hu are with the SchoolFIRE-LIVWO is a tightly coupled multi-modal odometry framework for underground coal mines that integrates 4D mmWave radar, LiDAR, vision, and wheel odometry in a unified VoxelMap with an iterated error-state Kalman filter. It detects degeneracy online, adaptively reweights modalities, and uses Doppler constraints and non-holonomic vehicle constraints to maintain localization accuracy (≈5.7 m error) under smoke, dust, and corridor degeneracy.Link
A Schema Bounded Language Model for Refining Robot Policies Without Destabilizing Local LearningThis study investigates decentralized navigation by heterogeneous robots combining LLM-based policy agents, UCB bandits, and Double DQN controllers, with LLMs operating at round-level rather than per-tick decisions. Cross-robot policy summaries allow schema-bounded refinement, and experiments in a shared simulation show that the full configuration consistently reaches goals fastest without destabilizing low-level learning.Link
ToPos: Automated Optimal Positioning on Topographic Manifolds using Constrained Geodesic Voronoi DecompositionToPos formulates the distribution of spatial reference sites on complex terrain as a geodesic Voronoi decomposition problem on a 2D manifold embedded in 3D. Using Riemannian Nesterov-accelerated optimization and feasibility constraints (e.g., safe zones, slopes, occlusions), it achieves approximately 74% improvement in surface area balance over planar methods, supporting mapping, sampling, and deployment planning in high-relief environments.Link
Open-Set 3D Scene Graphs for Field Robotics: An Outdoor Case StudyBrigham Young UniversityUsing Terra 3DSG as a case study, this field report analyzes open-set 3D scene graphs for outdoor robotic navigation and object retrieval across five datasets. It finds that while compact, consistent 3DSGs can support navigation-based retrieval with ~70% success, challenges remain in handling multimodal semantic embeddings, integrating traversability, and achieving reliable region-level understanding.Link
Scalable Edge-assisted Fusion and Path Prediction for Connected Autonomous VehiclesGeorgia Institute of Technology; University of EdinburghConductor is an edge-based system that fuses AV and roadside-unit data into a unified world model and predicts vehicle trajectories under strict age-of-information constraints. An occlusion-aware selector and adaptive controller jointly manage which AV inputs and prediction horizons to process, achieving near-oracle fusion fidelity and maintaining AoI safety bounds in simulations with up to 31 connected autonomous vehicles.Link
One Diffusion Model, Two Roles: Guided Trajectory Planning and Safety-Critical Scenario Generation in Closed-Loop SimulationKTH Royal Institute of TechnologyThis work leverages a single diffusion-based traffic model both as an ego-vehicle motion planner and as a generator of safety-critical scenarios in closed-loop driving simulation. A new SSDS decoder improves planning, while the DAPSE guidance method injects arbitrary energy functions at inference to steer agents toward realistic but adversarial interactions that expose planner vulnerabilities beyond standard benchmarks.Link
