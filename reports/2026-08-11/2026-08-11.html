Executive Summary

  • Navigation and planning see multiple advances: scalable lifelong multi-agent path finding, cross-floor semantic ObjectNav, unordered image-based visual navigation, and cross-embodiment VLA navigation.
  • Mobile manipulation and whole-body control progress via real-time planning for payload-carrying bases and a practical OpenArm lab automation prototype.
  • Language-conditioned and VLA-based world models are extended with 3D scene conditioning, persistent world-ego memory, adaptive model selection, and intention–trajectory decoupling for more robust closed-loop control.
  • Dexterous and tactile manipulation capabilities improve through contact-consistent video-to-hand retargeting, high-bandwidth dynamic tactile contact localization, and a biomimetic haptic fingertip for fine instrument playing.
  • Tool and multi-robot planning pipelines gain from real-time LiDAR-based accessibility fields and low-power Ising hardware acceleration for multi-target assignment and routing.
  • Institution-highlighted work from CMU, UW, and ETH Zurich spans massive-scale multi-agent path planning, force actuation for surgical robots, and self-supervised outdoor LiDAR representations.

Scalable Long-Horizon Planning with Staggered Updates for Lifelong MAPFCarnegie Mellon UniversityIntroduces PUSH, a lifelong multi-agent path finding planner that combines staggered planning windows, RHCR-style windowed paths, and EPIBT-inspired priority inheritance to achieve long-horizon reasoning at massive scale. In realistic benchmarks, PUSH coordinates up to 10k agents in under a second and significantly boosts throughput in congested environments over prior MAPF baselines.Link
A Disturbance in the Force: Force Actuation on the RAVEN II Surgical Robot with Parallel Motor-Cable UnitsUniversity of WashingtonDevelops a parallel motor–cable system that applies controlled external forces to a RAVEN II surgical robot end-effector without impeding motion, enabling rich training data for learning-based force estimation. Six cable units deliver precise force actuation with preliminary errors under 1 N, supporting future haptic feedback and dynamics modeling in surgical robotics.Link
Vernata: Self-Supervised Learning of LiDAR Point RepresentationsETH ZurichPresents Vernata, a multi-modal, multi-teacher self-supervised framework for outdoor LiDAR that adds sparse view augmentation, a memory bank, and cross-modal distillation from high-resolution images. On several large-scale datasets, Vernata substantially improves mIoU over Sonata baselines and remains competitive even when LiDAR inputs lack color or normals.Link

Real-time Whole-Body Motion Planning for Mobile Manipulators Carrying Arbitrarily Shaped Payloads via Kinematically-Coupled SVSDFTsinghua UniversityProposes a three-stage whole-body planning framework for mobile manipulators transporting large, non-convex payloads through clutter, combining fast chain-decomposed collision checking, mid-level path smoothing, and KC-SVSDF-based trajectory optimization. Experiments show reliable real-time navigation of tightly constrained environments while preserving full robot–payload geometry.Link
Representation Handoffs for OpenArm-Based Laboratory Mobile ManipulationDescribes an OpenArm-based dual-arm mobile manipulation platform for lab tasks structured around explicit representation handoffs from language to skills, perception to maps, and objects to motion goals. The report emphasizes dry-run traces and startup checks as debugging interfaces, surfacing calibration and grounding gaps before full physical deployment.Link
LifelongCrossNav: Persistent 3D Semantic Memory for Cross-Floor Multi-Object NavigationIntroduces LifelongCrossNav, which maintains a shared sparse 3D semantic voxel memory during sequential multi-object navigation across multiple floors, integrating geometry, traversability, and vision–language features. A unified policy handles exploration, stair perception/traversal, and object search, outperforming planar semantic-map baselines on the new HM3D-MFMON benchmark.Link
Unordered Landmark Visual NavigationPresents ULVN, an RGB-only navigation framework that builds a 2D topological map from unordered images via geometric verification and maximum spanning forest refinement, avoiding temporal and odometric priors. Closed-loop execution relies on graph-based belief propagation and entropy-adaptive fusion for global localization and dynamic subgoal planning, outperforming prior methods in sim and real deployments.Link
CrossTracer: Cross-Embodiment Navigation via VLA Model Reasoning and Trace Residuals AdaptingPOSTECHIntroduces CrossTracer, which separates semantic navigation reasoning from embodiment-specific feasibility by representing paths as pixel-space traces refined with embodiment-conditioned residuals. A CE-RRT* planner synthesizes training traces from panoptic segmentation, and the approach surpasses strong VLA baselines on NaviTrace and improves real-world navigation for wheeled and legged robots.Link
WNM-3D: A World Navigation Model with 3D Scene Conditioning for Closed-Loop VLNProposes WNM-3D, a generative world navigation model that conditions joint future-view and action generation on geometry-aware 3D scene tokens extracted from egocentric RGB histories. Trained with supervised A* demonstrations, DAgger adaptation, and policy optimization, it yields better closed-loop VLN performance and improved alignment between predicted motion and visual dynamics on GN-Bench.Link
Ising Acceleration for Multi-Robot Multi-Target PlanningUniversity of Minnesota, Twin CitiesAnalyzes how compact CMOS Ising machines can accelerate multi-robot multi-target planning, mapping target sharing, tour construction, and pathfinding subproblems onto 45-spin hardware. With a multi-mapping pipeline tailored to hardware limits, the Ising-based target-sharing method achieves up to 8,000× lower energy and near-baseline route quality at 130× lower energy overall.Link
Beyond Visibility: Real-Time Surface Accessibility Fields from Sparse LiDARIntroduces Accessibility Fields, a per-point estimate of whether a surface is reachable by a specified tool, computed in real time from streaming sparse LiDAR via GPU-based kernel checks over tool poses. Built atop a scan-centric TSDF, the system runs on desktop and Jetson Orin and significantly outperforms visibility-only baselines, enabling access-aware planning without prior scene models.Link
Detection and Ranging of Transient Extrinsic Contacts Based on 6D Dynamic Tactile SensingHarbin Institute of Technology; University of Science and Technology of ChinaPresents TECDAR, using a tiny 6D IMU embedded in gripper tips to sense sub-millisecond deformations at 7 kHz and localize transient external contacts on grasped objects. Fusing tactile signals with robot pose via an EKF yields millimeter-level contact localization within 180 ms, enabling rapid trajectory correction and tactile-based mapping for delicate manipulation.Link
C2Dex: Contact-Consistent Reconstruction and Retargeting for Dexterous Manipulation from Monocular VideoMobile Research Institute, Beijing, China.C2Dex reconstructs human hand–object interaction from monocular video by aggregating stable object-side contact patterns in canonical object space, enforcing temporal and physical consistency. These contacts guide retargeting to robot hands via Laplacian interaction optimization and residual RL, substantially boosting dexterous trajectory success on DexYCB/TACO and validating on real hardware.Link
