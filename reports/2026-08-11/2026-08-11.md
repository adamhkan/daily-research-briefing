## Executive Summary

- Navigation and planning see multiple advances: scalable lifelong multi-agent path finding, cross-floor semantic ObjectNav, unordered image-based visual navigation, and cross-embodiment VLA navigation.
- Mobile manipulation and whole-body control progress via real-time planning for payload-carrying bases and a practical OpenArm lab automation prototype.
- Language-conditioned and VLA-based world models are extended with 3D scene conditioning, persistent world-ego memory, adaptive model selection, and intention–trajectory decoupling for more robust closed-loop control.
- Dexterous and tactile manipulation capabilities improve through contact-consistent video-to-hand retargeting, high-bandwidth dynamic tactile contact localization, and a biomimetic haptic fingertip for fine instrument playing.
- Tool and multi-robot planning pipelines gain from real-time LiDAR-based accessibility fields and low-power Ising hardware acceleration for multi-target assignment and routing.
- Institution-highlighted work from CMU, UW, and ETH Zurich spans massive-scale multi-agent path planning, force actuation for surgical robots, and self-supervised outdoor LiDAR representations.

| Scalable Long-Horizon Planning with Staggered Updates for Lifelong MAPF | Carnegie Mellon University | Introduces PUSH, a lifelong multi-agent path finding planner that combines staggered planning windows, RHCR-style windowed paths, and EPIBT-inspired priority inheritance to achieve long-horizon reasoning at massive scale. | Vernata: Self-Supervised Learning of LiDAR Point Representations | ETH Zurich | Presents Vernata, a multi-modal, multi-teacher self-supervised framework for outdoor LiDAR that adds sparse view augmentation, a memory bank, and cross-modal distillation from high-resolution images. On several large-scale datasets, Vernata substantially improves mIoU over Sonata baselines and remains competitive even when LiDAR inputs lack color or normals. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06919 |
