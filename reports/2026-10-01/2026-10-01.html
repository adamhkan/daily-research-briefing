Executive Summary

  • Institution-aligned work spans fast deformable material identification for manipulation, hierarchical language-guided navigation control, and multiple advances in VLA robustness, memory, and policy adaptation.
  • Several papers push test-time adaptation: from dynamics and actuator degradation compensation to memory formation and self-compensation under execution errors.
  • Humanoid and whole-body control see new kernels for physical interaction, SIM(3)-equivariant loco-manipulation encoders, spectral skill representations, and human-to-humanoid transfer and retargeting.
  • A dense cluster of VLA/WAM contributions tackle long-horizon manipulation, force-aware and contact-rich control, acceleration and pruning, domain robustness, and representation design.
  • Navigation-focused work introduces social navigation from internet videos, audio-guided navigation, safety certificates, RGB-only safety filters, and structured map-based multimodal reasoning.
  • Multiple agentic and in-context frameworks target scalable data generation, skill acquisition and reuse, autonomous policy improvement, and systematic evaluation/benchmarks for manipulation and navigation.

FORM: Robot Manipulation through Direct Material Law IdentificationUC BerkeleyFORM identifies deformable-material laws online from a single interaction using a weak-form momentum formulation matching the forward simulator’s discretization, turning identification into fast linear least-squares. It cuts identification time to seconds while maintaining accurate prediction, and reuses the learned model for planning manipulation of rods, elastoplastic shaping, and precise pouring both in simulation and on hardware.Link
WayFinder: Hierarchical Visual-Language-Action for Zero-Shot Waypoint Generation and Low-Level Kinematic ControlUC San DiegoWayFinder factors navigation into a zero-shot multimodal LLM that generates waypoints from language and maps, and a lightweight onboard controller that executes continuous kinematic control. By querying the high-level policy only upon failure, it avoids fine-tuning, reduces expensive MLLM calls, and boosts navigation success in AirSim by up to 27.45% over low-level baselines.Link
BlenDAgger: Blended Shared Control for Interactive Imitation LearningCarnegie Mellon UniversityBlenDAgger blends human and policy actions during interventions instead of fully handing over control, generating smoother corrections and trajectories closer to the training distribution. Across five manipulation tasks, it yields over 30 percentage-point gains in autonomous success versus HG-DAgger and speeds up real-world data collection without worsening user experience.Link
T$^2$Mem: Learning Test-Time Memory for RoboticsStanford UniversityT$^2$Mem endows a frozen VLA manipulation policy with test-time-learned fast-weight memory, encoding observation history through online self-supervision without external memory labels. On 16 RoboMME tasks it lifts success from 17.93% to 56.83% and outperforms recurrent-memory baselines while being at least 3× faster than explicit history processing.Link
LIBERO-MAX: Do Robot Policies Adapt When the World Changes?Carnegie Mellon University; New York University; Stanford UniversityLIBERO-MAX is a paired benchmark that holds policy, initial state, and pre-change actions fixed while injecting mid-task scene changes, isolating robustness to temporal perturbations. Evaluating 14 policies shows consistent 11–25.7 percentage-point drops in success after events and shared failure modes for geometry and observation shifts, establishing a controlled testbed for adaptation under changing worlds.Link
One from Infinity: Actualizing Futures from Pretrained World Models into Robot ActionsUC BerkeleyRoboActualizer treats a pretrained video world model as a prior over possible futures and learns a small ‘actualizer’ that selects task-relevant futures and corresponding actions via flow matching, keeping the world model frozen. With ~60M trainable parameters—up to 100× fewer than typical WAMs/VLAs—it achieves strong performance on LIBERO, RoboTwin 2.0 and real robots with ~39 ms control latency.Link
KPI: A Promptable Kernel for Physical Interaction on HumanoidsCarnegie Mellon UniversityKPI inserts a ‘contract’ layer between trajectory sources and a generic whole-body tracker, allowing per-direction prompts such as track, comply, or hold-force-range. It adapts arm impedance and references at contact rate from tracking error and wrench estimates, enabling instruction-driven tasks like winch cranking, door opening, and box transport without task-specific low-level controllers.Link
Scouting the Dynamics Gap: Test-Time Policy Adaptation via Action-Outcome FeedbackCarnegie Mellon UniversitySCOUT meta-learns a shared latent ‘belief’ space linking a dynamics model and policy, updating this latent online by minimizing prediction error between expected and observed action outcomes. This enables rapid sim-to-real and dynamics adaptation for manipulation without reward signals, as the policy continuously revises its internal dynamics beliefs during deployment.Link
doPlan: A Variable-Horizon Dataset for Multi-Stage Language-Conditioned Planning in Autonomous DrivingUC San DiegodoPlan augments nuPlan with over 5,000 human-written passenger instructions aligned to up to ~8.5 minutes of driving, capturing immediate, deferred, event-conditioned, and multi-stage goals. Evaluations of language-conditioned driving models show that while they respond to language, they often fail to realize requested maneuvers, underscoring the challenge of connecting persistent intent to successive plans.Link
Comparing Utility of Inertial, Occupancy, Semantic, and Intent Information in Human Motion Prediction During Daily TasksStanford UniversityUsing a diffusion model trained on Meta Aria egocentric data, this work quantifies how inertial cues, scene occupancy/semantics, gaze, and explicit intent affect indoor human motion prediction. Including body motion, environment structure, and particularly explicit intent significantly cuts prediction error over constant-velocity baselines, highlighting that intent estimation or input is key for fine-grained motion forecasting.Link

Skill-Space Shooting for Autonomous Robot Policy ImprovementTsinghua UniversitySkill-space shooting uses foundation models to explore corrective ‘skills’—short reusable behaviors—to repair failures of existing task policies and distill them into policy updates. Real-world experiments show repeated autonomous improvement and cross-task sharing of learned skills, reducing human supervision for scalable manipulation policy refinement.Link
In-context Robot Learning Made Simple: A Democratized Recipe for Manipulation TasksSimpleICL formalizes robot in-context learning by clearly defining what aspects of visual demonstrations should guide behavior and introduces a minimalist framework with a visual prompt encoder and lightweight data collection. Without massive pretraining, it achieves strong manipulation performance in sim and real, and surfaces key properties such as action and affordance discrimination.Link
Counterfactual Video Generation Enables Scalable Humanoid Loco-ManipulationPRISM amplifies a few real human loco-manipulation videos into hundreds of diverse ‘counterfactual’ interactions via video-to-video generation, then reconstructs contact-consistent trajectories in simulation. A policy trained on these synthetic trajectories generalizes across object categories and transfers to a real humanoid, enabling depth-only pickup, carrying, and drop of varied objects without real fine-tuning.Link
Rho: A Foundation for Efficiently Adaptable VLA ModelsRho is an open VLA family for dual-arm manipulation that couples an action-expert architecture with embodiment midtraining across three common robot platforms, improving data-efficient adaptation. A small online latent module allows rapid correction from a handful of interventions, making Rho a practical base for bimanual manipulation in research and industry.Link
MotorMind: Scaffolding General Vision Language Models for Zero-Shot Robot ManipulationUniversity of Illinois Urbana-ChampaignMotorMind wraps a general-purpose VLM with a mid-level action interface and deterministic control harness so the VLM can issue and adapt manipulation actions directly, without learned action experts or coding agents. On LIBERO-PRO and a real xArm, it achieves much higher zero-shot success than prior methods, and benefits directly from stronger backbone VLMs.Link
WorldLine: Action-Driven Visual Simulation for Robotic ManipulationWorldLine learns action-driven visual dynamics from 10k+ hours of action-free robot video plus 2k hours of action-labeled data, using an image-space action interface to span many embodiments. It produces multi-view, interaction-sensitive rollouts that predict success/failure and can be distilled for efficient causal rollout, improving task success by up to 21.4 percentage points over direct execution.Link
EgoAlign: Bridging the Human-Humanoid Gap for Long-Range Loco-ManipulationBeihang UniversityEgoAlign converts egocentric human demonstrations into supervision compatible with a continuous whole-body humanoid controller, aligning locomotion and upper-body interaction via scale corrections and controller-in-the-loop refinement. Policies trained solely on adapted human data perform long-range relocation and foot interaction on real humanoids, competitive with teleoperation-trained baselines but cheaper to collect.Link
Explore, Execute, Evolve: A Skill Acquisition and Reuse Loop for Embodied AgentsRoboSkill organizes LLM-based agents in an Explore–Execute–Evolve loop that acquires, stores, and reuses skills with both visual and tactile feedback plus reusable code snippets. On LIBERO-10 and real robots, this loop cuts runtime significantly and raises first-episode and overall success, showing how structured skill reuse reduces repeated exploration cost.Link
MVG-WAM: Multiple View Geometry-Aware World-Action Modeling for Robotic ManipulationHong Kong University of Science and TechnologyMVG-WAM treats synchronized camera views as projections of a single physical world, learning a shared global state plus view-indexed geometric states with epipolar constraints and depth supervision. Camera-aware routing fuses these for action prediction, achieving ~99% success on LIBERO and strong real-world performance on multi-view bimanual tasks.Link
Geometry-Preserving Human-to-Robot Upper-Body Motion Retargeting from Monocular VideoThis framework reconstructs unified body–hand human motion from monocular RGB, then retargets it to dual-arm robots by preserving geometric relations—segment directions, elbow configurations, palm orientations—via multi-stage IK. It yields accurate kinematic playback in simulation and real signing and reach-to-grasp motions, enabling upper-body manipulation transfer from plain videos.Link
