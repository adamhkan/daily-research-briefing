Executive Summary

  • Sim-to-real and hybrid learning methods are advancing contact-rich manipulation, achieving high success in tight-clearance insertion, book shelving, garment unfolding, and deformable bag handling.
  • Navigation and motion planning see progress in resilient space-robot planning, off-road world models, semantic and task-aware active exploration, and large-scale multi-object navigation under resource constraints.
  • Whole-body and legged control are extended to challenging terrains and tasks, including roofing locomotion, humanoid multi-floor navigation, and fast dynamics-relaxed MPC for quadrupeds.
  • Vision-language(-action) models are increasingly integrated with classical planning, safety harnesses, and structured world models to improve long-horizon manipulation and language-guided navigation.
  • Tactile and compliance-centric approaches, from human-touch world models to bilateral teleoperation for impedance learning and agile tactile WAMs, significantly improve dexterous and contact-rich control.
  • Foundational scene and memory representations (scene graphs, workspace tokens, object-centric splatting) provide more robust spatial reasoning and long-horizon memory for manipulation and navigation stacks.

Visual Sim-to-Real Learning for Robotic Insertion under Geometric Variations: Application to Rebar InstallationUniversity of WashingtonRebarSim trains a privileged-state RL teacher in simulation over procedurally varied rebar geometries, then distills to a multi-view RGB+proprioception student under strong domain randomization. The student transfers zero-shot to a real factory rebar line, achieving 91.3% success in 1.4 mm-clearance insertion and requiring 4–6× fewer samples to adapt to new designs when pretrained.Link
Resilient Motion Planning for Free-Flying Space Robots under Actuator FailuresETH ZurichThis framework models thruster failures as a Markov chain and precomputes reachable sets to evaluate goal-attainment probability under degraded actuation. An RRT* planner stitches waypoints to maximize success probability, yielding motion plans that remain controllable under injected actuator failures on a real free-flyer platform.Link
Self-excited actuation enables adaptive and resilient flapping-wing flightUC San DiegoThe authors build the first flight-capable flapping robot using asynchronous, self-excited actuation inspired by insect flight muscles. The embodied dynamics let wings automatically adapt to body resonance changes and collisions without sensing, significantly improving flight stability and obstacle resilience versus synchronous actuation in cluttered environments.Link
Workspace Models: Lightweight Robotic Memory via Saliency-Driven SupervisionMassachusetts Institute of TechnologyWorkspace Models introduce a compact “workspace token” learned by distilling VLM-identified salient historical information during training into a single latent memory. At deployment, policies query only this token (no online VLM), improving both success and efficiency on memory-intensive manipulation tasks compared with conditioning on full observation histories.Link
OPTED: On-Policy Fine-Tuning for End-to-End Driving using a Render-Free TeacherETH ZurichOPTED trains a privileged vectorized teacher with RL and uses it to supervise camera-based end-to-end driving policies during closed-loop post-training. In AlpaSim 3DGS simulations, this decoupled scheme boosts driving scores up to 9.5× with roughly three orders of magnitude fewer simulator interactions than direct RL on the vision policy.Link
FAMOS: Feed-Forward 3D Articulation Modeling from Sparse ObservationsETH Zurich; Stanford UniversityFAMOS predicts movable-part segmentation and joint parameters from sparse, unordered point clouds using a multi-state articulation transformer that aggregates cues across views. With a procedural data generator and an “observed articulation span” objective, it improves over feed-forward and optimization baselines on multiple articulated-object datasets.Link
ParticleSplat: Self-supervised Object-centric Latent Particle SplattingCarnegie Mellon UniversityParticleSplat lifts latent 2D particles into a 3D Gaussian splatting space trained via self-supervised view synthesis, yielding an object-centric scene factorization. The learned particles provide unsupervised object masks, support controllable 3D scene edits, and enhance downstream robotic manipulation performance.Link

Planning over this “physical future” yields better rough-terrain route selection and higher success than visual-only world models, including real deployment on a Husky on mountain trails.Link
Task-Oriented Active Learning of Residual Dynamics for Model Predictive Path Integral ControlTechnical University of MunichToIA scores candidate rollouts in MPPI by how much early observations would reduce uncertainty at task-relevant future states, guiding online Gaussian-process residual learning. In simulated off-road navigation, this task-oriented criterion improves goal-reaching by up to ~27 percentage points over passive learning and outperforms task-agnostic active baselines, especially with sparse updates.Link

Coupled with a bounded 2-opt refinement, it yields up to 94× speedup over heavy meta-heuristics with bounded-latency inference and state-of-the-art PPL on >2000 m² ProcTHOR environments.Link

Learning Slope-Adaptive Whole-Body Locomotion for Humanoid Robots in Roofing ConstructionUniversity of FloridaThis framework retargets human roofing motions to a Unitree G1 and grounds them on a metric roof model, then refines via execution-aware RL to maintain support and work relations under tracking errors. The humanoid executes uphill walking, nailgunning, hammering, and bending on pitched roofs with <8 cm base-frame error while respecting support and clearance constraints.Link
DR-MPC: Fast and Feasible Dynamics-Relaxed Model-Predictive Control for Legged LocomotionDR-MPC relaxes dynamics and affine constraints into penalties, leaving only box constraints and yielding a structured QP solvable via a tailored interior-point method with Schur complements. On a Unitree Go1, it achieves ~4.4 ms onboard MPC cycles, 16× faster than HPIPM and 4.4× faster than OSQP, with comparable locomotion quality.Link
Learning Safe Humanoid Navigation from Reduced Order ModelsCalifornia Institute of TechnologyRoM-Nav first trains a navigation policy on reduced-order dynamics with 3D LiDAR, then transfers it to full humanoid dynamics with a frozen locomotion controller and a Poisson safety filter. Deployed on a Unitree G1, it achieves mapless multi-floor navigation with over 10 m vertical and 100 m path length while maintaining safety around out-of-distribution obstacles.Link
TADreamer: Zero-Shot Language-Guided 3D Navigation for Terrestrial-Aerial Bimodal Robots via Video ImaginationTADreamer uses a VLM to turn onboard observations and language into prompts for a video generator, reconstructing imagined navigation videos into 3D waypoints with mode (ground/air) labels. A two-stage calibration aligns these trajectories to real geometry, enabling zero-shot language-guided bimodal navigation across seven real indoor/outdoor scenarios.Link
