Executive Summary

  • Stanford and ETH Zurich contribute strong navigation and aerial-interaction methods, including tunable human-following in crowds and millimeter-accurate ceiling marking with a compliant aerial end-effector.
  • Multiple works advance risk- and safety-aware navigation and planning, from CVaR-based kinodynamic planning in uncertain planetary terrains to dual-space MPC safety monitors and robust safety filtering with control barrier functions.
  • Mobile manipulation and whole-body control see notable progress via semantic 3D Gaussian splatting for open-vocabulary manipulation and volume-informed whole-body planning for humanoids in confined environments.
  • Dexterous and deformable manipulation capabilities are extended with real-world cooperative bimanual grasping of large objects and competition-grade autonomous T-shirt handling.
  • World-action models and skill abstractions continue to mature, emphasizing failure-aware training, multi-stream geometry/semantics, and stage-level futures for robust, data-efficient manipulation policies.
  • Several works strengthen long-horizon autonomy infrastructure, including persistent world models for service robots, human-centric spatio-temporal memories, and fabric-specific perception tailored for deformable manipulation.

Navigating the Proximity-Safety Balance: Constraint Decomposition for Human Following in Pedestrian CrowdsStanford UniversityIntroduces a multi-constraint RL formulation for human-following in dense crowds that decomposes proximity and safety into separate cost constraints with interpretable thresholds. By explicitly incorporating human-motion prediction uncertainty into the costs, the method enables tunable trade-offs between staying close to a target and collision risk, validated in both simulated and real-robot deployments.Link
Aerial Layouting: Design and Control of a Compliant and Actuated End-Effector for Precise In-flight Marking on CeilingsETH ZurichPresents an aerial system with a novel smart end-effector based on a stability-optimized Gough-Stewart platform to achieve millimeter-level precision in ceiling marking tasks. Through compliant, multi-contact, and actuated design, the end-effector absorbs vehicle-level pose errors, enabling accurate aerial interaction without demanding extreme control precision from the drone.Link
Protection Levels for Vision-Based Pose EstimationStanford UniversityDerives protection levels—probabilistic bounds on pose error—for a nonlinear PnP-based, runway-relative vision navigation pipeline, even under undetected measurement faults. The work quantifies how redundancy, pixel uncertainty, and runway distance shape these bounds, offering a path toward certifiable integrity for vision-based aircraft navigation.Link
Lost in Reconstruction: Aligning Action Representations with Language in Vision-Language-Action ModelsCarnegie Mellon UniversityShows that reconstruction-only action tokenization in VLAs discards verb-level semantics relevant for language-conditioned control. Proposes SALT, an action tokenizer that aligns discrete action codes with language via an auxiliary instruction-reconstruction loss, yielding substantially higher success rates and verb-specialized codes while preserving action fidelity.Link

Whole-Body Planning for Humanoids Navigating Confined Spaces via Self-Collision Avoidance ReferencesUniversity of Texas at AustinProposes a three-stage whole-body planner for humanoids in highly confined environments that optimizes over reachable rigid-body volumes with differentiable collision avoidance and self-collision constraints. The resulting trajectories, used to train a residual RL policy, enable the Unitree G1 to execute complex multi-contact motions in spaces exceeding NIST emergency response standards where standard planners fail.Link
Embodied Multimodal Grounding for Open-Vocabulary Mobile Manipulation via Semantic 3D Gaussian SplattingHong Kong University of Science and TechnologyIntroduces a mobile manipulation framework that builds a task-driven Semantic 3D Gaussian Splatting map to unify active sensing, language-conditioned 3D grounding, and reachability-aware base positioning. Coupled with a diffusion-based VLA policy, this explicit 3D semantic interface improves robustness to clutter, occlusion, and embodiment shifts, significantly outperforming PointVLA and DexVLA in real-robot tests.Link
Risk-Aware Kinodynamic Motion Planning Under Uncertainty For Safe Navigation on Planetary EnvironmentsAerospace Engineering at the Georgia Institute; Georgia Institute of TechnologyCombines an AO-RRT kinodynamic planner with sequential convex programming to generate dynamically feasible, risk-aware trajectories under uncertain terrain and perception. Using CVaR as a risk metric, the approach cuts trajectory risk by roughly 97% in simulated and hardware experiments, targeting safe planetary rover navigation under learned interaction uncertainties.Link
AECNav: Active Evidence Consolidation for Efficient Zero-Shot Open-Vocabulary Object NavigationRecasts zero-shot object-goal navigation as an evidence-driven process, with shared semantic encodings, log-odds evidence consolidation, and active frontier selection for information gain. The training-free AECNav pipeline attains state-of-the-art success rates on HM3D and MP3D benchmarks while reducing inference overhead, and transfers effectively to a quadruped platform at ~5 Hz.Link
Dual Stress: Runtime Safety Monitoring for Safety-Constrained MPC NavigationExploits KKT multipliers from a safety-constrained MPC as a dual-space "stress" signal indicating marginal control effort to maintain safety around obstacles. This dual stress monitor complements geometric hazard metrics, jointly flagging about three-quarters of avoidable collisions in simulation, compared to under half for geometric detectors alone.Link
Nonlinear Model Predictive Control via Sequential Convex Programming for Drone-to-Drone DockingIndian Institute of Technology HyderabadFormulates mid-air drone docking as a nonlinear optimal control problem with disturbance-augmented dynamics, solved via sequential convex programming in a receding-horizon fashion. High-fidelity simulations show reliable convergence into tight docking cones and robustness to wind and estimation noise, with bounded approach velocities and constraint satisfaction.Link
Wind-Informed Rapid Flight-Planning in Complex Urban Topologies via Machine Learning and Experimental ValidationTrains a surrogate flow model to infer urban wind fields from building geometry and incident wind, then derives a volumetric flight-challenge field for path planning. Experimental MAV tests in a wind-tunnel urban mockup show that wind-aware trajectories reduce undesired displacement and improve stability compared to wind-agnostic plans.Link
Toward the Cognitive--Physical Limits of Embodied Intelligence through a World-Model-Centric Autonomous Racing AgentNanyang Technological University; National Taiwan UniversityDevelops a world-model-centric autonomous racing agent that learns predictive models near vehicle limits to couple world-state construction, future reasoning, and high-g control. Using data from real-vehicle racing up to 256.3 km/h and 26.8 m/s² lateral acceleration, the agent achieves high interaction success in simulation and refines its boundary-aware behavior via closed-loop world-model updates.Link
Dreamer-SAC: Off-Policy Learning in Latent World Models for Sample-Efficient Autonomous DrivingIntegrates a recurrent state-space world model with off-policy Soft Actor-Critic, training both policy and value functions directly in latent space using short-horizon rollouts and n-step targets. In autonomous driving tasks balancing efficiency and safety, Dreamer-SAC surpasses DreamerV3, SAC, and PPO while requiring substantially fewer real interactions, highlighting an optimal intermediate length for imagined rollouts.Link
Real-World Cooperative Bimanual Dexterous Grasp of Large Objects from Single-View ObservationsUniversity of AucklandPresents a real-world system for cooperative bimanual dexterous grasping of large objects using a DDPM-based grasp generator conditioned on single-view segmented point clouds. Coupled with motion planning and online grasp refinement, the framework achieves high success on a dual-arm platform across unseen objects, without requiring full 3D models.Link
