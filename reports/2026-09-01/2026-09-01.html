Executive Summary

  • Navigation under extreme or complex conditions is a major theme, from underwater caves and subarctic forests to hazardous multi-robot exploration with risk-aware coordination.
  • Multiple works push task and motion planning forward, including iterative game-theoretic coordination for multi-arm manipulators and reactive TAMP under partial observability.
  • Dexterous and contact-rich manipulation advances include a simulation-ready tendon-driven hand and improved VLA-based anticipatory reasoning.
  • Several papers target generalist VLA models and metric-aware perception, improving representation quality and geometry alignment for robust manipulation policies.
  • Industrial and applied systems focus on safe, verifiable procedures and reliable grasping/bin-picking in cluttered or deformable-object settings.
  • Multi-robot and distributed planning methods emphasize robustness to latency, heterogeneous sensors, and risk distribution while maintaining mission performance.

Contact-Guided Exploration for Non-Prehensile Locomanipulation with Multi-Critic RLIntroduces a multi-critic RL framework where a dedicated exploration critic, guided by dense contact-seeking rewards, drives a mobile manipulator toward meaningful contact points for non-prehensile tasks like box pushing and chair transport. Candidate interaction points from a generic grasping algorithm improve generalization across objects, and real-world results on a quadrupedal mobile manipulator demonstrate deployable non-prehensile locomanipulation.Link
Coordinated Motion Planning for Multi-Arm Systems via Iterative LQ GamesFormulates multi-arm motion planning as an iterative linear-quadratic game where each manipulator is an agent optimizing its own objective subject to shared state and collision constraints. Linearization around nominal trajectories and Riccati-based feedback Nash strategies yield smooth, collision-aware trajectories that scale to high-DOF systems and outperform traditional planners.Link
Plan Along the Way: Event-Triggered Foundation-Model Planning for TAMP Execution in Partially Observable ManipulationProposes ROBUST TAMP, an LLM/VLM-guided framework that repeatedly replans as new objects are discovered in partially observable manipulation scenes. By constraining the planner to validated, executable actions over the currently visible relational state and treating object discovery as explicit replanning events, the system improves long-horizon task success in RLBench kitchen and grill tasks with hidden objects and articulated containers.Link
STEGNav: Spatio-Temporal Event Graph Reasoning for Multimodal Lifelong Object NavigationPresents STEGNav, a training-free navigation framework that replaces static scene graphs with spatio-temporal event graphs capturing both target instances and exploration frontiers plus trajectory-aware memory. A VLM agent reasons over this representation to select navigation goals, achieving strong success rates on GOAT-Bench and HM3D variants and demonstrating reliable cross-task experience reuse.Link
Spatial-Semantic Reasoning using Large Language Models for Efficient UAV Search OperationsDevelops a real-time semantic navigation system for UAV ObjectNav where an LLM interprets natural language instructions and prioritizes high-probability search regions based on object detections and 3D spatial context. Integrated with online mapping and spline-based trajectory planning, the framework reduces mission duration in simulation and real-world tests while maintaining search accuracy.Link
CAVE-NAV: VLM-Based Autonomous 3D Navigation in Underwater Cave EnvironmentsUniversity of South FloridaIntroduces a VLM-guided framework for 3D navigation in visually degraded underwater caves, using chain-of-thought reasoning over RGB, depth, and sonar-derived clearance cues to infer safe motion directions. Simulated experiments across varied cave topologies show collision-free end-to-end traversals with maintained clearance from cave walls.Link
One year in a forest: Analyzing the challenges of autonomous navigation in subarctic environmentsLaval UniversityReports on a year-long deployment of a mobile robot in a subarctic forest, benchmarking nine odometry, localization, and mapping pipelines over 64 km of data. Seasonal appearance changes, self-similarity, and snowbanks significantly degrade state-of-the-art SLAM, with complex methods offering limited gains over proprioceptive baselines and lidar outperforming visual and radar for cross-season localization.Link
Cooperative Risk-Aware Exploration in Heterogeneous Multi-Robot Systems Using Algorithmic AltruismElectrical and Computer Engineering at Oklahoma State UniversityProposes a game-theoretic exploration framework where heterogeneous robots optimize information gain while internalizing teammates’ risk via altruistic coupling inspired by Hamilton’s rule. The resulting Social Nash Equilibrium reallocates hazard exposure toward lower-valued robots, reducing redundant coverage and improving team separation without sacrificing map coverage, with both simulations and hardware validation.Link
Probabilistic Multi-Robot Gas Source Localization with Uncalibrated Sensors: A Distributed Estimation ApproachEPFLIntroduces a calibration-free, distributed probabilistic scheme where each robot builds a local belief using rank-based features invariant to sensor nonlinearities, then fuses beliefs via a product-of-experts model. Coupled with informative-region allocation and path planning, the method yields accurate gas-source localization in high-fidelity simulations despite strongly heterogeneous, uncalibrated sensors.Link
Distributed Model-Based Diffusion: Finite Horizon Contraction under Bounded DelayAnalyzes Distributed Model-Based Diffusion, a sampling-based MPC approach for nonlinear, nonconvex multi-agent trajectory optimization under communication latency. Theoretical results show contraction and robustness to bounded delays, and experiments on driving and aerial combat tasks demonstrate improved makespan and win rates over centralized diffusion-based control.Link
