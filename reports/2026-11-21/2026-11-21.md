## Executive Summary

- Several works advance autonomous navigation and spatial decision-making: ROAM combines a pre-trained vision-language model, open-vocabulary segmentation, and two RL policies to build dense risk maps that enable kilometer-scale autonomous legged navigation in alpine and urban environments, including sign interpretation and safe foothold selection despite residual VLM and spatial-reasoning limitations; CHAC augments frozen VL navigation policies with training-free, memory-guided action chunking to cut model calls by 33.4–64.7% on HM3D-OVON and R2R-CE with modest success-rate changes and achieves more task completions in real-world runs while halving compute; passage-conditioned navigation for wheeled-legged robots stores each stair or ledge crossing as a passage event and enforces an entry gate during replanning, boosting disturbed ledge-crossing accuracy from 24.1% to 88.6%, preventing simulated falls in 180 disturbed stair climbs, and enabling fully autonomous completion of a 271 m multi-obstacle competition course; EAGLE trains a VLM-conditioned Swin teacher and a distilled context-free student to build explicit spatial-belief tensors for a compact PPO agent, then runs without any VLM at inference, achieving state-of-the-art active geo-localization success (78.1% on MASA, 76.4% on xBD zero-shot, 76.8% on MM-GAG); and a physics-informed probabilistic framework for gas source localization incrementally reconstructs indoor airflow fields from onboard wind and gas sensing to update a belief map and guide informative path planning, improving robustness and exploration efficiency in high-fidelity simulations and indoor robot experiments compared with less physically structured baselines. ([arXiv:2611.25215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25215); [arXiv:2611.25587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25587); [arXiv:2611.25788](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25788); [arXiv:2611.25866](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25866); [arXiv:2611.25067](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25067))
- New world-model and foundation frameworks support whole-body and embodied control: WEVO post-trains humanoid vision-language-action policies inside an action-conditioned video world model using simulated play plus a few autonomous real rollouts, where RL in the learned world model raises mean success from 23.3% to 75.0% on three real loco-manipulation tasks and outperforms real-robot RL (48.3%) under the same data budget; ECHO is an in-context humanoid behavior foundation model that learns a general behavior prior and dynamics-specific residuals from interaction histories without test-time parameter updates, surpassing prior BFMs across diverse motions and physical conditions and enabling extreme acrobatics, terrain interaction, and payload-adaptive whole-body teleoperation on Unitree G1 hardware; LocoMuJoCo offers an open-source benchmark and library unifying CPU MuJoCo with GPU MJX/MjWarp across 9 humanoid, 3 musculoskeletal, and 4 quadruped embodiments, with modular observation/reward/terrain plugins, curated motion-capture retargeting, and compact JAX RL/IL implementations plus a sim-to-real companion; O²LaF reuses a single video diffusion transformer as a shared prior for inverse and forward dynamics via a compact latent-action bottleneck, learning transferable actions from unlabeled videos that yield state-of-the-art few-shot action probing and strong zero-shot action transfer across navigation and manipulation settings; and TuringPAI introduces on-device Physical AI models built on a HybridViT-MLQ backbone—where a 0.65B-parameter TuringPAI-VL improves geometric perception over Qwen2-VL-2B, TuringPAI-Drive maintains driving ADE/FDE within 1.2% of a larger baseline at 12 Hz on an Orin-X SoC, and TuringPAI-Embodied achieves 96.8% average success across four LIBERO suites. ([arXiv:2611.25251](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25251); [arXiv:2611.25778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25778); [arXiv:2611.26293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26293); [arXiv:2611.26617](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26617); [arXiv:2611.25474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25474))
- Multi-agent coordination, tool use, and safety are addressed by several modeling advances: MAWAM extends a single-robot world-action backbone into a multi-agent world-action model using cross-agent attention and team-stage future-video supervision to jointly generate future views and action chunks, achieving 67.8% mean success on three real multi-arm manipulation tasks (18.9 percentage points over a MA-VLA baseline) and 90.3% mean success on eight simulated tasks, with ablations showing large performance drops when either cross-agent attention or team-stage supervision is removed; A²-VLA discovers and verifies tool affordances by reconstructing teleoperated demos in simulation and running autonomous experiments to build a reusable affordance library that conditions a π₀.₅ backbone, attaining 86.1% simulated cross-tool trajectory retargeting success and improving real-robot tool-use performance by 30.7 percentage points over the unaugmented baseline; SAFI enhances safety of vision-language-action policies by injecting fallback demonstrations that command stationary arms and consistent gripper states during prohibited tasks and jointly training on them, which with OpenVLA-OFT on LIBERO and BadLIBERO cuts aggregate target success after unsafe-policy takeover to 1.3% with no observed physical incidents while preserving performance on allowed tasks; and AgentRoboCritic introduces an intent-grounded multimodal critic that analyzes action-level outcomes and failure modes from frontier agents across 12 manipulation benchmarks, with fine-tuning of Muse-Glimmer-30B on 12,844 annotated actions improving failure-mode classification accuracy from 32.58% to 74.24%, enabling lower-cost, systematic action-level evaluation. ([arXiv:2611.25105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25105); [arXiv:2611.25656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25656); [arXiv:2611.26239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26239); [arXiv:2611.26613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26613))
- Several works improve manipulation robustness, correction, and trajectory repair: VG-Steer derives visual-geometric guidance from object-relational distance pairs plus semantics from large vision and VLM models to steer existing manipulation policies under distribution shift, improving simulated flow-matching policies under pose and goal shifts and more than doubling success one grid step outside training positions for a cable-placement policy while largely eliminating object confusion when steering to unseen targets; TipDex-VTLA combines Visual Action Sensitivity Estimation with Tactile Anticipation and Correction during Execution to gate bounded tactile corrections based on predicted vs. measured signals, achieving 87.5% average success on four real dexterous tasks under normal lighting and 73.8% under challenging unseen lighting, outperforming several baselines under visual perturbations; Hindsight Self-Distillation trains a teacher with access to current and future visual frames and a student with only current frames to match denoised actions and visual summaries, then deploys the student alone with single-pass encoding and reused summaries, attaining average success rates of 98.50% on LIBERO and 92.31% on RoboTwin 2.0; and Close&Safe repairs trajectories for redundant manipulators by following the nominal end-effector path exactly where feasible and computing minimal-deviation configuration-space detours near unforeseen obstacles, recovering 52.4–78% of nominal trajectories while avoiding collisions in diverse simulations and Franka hardware experiments. ([arXiv:2611.25199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25199); [arXiv:2611.25683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25683); [arXiv:2611.25777](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25777); [arXiv:2611.26031](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26031))
- Perception and 3D reconstruction methods target challenging sensing regimes: MIR-Depth performs single-view RGB-D depth completion for transparent objects by constructing coordinate-aware multi-range hypotheses (a global inverse-depth volume plus local depth-filtered volumes) and applying Selective Recurrent Refinement with ConvGRUs and Spatially Gated Correction, achieving lower depth errors than ReMake, DFNet, BP-Net, and PSD across multiple transparent-object benchmarks and supporting accurate 6-DoF grasp pose prediction in real-robot tests; Infer3D frames 3D reconstruction as inverse generative modeling that optimizes latent variables of a generative image model under constraints from a frozen 3D lifting network to enforce multi-view-consistent, non-degenerate geometry, yielding higher out-of-distribution reconstruction accuracy than feedforward networks on synthetic and real data—especially under extreme viewpoints—and introducing an adaptive inference strategy that allocates more test-time computation to ambiguous OOD inputs while routing in-distribution inputs more efficiently. ([arXiv:2611.25417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25417); [arXiv:2611.25061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25061))
- Additional control and locomotion contributions span driving, legged motion, ferrofluids, and exoskeleton assistance: a differentiable MPC module is used to replace the action head of pre-trained PPO driving policies while enforcing geometric constraints (road-boundary half-planes and agent ellipses), with DAgger distillation from the PPO teacher yielding constrained DiffMPC students that match or improve teacher performance across nuPlan, CARLA, and OOD Waymo benchmarks while using at most 30% of the original training time; deep RL with networks of abstract oscillators modeled on spinal central pattern generators learns 11 quadrupedal gaits and styles plus a meta-policy that selects gaits to optimize terrain- and velocity-specific metrics, with each gait optimal under at least one terrain–metric–velocity combination and zero-shot adaptation demonstrated to unseen gait couplings, OOD terrains, heavier quadrupeds, and even bipedal-mode locomotion under a pitch-tracking reward; a deep model-based RL framework learns to control ferrofluid shapes directly from physical interaction by mapping visual observations to electromagnet currents, repeatedly realizing transient target geometries such as polygons, asterisks, and pentominoes with a single fixed electromagnet; and a sim-to-real pipeline that combines motion imitation with RL on a muscle-driven neuromusculoskeletal model trains an ankle-exoskeleton controller using motion capture from a single virtual human, which when deployed on five unseen subjects increases tolerated backward-translation perturbations by 11.8% and reduces peak center-of-mass displacement and velocity by 13.5% and 28.2%, respectively, while highlighting inter-user variability and the potential benefits of more diverse virtual training populations. ([arXiv:2611.25197](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25197); [arXiv:2611.25958](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25958); [arXiv:2611.25232](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25232); [arXiv:2611.25480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25480))
