## Executive Summary

- Navigation and motion-planning papers span UAVs, quadrotors, surface vessels, and ground robots, with strong emphasis on MPC, safety under uncertainty, and GPS-/IMU-denied operation.
- Multiple works advance dexterous and robust manipulation via new gripper hardware, visuotactile and physical-property-aware policies, and future-aware VLA control for dynamic targets.
- Bimanual and long-horizon task structure planning methods target more complex manipulation, including rigid-object bimanual constraints and sparse task precedence recovery.
- Humanoid whole-body control progresses through RL-based stand-up and sit-to-stand policies that handle deformable terrain and variable chair heights.
- Vision-language(-action) architectures are pushed in medical endoscopy, autonomous driving, conveyor-belt manipulation, and active perception with physics-informed exploration.
- A unified branch-and-bound GCS Steiner-TSP formulation connects high-level combinatorial planning with continuous trajectory generation for mobile-manipulator inspection.
